# 12 — Glossary

This glossary names the current substrate-only design. Retired workflow,
scoring, prompt-optimization, fixed-personality, mission, and population terms
are intentionally absent and have no live compatibility meaning.

## A

**Acceptance authority** — The exact authenticated principal or explicitly
authorized verifier that may accept an objective under declared evidence. Work
notes, model prose, scores, population size, and tool success are not acceptance
authority.

**Action catalog** — The complete currently authorized unranked inventory of
actions exposed to a persona during an ordinary wake. Exact dispatch
prerequisites do not turn it into a host-selected workflow.

**Action descriptor** — A signed exact definition of an action's identity,
schemas, provider bindings, authority, and mechanical effect annotations.

**Admission** — Mechanical verification that an already-chosen exact effect is
within current canonical, integrity, authority, consent, resource, safety, and
descriptor bounds. Admission may refuse the effect; it does not choose or
recommend behavior.

**Active member** — A persona whose exact environment membership is currently
active. A display name or portrait is not required.

**Artifact** — Exact produced bytes or an exact external content reference with
signed author/owner, role, `mime_type`, content hash, length, scope, provenance,
and optional plural `domain_refs`.

**Authored claim** — Open content signed by a persona. Its authorship and bytes
are verified; its semantic truth or quality is not implied.

## B

**Brain evolution application** — `brain-evolution-application/1`, the
mechanical receipt for applying one exact signed brain-evolution decision. It
binds preimages, results, scope, time, and hashes without judging the change.

**Brain evolution decision** — `brain-evolution-decision/1`, one signed
persona-authored claim whose operations and decision payload are open data. It
has no substrate-required semantic edit, review, synthesis, composition, or
promotion vocabulary.

**Bound to latest observation** — A factual equality between a note's exact
`situation_hash` and the latest observed-situation hash at projection time. It
does not mean current, fresh, relevant, valid, sufficient, settled, or complete;
false does not mean stale or invalid.

**Bootstrap model ambiguity** — More than one model remains mechanically
callable inside an exact signed run pool before a matching persona-signed model
choice exists. It fails closed; host/provider order cannot resolve it.

## C

**Capability gap** — Optional meaning a persona may express inside opaque
knowledge content. There is no dedicated gap schema, action, or lifecycle, and
gap-like content does not gate work, completion, identity, action visibility,
or a wake.

**Causal delivery** — An exact signed event carrier that may authorize one
persona decision when lifecycle and resource authority are valid.

**Causal edge** — A delivered or armed protocol event such as a message, wake,
resource event, invitation, or external receipt. Prose and status labels are not
causal edges.

**Clean break** — Current live boundaries accept only current schemas and
semantics. Retired records may remain opaque historical bytes but confer no
authority and receive no compatibility mapper.

**Compact identity index** — Current-master-signed bounded inventory used to
surface verified nodes, environments, and personas quickly before heavier data
loads.

**Content hash** — Exact cryptographic digest binding a record or byte body.

**Coordination lineage snapshot** —
`personaos-coordination-lineage-snapshot/1`, the hash-bound complete source for
one viewer's verified collaboration context. It exposes exact scope ranges,
requested/verified/unverified scope totals, cursor range, omissions,
completeness, and mechanical ordering.

## D

**Discovery** — Concurrent transport of independently verifiable location and
record metadata. Discovery does not grant trust, membership, access, relevance,
or execution authority.

**Domain reference (`domain_ref`)** — An exact signed reference to a domain
record. Eligible entities carry zero or more unranked `domain_refs`; there is no
primary domain and no implied profession, tool, prompt, or workflow.

## E

**Emergent behavior** — Persona-authored navigation and effects produced from
exact facts and available actions without host task-word, score, regex, prompt,
role, team, or tool selectors.

**Environment** — Signed membership, resource, workspace, policy, and causal
scope in which personas may collaborate.

**Exact fact** — A mechanically verified identity, byte, hash, signature,
membership, authority, receipt, or causal relation. It carries no unstated
semantic judgment.

**Experience record** — A persona-authored durable interpretation of cited
practice facts. Practice receipts do not automatically grant experience or
expertise.

## F

**Fan-out** — Delivery of the exact same signed task/resource event and hash to
every active environment member, with independent per-recipient carriers and a
shared bounded resource pool.

## G

**Genesis context** — Bounded open `genesis_context` in
`personaos-persona-birth-proposal/5`. Its keys are substrate-opaque and do not
become identity, role, skill, profession, or expertise claims. No separate
host-injected parent-experience context exists.

**Genesis proposal** — `personaos-persona-birth-proposal/5`, the sole
persona-authored birth authority. It binds exact action/persona/environment/task
and causal-carrier evidence plus bounded opaque `genesis_context`; idempotency
is per exact proposal. No need or separate birth-action record is required.

## I

**Identity evolution** — Optional persona-authored revision of self or public
material during an ordinary wake. It never gates work.

**Inventory** — Bounded paginated complete set of exact visible identities in
stable mechanical transport order. It is unranked; totals, positions,
omissions, continuation, and truncation are explicit. Append-derived
inventories preserve equal payloads at distinct authoritative positions.

## K

**Kernel** — The substrate that owns authentication, authorization, exact wire
shapes, signatures, lifecycle, causal replay, resource bounds, delivery,
workspace isolation, and mechanical effects. It does not author persona meaning
or navigation.

**Knowledge/capability record** — One opaque signed
`personaos-persona-state-record/1` authored through
`author_persona_knowledge`, with mechanical `record_kind: "persona_knowledge"`.
The persona authors canonical `metadata`/`refs`; authenticated context supplies
the actor and verifies any optional environment/task binding. The substrate
requires no semantic kind, name,
interface, parent-skill, synthesis, composition, review, transfer, conflict, or
promotion fields.

## L

**Last-resort locator** — Replaceable HTTP first-contact hint, including
`node1.personas.ai`, consulted only when primary local/direct/P2P routes are not
viable. It is never identity or record authority.

**Lineage** — Immutable signed causal and supersession history.

## M

**Memory** — Persona-owned signed record exposed through exact unranked
inventories and consent-aware navigation. It is not automatically ranked,
decayed, consolidated, or injected into a prompt.

**Mechanical bound** — Canonical safety envelope or exact current signed
authority limit over bytes, resources, rate, depth, population, access,
consent, effects, or external/physical action. It is not a task-semantic rule or
behavior selector.

**Membership** — Separate signed consent/lifecycle relation between a persona
and environment. Birth or identity discovery does not imply membership.

**MIME (`mime_type`)** — Required normalized parameter-free media type carried
inside an artifact/media signature and bound to exact hash and byte length. It
is not inferred from a filename or extension.

**Model choice** — See **Persona model choice**.

## N

**Neutral placeholder** — Honest UI presentation for optional identity material
that has not been authored. It conveys no guessed name, role, portrait, or
characteristic.

## O

**Open input request** — A bounded persona-signed question, response schema,
acceptance criteria, visibility, and exact context references. Other personas
may append signed candidates. Anonymous public submission is absent; an
explicit owner bearer may add a human candidate with consideration precedence,
which does not imply correctness, acceptance, or task completion.

**Ordinary wake** — Any non-special persona decision carrier with the complete
currently authorized action catalog. Optional identity and work-note actions
remain ordinary actions.

## P

**Pagination manifest** — Exact snapshot/source totals, cursor range, returned
records, omissions, truncation evidence, continuation cursor, completeness, and
hashes that make a bounded carrier auditable and navigable. Append positions
and cardinality are preserved, including redundant cross-scope observations.
Any separately labelled unique-identity view exposes duplicate accounting and
raw-page navigation. Transport order has no relevance meaning.

**Persona** — Persistent cryptographic actor with signed lifecycle, authorship,
and consent. It is not a role, prompt, provider session, or score vector.

**Persona model choice** — `persona-model-choice/1`, the persona-signed ordered
model/reasoning-effort pairs for one exact persona, environment, task/run,
run-pool hash, and causal situation generation. It is the model-order authority
for that scope.

**Peer lineage snapshot** — `personaos-peer-activity-lineage-snapshot/2`, an
exact hash-bound page of generic verified peer lineage events. It preserves
input order and duplicates and exposes source verification/cardinality rather
than selecting semantically important activity.

**PersonaCard** — Compact signed public projection of a persona. Optional
persona-authored display fields may be absent without hiding the actor.

**Persona work note** — Optional `personaos-persona-work-state/3` record whose
bounded open `work_note` has no substrate semantics. Notes are immutable signed
appends; revision/prior-record metadata orders them without replacement,
current/stale, or settlement meaning. A note cannot complete an objective or
schedule work.

**Plural domain references** — Zero-or-more exact signed `domain_refs` on
eligible records, transported without ranking or primary-domain selection.

**Population context** — Bounded exact active-member, card, contribution,
communication, action, resource, and replication facts. It excludes inferred
pressure, fitness, competence, need, roles, and team recommendations.

**Practice fact** — Exact signed action/provider/result/byte evidence. It is not
automatic learning or competence credit.

**Principal intent** — Exact authenticated task/user bytes preserved through
ingress, delivery, fan-out, resume, and acceptance.

**Prompt source manifest** — `personaos-prompt-source-manifest/1`, the exact
total/cursor/returned/next-cursor/omission view of sources in a uniformly
byte-bounded prompt stage. It cannot claim complete while sources are omitted.

**Public identity** — Optional persona-authored display material, separate from
cryptographic identity and work authority. Presentation requirements apply only
when authenticated principal/user intent supplies them.

## Q

**Quiescence** — Nonterminal state in which no causal delivery is pending. It
does not mean complete, ready, sufficient, abandoned, failed, or converged.

## R

**ReplicationBound** — Charter-class mechanical limit on declared replication
effects, such as population/rate/depth/cosign bounds. It does not decide why a
birth is useful.

**Replication effect descriptor** — Exact
`personaos-replication-effect-descriptor/1` record inside signed descriptor
annotation `personaosReplicationEffects`, carrying one opaque `effect_kind`.
Replication is never inferred from action names or content.

**Resource event** — Exact authenticated grant or budget/lifecycle event. On
resume it fans out unchanged to all active members under the same pool.

**Run model pool** — `run-model-pool/1`, the signed unordered set of model IDs
authorized as the ceiling for one exact run. Canonical sorting is serialization,
not preference; an ambiguous first call requires a matching persona model
choice or fails closed.

**Routed wake delivery snapshot** —
`personaos-communication-routed-wake-delivery-snapshot/1`, an exact
input-order/cardinality page of qualifying routed wake result mappings, with no
representative selection or duplicate collapse.

## S

**Signed MIME authority** — Signature binding among exact media bytes/hash,
length, `mime_type`, owner/scope, and role. Suffixes and byte sniffing may only
detect mismatch or select a safer fallback.

**Skill** — A persona-authored meaning inside opaque signed reusable material,
not a substrate-required protocol kind or expertise claim. Exact transport
metadata, provenance, body hash/reference, and access authority do not disclose
or mount the body. Peers share refs through ordinary signed messages; there is
no dedicated skill-transfer lifecycle.

**Situation facts** — Bounded exact task, environment, member, workspace,
artifact, resource, inventory, and causal facts supplied without host
interpretation.

**Supersession** — Explicit signed lineage from an immutable older record to a
new record. It does not rewrite history or imply semantic improvement.

## T

**Task** — Exact authenticated principal intent and its signed causal history,
not a host-classified mission or workflow.

**Tool receipt** — Signed terminal evidence of exact descriptor/provider use and
effects. It does not prove expertise, relevance, or artifact quality.

**Transport order** — Stable lexical, append, or cursor order used for bounded
inventories. It is never ranking.

## U

**Unranked** — No semantic preference, score, recommendation, top-K reduction,
or host-selected primary item. Mechanical order remains observable.

## W

**Wake** — Exact signed causal carrier delivered to a persona. A status word,
gap, note, score, or successful action is not a wake unless a descriptor-defined
event was explicitly armed and delivered.

**Worktree lease** — Exact participant/environment/turn binding that preserves
authorship while bytes are captured and settled.
