# Visual guide

[Home](README.md) · [Start here](START-HERE.md) · [Design guide](design/README.md)

These editable diagrams explain the current directly editable numerical-persona design. They are teaching illustrations, not screenshots, fixed task workflows or observed learning results. Every linked chapter gives a text explanation of the same meaning. Open a figure to view it at full size.

## How to read the figures

A box represents a state, responsibility or choice. An arrow names a possible relationship or transition; it does not promise that the destination occurs or improves the result. Purple distinguishes personal character, blue work and reasoning, green experience, amber evidence, and gray supporting context. Labels carry the meaning without relying on color.

A six-box loop is a compact explanation, not a requirement for six model calls. Some decisions can combine independent responsibilities. Results that have not happened cannot be used as earlier evidence.

## Artwork and revision history

The figures are repository-native SVG artwork with editable text, accessible titles and descriptions, and explicit text explanations in the chapters. They replace the older fragment-based and quota-era artwork on the active reading path. The prior images and their original claims remain at the [exact predecessor asset tree](https://github.com/ai-personas/ai-personas-design/tree/60fc268eea909ea2cfc4e91cf180c0020bde6ef1/assets).

Presentation inspection does not establish model behavior, psychological realism or a working implementation. See [status](implementation/STATUS.md) for that distinction.
