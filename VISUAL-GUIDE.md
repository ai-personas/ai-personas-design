## A complete text reading

### The whole loop constitutes the persona

The figure depicts the persona as the whole continuing loop: characteristic-bearing prompt fragments, the context assembled from selected fragments and current character together with needed supporting resources, LLM reasoning in that context, work and interaction, and possible self-revision in response to actual experience. The persona is constituted by that relationship across the loop. Neither a file collection alone nor an isolated LLM call is the complete account.

Fragments are persona-defining prompt parts. They express characteristic ways of noticing, interpreting, reasoning, communicating, cooperating, and choosing, so their role is broader than storing things for later recall. Experience can inform their content, but an experience-derived fragment still contributes a characteristic perspective to reasoning when selected and supplied. Factual records and attributed evidence support that process without becoming persona-authored character merely by being retained.

The primary chooses which exact eligible fragments and supporting resources to supply for the next decision; the host faithfully assembles them with the required context. Retaining a resource, discovering it, actually supplying it to a model, and being authorized to execute an operation remain distinct. In that context, the LLM reasons and interacts as the persona, and actual consequences can motivate proposed revisions that change later context. Self-revision here includes ordinary fragment refinement and the separately controlled character-revision branch. It remains conditional: explicit justified no change is a valid maintenance outcome. This is an operational account of persona continuity, with no claim of consciousness, human equivalence, automatic training, or guaranteed improvement.

### From a trait seed to initial own-voice fragments

The top strip shows the normal creation path. An attributed trait seed and bounded authoring instructions reach the initial LLM authoring call. It authors characteristic prompt fragments in the persona's own voice, developing how the supplied traits express a coherent perspective. This is an initialization role: it may use the continuing primary's inference configuration or a separately declared, admitted configuration. The first ordinary primary decision receives the accepted starting fragments and their actual attribution. This does not introduce another continuing author, grant ongoing rewriting authority, or require an extra handoff, conversion, or reflection call. The host accepts the coherent initial profile and own-voice account together under the creation rules. Seed provenance, the generated candidate, and the accepted initial revision remain separate facts; fluent output alone does not prove acceptance. Initial generation has a finite allowance, the maintenance responsibility appropriate to that mode, and an explicit outcome. Retries retain the same creation identity and actual accepted outcome.

Imported or operator-supplied starting accounts remain an attributed alternative; they must not be relabeled as model-authored. No starting route invents lived experience. Later self-harmonization means primary-authored, evidence-sensitive refinement of compatible fragments, cues, and qualifications, with truthful no change allowed. It is not automatic corpus-wide restyling, a second writer, or inevitable trait drift. Any effective-character revision follows the separately controlled branch below.

### One character account shapes voice and choices

The character band below creation shows one coherent, accepted, versioned character account. Its OCEAN profile describes relatively stable tendencies: Openness, Conscientiousness, Extraversion, Agreeableness, and Neuroticism or Negative Emotionality. VAD describes modeled valence, arousal, and dominance or perceived control. Baseline expression and event-sensitive current state stay distinct; a transient state does not silently revise a stable trait. Neither profile nor modeled affect asserts felt emotion, competence, or permission to control others.

The profile and its own-voice prose express the same accepted character. Qualitative descriptions suffice; scores, a universal trait-to-affect conversion, and a separate personality controller are not required. Character informs every discretionary interpretation and choice: what the persona notices, asks, tries, corrects, communicates to humans and peers, chooses to do together, searches for, and selects as context. The same primary persona makes those choices; a generic decision engine followed by a decorative voice rewrite would not express this design. Character is not the sole cause of behavior. It does not route professions or roles, prescribe tool frequency, override evidence, or guarantee a useful result. Decisive constraints may appropriately lead different personas to the same choice.

### Characteristic prompt parts form a compact network

The upper-left store shows persona-defining prompt fragments held in ordinary persona-owned files. Every persona-authored fragment carries its characteristic perspective, including methods, experience interpretations, relationship understandings, and coordination habits. Their connections help the primary find and compose relevant characteristic context, rather than making recall the defining purpose of the collection. The quoted fragment is explicitly hypothetical, not a recorded event or a tested lesson. Own-voice does not require a trait label or an “I” in every sentence. Raw observations, exact quotations, and source material retain their original form and attribution.

The persona chooses useful names and organization; the pictured subjects are not a compulsory folder taxonomy. It can list, search, use supported grep-like or regular-expression matching, read exact permitted versions, and propose attributable changes. Its prose can contain useful search cues that suggest where to look, including when terminology or organization has changed. The authored-cue label denotes possible associations, not precomputed mandatory edges. A network can emerge through ordinary files, authored cues, and selective searches without a graph engine, compulsory index, or additional semantic selector.

The primary decides whether a cue helps the current need, how to phrase the bounded query, which candidates to inspect, which exact accepted fragments to select, and when to stop. The host executes the requested discovery under current scope, source, matching, and resource constraints; it does not automatically follow cues or recursively expand a neighborhood. Cue text is data, not executable shell instructions. Matches are candidates, not endorsement, truth, persistent selection, or proof of full-text supply. A no-match result establishes only the stated search result, not absence of relevant experience. Different wording, stale cues, incomplete scope, permissions, or exhausted limits can leave useful material undiscovered. Cumulative searches, fanout, repeated reads, returned text, prompt occupancy, and relevant time and cost share a finite work allowance; cycles and reformulated queries do not renew it.

The branched return arrow from the host to both stores represents only exact accepted changes to fragments or supporting assets. New experience can sharpen an existing fragment, connect useful ideas, consolidate duplicates, prune a misleading cue, or retire obsolete guidance instead of creating another note. The figure's refinement line is a menu of possibilities, not a compulsory sequence. A proposal is not a successful save, and a failed save cannot support a claim that a lesson was retained. Accepted warnings, significant exceptions, source restrictions, and unresolved consequential obligations cannot be discarded merely to make the collection smaller.

Compactness applies to both the supplied prompt and the whole retained footprint. Fragments, indexes, aliases, revisions, supporting evidence, warnings, archives, and caches are not an unlimited outside store. Lawful retention and explicit budgets determine what can remain; moving old text to an ever-growing archive is not a solution. If required material consumes the available capacity, the honest outcome may be a narrowed claim, ineligible guidance, blocked admission, or an authorized capacity or retention decision. A small prompt alone proves neither bounded storage nor useful maturation.

### Stored supporting resources are not automatically prompt context

Plain domain knowledge, generic technical procedures, tools, skills, code, data and records are supporting assets whether persona-authored or imported. Their purpose differs from characteristic and behavioral fragments. Own-voice styling cannot change that purpose; a fragment can refer to an exact asset, whose operative instructions may guide authorized work without becoming character.

The lower-left store holds separately identifiable supporting resources: histories, tool-use records, tool definitions, reusable or imported skills, scripts, code, and data. These resources can support the persona without becoming its characteristic prompt fragments. They are also part of the primary's every-call maintenance responsibility: relevant history, tools, skills, scripts, code, or data may need to be stored, refined, organized, or retired within the accepted authority and retention rules. A characteristic account of a method expresses its perspective; a technical skill or procedure, whether persona-authored or imported, retains its operative meaning and actual authorship, as do exact schemas, code artifacts, datasets, and tool receipts. No fixed directory tree is implied.

The arrows labeled “selected via host” mean only the primary-requested, eligible material actually supplied through validated assembly. They do not put every stored file, resource descriptor, skill summary, or tool schema into the choosing call. The inventory stays outside the model context and is not transmitted wholesale in the provider request for subsequent filtering. Calling the catalogue “metadata,” deferring its interpretation, or asking another LLM to choose from it does not create a bounded discovery path.

The small bootstrap makes selection possible without such a preload. Besides the current accepted character and required work state, it provides usable generic discovery and exact-read/exposure controls: permitted scope names, matching and pagination semantics, finite limits, and meaningful failure outcomes. During creation, the attributed seed and bounded authoring instructions take their appropriate place. A bounded relevant seed cue or known exact reference may also be available with honest provenance. The bootstrap is navigation infrastructure, not a hidden relevance judgment or a synopsis of every resource.

From the current need, the same primary can request a bounded lexical or supported regex query, inspect a small returned preview, or go directly to a known exact reference. A useful hit identifies an exact source-qualified resource and revision or version-bound locator, enough permitted purpose or matched text to decide the next step, relevant source limits, and an exact read or exposure route. A preview is not a full read. The primary then chooses the specific supporting context, fragment, result, or operative definition needed by a later decision. It can narrow, replace, clear, retain a still-valid selection, or stop within the existing work-scoped contract; no separate resource selector is introduced.

The right-hand operation box distinguishes execution from loading. Reading tool documentation does not register a native callable schema. Actual schema exposure does not invoke the tool or grant authority. A script's source, dependencies, and complete input dataset need not all occupy the prompt merely because an authorized operation uses them. Such execution still requires the current complete operative interface and applicable guidance, trusted artifact/environment binding where claimed, permissions, and resources. A declared data slice or code excerpt is an exact partial observation, not a full-file read; required skill instructions, schemas, warnings, and qualifications cannot be silently replaced by incomplete snippets. If the selected provider or interface cannot expose the needed capability within its contract and budget, the limitation is explicit rather than a claim that an unloaded tool was callable.

### Each invocation does work and addresses maintenance

The center is the primary language model reasoning in the assembled characteristic context and acting within the continuing persona loop. Every persona invocation receives the unified maintenance responsibility and its current limits for both persona-defining fragments and supporting assets. Every complete valid response combines useful work, an explicit maintenance outcome covering both, and any needed next-context requests. Maintenance can propose fragment creation, revision, organization, consolidation, or deletion/retirement, and supporting-asset storage, refinement, organization, or deletion/retirement under current ownership, authority, source, and retention controls. The explicit outcome can be proposed changes, justified no change, or a blocked or bounded deferred outcome; it need not duplicate a form for each asset kind. Useful work can include a search decision, selection, a question, a peer response, coordination, or a repair within that mode's limits. Calling a persona decision a helper does not exempt it.

This is mandatory consideration of both fragments and assets with an explicit outcome, not mandatory mutation. An ordinary call can briefly justify leaving both unchanged; it does not need an asset write, a fragment edit, a separate maintenance call, or an invented observation to justify storage. A concise, justified unchanged outcome is valid; silent omission is not. A known unresolved correction cannot be disguised as no change. A block or deferral names the unresolved need, reason, consequences for dependent work, accepted responsibility or ownership gap, and any existing bounded opportunity to revisit it. If that opportunity expires, an honest unresolved disposition does not claim that the issue was fixed. No outcome creates a reflection wake, new allowance, or extra reflection call by itself. A failed call that produces no usable persona response remains an attempt failure; the host does not invent a no-change judgment for it.

The same primary LLM chooses useful storage paths, organization, and search cues when proposing retained changes, and the relevant exact optional fragments and supporting resources for its next work-scoped context, including the bounded discovery or exact-read request that obtains them. A proposed path, cue, body, or selection does not prove that anything was saved. The actual accepted save, exact retained version, any failed or uncertain disposition, and later actual supply keep separate receipts. Moving or refining a technical asset cannot erase its real authorship, silently grant execution authority, or rewrite source evidence. Explicitly preserving a still-appropriate valid selection, replacing it, clearing it or reporting no optional need have distinct meanings. Every accepted response states next-context intent for fragments and assets; omission invalidates the combined response rather than supplying preservation. Recovery may retain stored state without proving consideration. No choice renews permission/lifetime or forces another call. This judgment remains separate from maintenance no-change. No additional semantic selector or background writer owns either judgment.

### Actual experience can change later choices

The right panel contains actual work and received observations: the user's need and constraints, human conversations, peer cooperation and disagreement, environmental and tool results, and evidence of the persona's own earlier choices. Proposed actions require current admission. Only actual received evidence establishes an observation; a plan, pending action, or intended effect cannot be rewritten as success. A peer's claim establishes what was said, not automatically the claim's truth. Praise may support a communication preference without verifying the underlying advice. A peer can decline, a capability can fail, and a persona can wait or stop honestly. Applicable obligations and unknown effects remain visible. Dependent work requires a fresh decision that has received the relevant actual result.

The ordinary feedback loop runs through the primary's interpretation, its explicit maintenance outcome, and the host's exact accepted update. Revised fragments can then be found, selected, and actually supplied in later work. Their use can change methods, communication, relationships, collaboration, and navigation habits. Later outcomes may reinforce the interpretation, narrow an exception, reveal a bad lesson, or justify no durable change. The persona can refine how it finds and organizes useful context as well as how to perform a task. Each claimed link needs its own evidence: observation, accepted interpretation, exact later supply, behavior, and assessed consequence are different claims.

### Character feedback is optional and separately controlled

The dashed outer return path is deliberately separate from ordinary fragment refinement. The primary may propose a broader revision of effective current character when the user's self-authorship control permits it. An experiential claim must have relevant actual supporting experience; an authorized operator override keeps its own attribution rather than being narrated as personal growth. The host accepts only the exact coherent change under the existing character, evidence, source, and resource rules. It does not infer a new character from accumulated notes or write a new self-description in the background.

The adoption request is resolved before any other fresh operation from the same response. An accepted effective-character change fences all of those operations, including reads and waits, until a fresh primary decision receives the accepted character and current policy. Known rejection or an explicit deferral with a definite no-commit disposition preserves the prior account and permits only work that meets the existing independence, freshness, and current-guard checks. An unresolved or unknown commit disposition holds that work. A retained candidate never commits automatically later. These details follow the [current-character rules](design/PERSONA-CORE.md#character-in-its-own-voice) and [complete-response contract](implementation/CONTRACTS.md#complete-response-persistence-and-dependent-work); the short label in the figure does not replace them.

Ordinary methods, relationship interpretations, cue improvements, and scoped transient affect can change without revising stable traits. Disabled self-authorship does not forbid contextual refinement, but an always-supplied fragment or supposed affect update cannot disguise a replacement character. The separate branch is optional: elapsed time, invocation count, praise, or archive size does not create wisdom or require trait change. Development may include useful disagreement, narrower confidence, retirement of a lesson, or no change. The analogy to a continuing individual receiving experience implies no human biology, felt memory, automatic model training, or guaranteed improvement.

### The host validates, commits, and assembles faithfully

The lower panel makes the handoff explicit. The host validates the complete response before substantive dispatch. It records the accepted unified maintenance disposition and commits only the exact accepted fragment or supporting-asset changes, with the required authorship, permissions, base-version checks, and coherent dependencies. It cannot generate a missing outcome, silently repair persona prose, or turn a rejected edit into an accepted lesson. Missing or malformed maintenance or next-context intent prevents new substantive dispatch from that response, including reads and selection changes. Independent cancellation, receipts, and accounting remain possible when a response is invalid. Response validation differs from later operation acceptance: a valid proposal can still fail to save, and only demonstrably independent work remains eligible under the current guards. The [separate character-feedback branch](#character-feedback-is-optional-and-separately-controlled) governs effective-character changes and their fresh-decision fence.

For the next decision, the host faithfully assembles current character and relevant modeled affect, mandatory work and authority, maintenance responsibility and limits, available work allowances, consequential observations, unresolved effects, and the primary's selected exact fragments and supporting content with required qualifications. Needed callable definitions and operative guidance are exposed through the actual supported interface and recorded separately from text-only reading. The arrows show continuing relationships, not a compulsory sequence of extra model calls. The host supplies relevant selected optional prompt fragments and supporting resources rather than loading their entire corpora or catalogues. Mandatory context does not compete with optional fragments. A hit, requested selection, successful fetch into host storage, and actual provider-attempt exposure each have their own evidence; the intended selection alone proves none of the later events. Faithful assembly gives the primary the accepted characteristic context from which to reason; it does not make the host a persona author or selector.

Search discovery, a full read, an authored selection, actual inclusion or callable exposure, authorized invocation, received result, subsequent application, and measured benefit remain distinct. A search match does not select itself. A read is an observation and does not automatically become endorsed guidance or permanent attention. Required corrections accompany usable full content; optional associations do not activate a neighborhood. Stale, unavailable, private, or oversized required context leads to the applicable bounded recovery or an explicit block, rather than silently incomplete guidance. Restricted repair still includes maintenance consideration within its existing limits; it is not permission to change prohibited text or perform substantive task work.

Three kinds of connection therefore remain distinct:

- An optional authored cue suggests a bounded search or useful direction to consider. It may find different candidates as permitted files evolve.
- An exact reference identifies the particular accepted text or observed evidence. A search hit or a newer file at the same path cannot silently replace it.
- An accepted required qualification binds what must accompany guidance before that guidance is supplied as usable. It is not optional advice to search for a caveat later.

The complete eligible qualification bundle must fit; optional navigation bounds do not justify dropping a required exception. Renaming, consolidation, and retirement preserve honest exact identities and accepted successor or unavailable dispositions. A hash cannot reconstruct erased evidence. A retained lesson may survive source expiry only where derivative-use and retention policy permit, with honest limits on what remains verifiable; required derivative deletion and disclosure restrictions still apply. Unresolved receipts or commitments cannot be compressed into a generic lesson and silently settled.

The bottom boundary applies throughout. Current access, processing destination, source restrictions, exact versions, retention, budgets, and cancellation are checked independently of what a persona's files say. Real receipts, commitments, charges, and unresolved effects remain trustworthy records. A first-person fragment cannot confer permission, cancel another participant's obligation, change an actual observation, or prove its own usefulness. Navigation, maintenance, failed attempts, repair, and finishing consume the same controlling allowance. Neither an association nor a known unfinished correction creates an idle wake, new funding, or a perpetual compaction process.

### A hypothetical selective-resource trace

This example illustrates dependency boundaries, not a compulsory sequence of calls or a tested product result:

1. A primary call receives its current accepted character, task, protected obligations, maintenance responsibility and limits, and the compact discovery interface. It already knows a relevant own-voice “early reopen check” cue. No tool or skill catalogue is supplied. It requests a bounded lookup for a suitable editable-document validation resource and explicitly records a justified no-change maintenance outcome for fragments and supporting assets.
2. A later call receives the bounded candidate descriptions with exact identities, versions, source limits, and the search's completeness. The primary chooses its exact characteristic fragment and one relevant validation skill, requests the needed tool definition and a bounded artifact observation, and again gives an explicit unified maintenance outcome. If a new resource should be retained or an existing one better organized, the primary can propose its permitted storage path and useful search cues; retention remains unproven until the save is accepted. Other candidates stay unloaded. A previously known exact reference could have avoided discovery.
3. The host records what the next call actually receives: the selected fragment, complete eligible operative skill and tool contract, exact declared artifact observation, mandatory qualifications, and protected state. The primary uses this actual context to propose the authorized check. A descriptor is not mistaken for a callable schema. When an operative schema is already supplied and current, no redundant discovery or loading call is required.
4. Admission checks the real capability, current version and environment, authority, arguments, and resources. The script and its dependencies may execute without all their source entering the prompt. The actual result, including failure or an uncertain effect, reaches a fresh decision before dependent work. That decision may propose a grounded fragment refinement, a permitted supporting-asset save or organizational change, or explicitly justify no change across both. A valid proposal, an accepted edit, later exact supply, and demonstrated usefulness remain different claims.

Every persona invocation in this progression carries unified fragment-and-asset maintenance input and an explicit accepted-response disposition, alongside its useful work and any needed next-context requests. Independent bounded requests may be grouped when the combined request fits; later decisions cannot anticipate observations they have not received. No search, read, result, or code preview executes itself. Unresolved jobs keep usable inspection, correction, cancellation, and closeout controls when optional support leaves context.

### Budget the whole request and the whole episode

The bottom band makes the efficiency aim explicit: need-directed context should support useful work within finite prompt, storage, time, and monetary allowances. It is not a claim that the selected prompt is mathematically minimal. Both the first choosing call and later calls are subject to the same aggregate boundary. Bootstrap instructions, bounded candidate previews, selected fragments, skill guidance, actual callable schemas and provider expansions, data slices, received results, continuing history, maintenance, and mandatory control information all count. Reserve output capacity and relevant provider overhead as part of admission. A small number of huge results is not a bounded prompt.

Measure actual request exposure as well as intended selection. Cached or provider-retained context is not assumed absent or free. A dynamic tool reference can expand into more material than its visible label; the adapter must bound the real expansion or report the guarantee as limited or unsupported. Unrelated loaded guidance should not accumulate indefinitely, while unresolved work must retain the controls and qualifications it still needs.

Evaluate total episode tokens, cost, and latency, including preparation, discovery, unsuccessful searches, exact reads, retries, maintenance, execution, and reload or exposure overhead. A smaller final action prompt can conceal costly navigation. Compare useful delivery and harmful omissions at matched total resources; token savings do not establish benefit if necessary work fails. Likewise, compact context does not excuse an unbounded supporting archive. Required context that cannot fit leads to permitted bounded recovery or a truthful block, not silent loss of a warning, pending effect, authority limit, or qualification.
