## A complete text reading

### The whole loop constitutes the persona

The figure depicts the persona as the whole continuing loop: characteristic-bearing prompt fragments, the context assembled from selected fragments and current character, LLM reasoning in that context, work and interaction, and possible self-revision in response to actual experience. The persona is constituted by that relationship across the loop. Neither a file collection alone nor an isolated LLM call is the complete account.

Fragments are persona-defining prompt parts. They express characteristic ways of noticing, interpreting, reasoning, communicating, cooperating, and choosing, so their role is broader than storing things for later recall. Experience can inform their content, but an experience-derived fragment still contributes a characteristic perspective to reasoning when selected and supplied. Factual records and attributed evidence support that process without becoming persona-authored character merely by being retained.

The primary chooses which exact eligible fragments to supply for the next decision; the host faithfully assembles them with the required context. In that context, the LLM reasons and interacts as the persona, and actual consequences can motivate proposed revisions that change later context. Self-revision here includes ordinary fragment refinement and the separately controlled character-revision branch. It remains conditional: explicit justified no change is a valid maintenance outcome. This is an operational account of persona continuity, with no claim of consciousness, human equivalence, automatic training, or guaranteed improvement.

### One character account shapes voice and choices

The upper band shows one coherent, accepted, versioned character account. Its OCEAN profile describes relatively stable tendencies: Openness, Conscientiousness, Extraversion, Agreeableness, and Neuroticism or Negative Emotionality. VAD describes modeled valence, arousal, and dominance or perceived control. Baseline expression and event-sensitive current state stay distinct; a transient state does not silently revise a stable trait. Neither profile nor modeled affect asserts felt emotion, competence, or permission to control others.

The profile and its own-voice prose express the same accepted character. Qualitative descriptions suffice; scores, a universal trait-to-affect conversion, and a separate personality controller are not required. Character informs every discretionary interpretation and choice: what the persona notices, asks, tries, corrects, communicates to humans and peers, chooses to do together, searches for, and selects as context. The same primary persona makes those choices; a generic decision engine followed by a decorative voice rewrite would not express this design. Character is not the sole cause of behavior. It does not route professions or roles, prescribe tool frequency, override evidence, or guarantee a useful result. Decisive constraints may appropriately lead different personas to the same choice.

### Characteristic prompt parts form a compact network

The left panel shows persona-defining prompt fragments held in ordinary persona-owned files. Every persona-authored fragment carries its characteristic perspective, including methods, experience interpretations, relationship understandings, and coordination habits. Their connections help the primary find and compose relevant characteristic context, rather than making recall the defining purpose of the collection. The quoted fragment is explicitly hypothetical, not a recorded event or a tested lesson. Own-voice does not require a trait label or an “I” in every sentence. Raw observations, exact quotations, and source material retain their original form and attribution.

The persona chooses useful names and organization; the pictured subjects are not a compulsory folder taxonomy. It can list, search, use supported grep-like or regular-expression matching, read exact permitted versions, and propose attributable changes. Its prose can contain useful search cues that suggest where to look, including when terminology or organization has changed. The small dashed connections in the file panel illustrate these possible associations, not precomputed mandatory edges. A network can emerge through ordinary files, authored cues, and selective searches without a graph engine, compulsory index, or additional semantic selector.

The primary decides whether a cue helps the current need, how to phrase the bounded query, which candidates to inspect, which exact accepted fragments to select, and when to stop. The host executes the requested discovery under current scope, source, matching, and resource constraints; it does not automatically follow cues or recursively expand a neighborhood. Cue text is data, not executable shell instructions. Matches are candidates, not endorsement, truth, persistent selection, or proof of full-text supply. A no-match result establishes only the stated search result, not absence of relevant experience. Different wording, stale cues, incomplete scope, permissions, or exhausted limits can leave useful material undiscovered. Cumulative searches, fanout, repeated reads, returned text, prompt occupancy, and relevant time and cost share a finite work allowance; cycles and reformulated queries do not renew it.

The return arrow from the host to the files represents only exact accepted changes. New experience can sharpen an existing fragment, connect useful ideas, consolidate duplicates, prune a misleading cue, or retire obsolete guidance instead of creating another note. The figure's refinement line is a menu of possibilities, not a compulsory sequence. A proposal is not a successful save, and a failed save cannot support a claim that a lesson was retained. Accepted warnings, significant exceptions, source restrictions, and unresolved consequential obligations cannot be discarded merely to make the collection smaller.

Compactness applies to both the supplied prompt and the whole retained footprint. Fragments, indexes, aliases, revisions, supporting evidence, warnings, archives, and caches are not an unlimited outside store. Lawful retention and explicit budgets determine what can remain; moving old text to an ever-growing archive is not a solution. If required material consumes the available capacity, the honest outcome may be a narrowed claim, ineligible guidance, blocked admission, or an authorized capacity or retention decision. A small prompt alone proves neither bounded storage nor useful learning.

### Each invocation does work and addresses maintenance

The center is the primary language model reasoning in the assembled characteristic context and acting within the continuing persona loop. Every persona invocation receives the maintenance responsibility and its current limits. Every complete valid response combines its useful work with an explicit maintenance outcome: proposed changes, justified unchanged files, or an explicit blocked or bounded deferred outcome. Useful work can include a search decision, selection, a question, a peer response, coordination, or a repair within that mode's limits. Calling a persona decision a helper does not exempt it.

This is mandatory consideration and an explicit outcome, not mandatory mutation. A concise, justified unchanged outcome is valid; silent omission is not. A known unresolved correction cannot be disguised as no change. A block or deferral names the unresolved need, reason, consequences for dependent work, accepted responsibility or ownership gap, and any existing bounded opportunity to revisit it. If that opportunity expires, an honest unresolved disposition does not claim that the issue was fixed. No outcome creates a reflection wake, new allowance, or extra reflection call by itself. A failed call that produces no usable persona response remains an attempt failure; the host does not invent a no-change judgment for it.

The same primary LLM chooses the relevant exact optional fragments for its next work-scoped context. Retaining a valid selection, replacing it, and clearing it have distinct meanings; omission of a selection update preserves an existing valid choice without renewing its permission or lifetime. This selection behavior is separate from the explicit maintenance outcome required in every valid response. No additional semantic selector or background writer owns either judgment.

### Actual experience can change later choices

The right panel contains actual work and received observations: the user's need and constraints, human conversations, peer cooperation and disagreement, environmental and tool results, and evidence of the persona's own earlier choices. Proposed actions require current admission. Only actual received evidence establishes an observation; a plan, pending action, or intended effect cannot be rewritten as success. A peer's claim establishes what was said, not automatically the claim's truth. Praise may support a communication preference without verifying the underlying advice. A peer can decline, a capability can fail, and a persona can wait or stop honestly. Applicable obligations and unknown effects remain visible. Dependent work requires a fresh decision that has received the relevant actual result.

The ordinary feedback loop runs through the primary's interpretation, its explicit maintenance outcome, and the host's exact accepted update. Revised fragments can then be found, selected, and actually supplied in later work. Their use can change methods, communication, relationships, collaboration, and navigation habits. Later outcomes may reinforce the interpretation, narrow an exception, reveal a bad lesson, or justify no durable change. The persona can learn how to find and organize useful context as well as how to perform a task. Each claimed link needs its own evidence: observation, accepted interpretation, exact later supply, behavior, and assessed consequence are different claims.

### Character feedback is optional and separately controlled

The dashed outer return path is deliberately separate from ordinary fragment learning. The primary may propose a broader revision of effective current character when the user's self-authorship control permits it. An experiential claim must have relevant actual supporting experience; an authorized operator override keeps its own attribution rather than being narrated as personal growth. The host accepts only the exact coherent change under the existing character, evidence, source, and resource rules. It does not infer a new character from accumulated notes or write a new self-description in the background.

The adoption request is resolved before any other fresh operation from the same response. An accepted effective-character change fences all of those operations, including reads and waits, until a fresh primary decision receives the accepted character and current policy. Known rejection or an explicit deferral with a definite no-commit disposition preserves the prior account and permits only work that meets the existing independence, freshness, and current-guard checks. An unresolved or unknown commit disposition holds that work. A retained candidate never commits automatically later. These details follow the [current-character rules](design/PERSONA-CORE.md#character-in-its-own-voice) and [complete-response contract](implementation/CONTRACTS.md#complete-response-persistence-and-dependent-work); the short label in the figure does not replace them.

Ordinary methods, relationship interpretations, cue improvements, and scoped transient affect can change without revising stable traits. Disabled self-authorship does not forbid learning, but an always-supplied fragment or supposed affect update cannot disguise a replacement character. The separate branch is optional: elapsed time, invocation count, praise, or archive size does not create wisdom or require trait change. Development may include useful disagreement, narrower confidence, retirement of a lesson, or no change. The analogy to a continuing individual receiving experience implies no human biology, felt memory, automatic model training, or guaranteed improvement.

### The host validates, commits, and assembles faithfully

The lower panel makes the handoff explicit. The host validates the complete response before substantive dispatch. It records the accepted maintenance disposition and commits only the exact accepted changes, with the required authorship, permissions, base-version checks, and coherent dependencies. It cannot generate a missing outcome, silently repair persona prose, or turn a rejected edit into an accepted lesson. Missing or malformed maintenance prevents new substantive dispatch from that response, including reads and selection changes. Independent cancellation, receipts, and accounting remain possible when a response is invalid. Response validation differs from later operation acceptance: a valid proposal can still fail to save, and only demonstrably independent work remains eligible under the current guards. A genuine accepted effective-character change still fences every fresh operation from that response, including reads and waits, until a fresh primary decision receives the new character and current policy.

For the next decision, the host faithfully assembles current character and relevant modeled affect, mandatory work and authority, maintenance responsibility and limits, resources, consequential observations, unresolved effects, and the primary's selected exact fragments with required qualifications. The arrows show continuing relationships, not a compulsory sequence of extra model calls. The host supplies relevant selected optional prompt fragments rather than loading the entire corpus. Mandatory context does not compete with optional fragments. Faithful assembly gives the primary the accepted characteristic context from which to reason; it does not make the host a persona author or selector.

Search discovery, a full read, an authored selection, actual inclusion, subsequent application, and measured benefit remain distinct. A search match does not select itself. A read is an observation and does not automatically become endorsed guidance or permanent attention. Required corrections accompany usable full content; optional associations do not activate a neighborhood. Stale, unavailable, private, or oversized required context leads to the applicable bounded recovery or an explicit block, rather than silently incomplete guidance. Restricted repair still includes maintenance consideration within its existing limits; it is not permission to change prohibited text or perform substantive task work.

Three kinds of connection therefore remain distinct:

- An optional authored cue suggests a bounded search or useful direction to consider. It may find different candidates as permitted files evolve.
- An exact reference identifies the particular accepted text or observed evidence. A search hit or a newer file at the same path cannot silently replace it.
- An accepted required qualification binds what must accompany guidance before that guidance is supplied as usable. It is not optional advice to search for a caveat later.

The complete eligible qualification bundle must fit; optional navigation bounds do not justify dropping a required exception. Renaming, consolidation, and retirement preserve honest exact identities and accepted successor or unavailable dispositions. A hash cannot reconstruct erased evidence. A retained lesson may survive source expiry only where derivative-use and retention policy permit, with honest limits on what remains verifiable; required derivative deletion and disclosure restrictions still apply. Unresolved receipts or commitments cannot be compressed into a generic lesson and silently settled.

The bottom boundary applies throughout. Current access, processing destination, source restrictions, exact versions, retention, budgets, and cancellation are checked independently of what a persona's files say. Real receipts, commitments, charges, and unresolved effects remain trustworthy records. A first-person fragment cannot confer permission, cancel another participant's obligation, change an actual observation, or prove its own usefulness. Navigation, maintenance, failed attempts, repair, and finishing consume the same controlling allowance. Neither an association nor a known unfinished correction creates an idle wake, new funding, or a perpetual compaction process.

## Scope and visual status

This is an illustration of the adopted target design. It is not a product screenshot, live persona trace, multi-call benchmark, or evidence of learning benefit. The detailed normative documents govern simplifications in the image. The loop does not require a second persona, a tool, a new lesson, a physical body, or between-task exploration for every request. Protocol coverage and recognizable prose do not establish psychological validity, behavioral influence, or better outcomes.

The current SVG is editable vector artwork with a title, accessible description, explicit labels, and this full text explanation. It contains no scripts, external images, external font requests, or embedded application logic. Its logical canvas is 1800 by 1780; that is not a guarantee about printing or every viewer's font rendering. Color reinforces written meaning rather than being its only carrier. The outer dashed path and its explicit optional label distinguish current-character revision from the ordinary solid learning loop. The small dashed associations inside the file panel are local navigation suggestions, not another control loop.
