## A complete text reading

### One character account shapes voice and choices

The upper band shows one coherent, accepted, versioned character account. Its OCEAN profile describes relatively stable tendencies: Openness, Conscientiousness, Extraversion, Agreeableness, and Neuroticism or Negative Emotionality. VAD describes modeled valence, arousal, and dominance or perceived control. Baseline expression and event-sensitive current state stay distinct; a transient state does not silently revise a stable trait. Neither profile nor modeled affect asserts felt emotion, competence, or permission to control others.

The profile and its own-voice prose express the same accepted character. Qualitative descriptions suffice; scores, a universal trait-to-affect conversion, and a separate personality controller are not required. Character shapes what the persona notices, asks, tries, corrects, and chooses when the task leaves meaningful discretion. It does not route professions or roles, prescribe tool frequency, override evidence, or guarantee a useful result. Decisive constraints may appropriately lead different personas to the same choice.

### Files are reusable prompt parts

The left panel shows ordinary persona-owned files. Every persona-authored fragment carries its characteristic perspective, including methods, experience interpretations, relationship understandings, and coordination habits. The quoted fragment is explicitly hypothetical, not a recorded event or a tested lesson. Own-voice does not require a trait label or an “I” in every sentence. Raw observations, exact quotations, and source material retain their original form and attribution.

The persona chooses useful names and organization; the pictured subjects are not a compulsory folder taxonomy. It can list, search, use supported grep-like or regular-expression matching, read exact permitted versions, and propose attributable changes. The return arrow from the host to the files represents only exact accepted changes. A proposal is not a successful save, and a failed save cannot support a claim that a lesson was retained.

### Each invocation does work and addresses maintenance

The center is the primary language model acting as that continuing persona. Every persona invocation receives the maintenance responsibility and its current limits. Every complete valid response combines its useful work with an explicit maintenance outcome: proposed changes, justified unchanged files, or an explicit blocked or bounded deferred outcome. Useful work can include a search decision, selection, a question, a peer response, coordination, or a repair within that mode's limits. Calling a persona decision a helper does not exempt it.

This is mandatory consideration and an explicit outcome, not mandatory mutation. A concise, justified unchanged outcome is valid; silent omission is not. A known unresolved correction cannot be disguised as no change. A block or deferral names the unresolved need, reason, consequences for dependent work, accepted responsibility or ownership gap, and any existing bounded opportunity to revisit it. If that opportunity expires, an honest unresolved disposition does not claim that the issue was fixed. No outcome creates a reflection wake, new allowance, or extra reflection call by itself. A failed call that produces no usable persona response remains an attempt failure; the host does not invent a no-change judgment for it.

The same primary LLM chooses the relevant exact optional fragments for its next work-scoped context. Retaining a valid selection, replacing it, and clearing it have distinct meanings; omission of a selection update preserves an existing valid choice without renewing its permission or lifetime. This selection behavior is separate from the explicit maintenance outcome required in every valid response. No additional semantic selector or background writer owns either judgment.

### Proposals and observations remain different

The right panel contains actual work and received observations: the user's need and constraints, environmental and tool results, peer replies, and evidence of the persona's own earlier choices. Proposed actions require current admission. Only actual received evidence establishes an observation; a plan, pending action, or intended effect cannot be rewritten as success. A peer can decline, a capability can fail, and a persona can wait or stop honestly. Applicable obligations and unknown effects remain visible. Dependent work requires a fresh decision that has received the relevant actual result.

### The host validates, commits, and assembles faithfully

The lower panel makes the handoff explicit. The host validates the complete response before substantive dispatch. It records the accepted maintenance disposition and commits only the exact accepted changes, with the required authorship, permissions, base-version checks, and coherent dependencies. It cannot generate a missing outcome, silently repair persona prose, or turn a rejected edit into an accepted lesson. Missing or malformed maintenance prevents new substantive dispatch from that response, including reads and selection changes. Independent cancellation, receipts, and accounting remain possible when a response is invalid. Response validation differs from later operation acceptance: a valid proposal can still fail to save, and only demonstrably independent work remains eligible under the current guards. A genuine accepted effective-character change still fences every fresh operation from that response, including reads and waits, until a fresh primary decision receives the new character and current policy.

For the next decision, the host faithfully assembles current character and relevant modeled affect, mandatory work and authority, maintenance responsibility and limits, resources, consequential observations, unresolved effects, and the primary's selected exact fragments with required qualifications. The arrows show continuing relationships, not a compulsory sequence of extra model calls. The host supplies relevant selected optional material rather than loading the entire notebook. Mandatory context does not compete with optional memories.

Search discovery, a full read, an authored selection, actual inclusion, subsequent application, and measured benefit remain distinct. A search match does not select itself. A read is an observation and does not automatically become endorsed guidance or permanent attention. Required corrections accompany usable full content; optional associations do not activate a neighborhood. Stale, unavailable, private, or oversized required context leads to the applicable bounded recovery or an explicit block, rather than silently incomplete guidance. Restricted repair still includes maintenance consideration within its existing limits; it is not permission to change prohibited text or perform substantive task work.

The bottom boundary applies throughout. Current access, processing destination, source restrictions, exact versions, budgets, and cancellation are checked independently of what a persona's files say. Real receipts, commitments, charges, and unresolved effects remain trustworthy records. A first-person fragment cannot confer permission, cancel another participant's obligation, change an actual observation, or prove its own usefulness. Maintenance, failed attempts, repair, and finishing consume the same controlling allowance.

## Scope and visual status

This is an illustration of the adopted target design. It is not a product screenshot, live persona trace, multi-call benchmark, or evidence of learning benefit. The detailed normative documents govern simplifications in the image. The loop does not require a second persona, a tool, a new lesson, a physical body, or between-task exploration for every request. Protocol coverage and recognizable prose do not establish psychological validity, behavioral influence, or better outcomes.

The current SVG is editable vector artwork with a title, accessible description, explicit labels, and this full text explanation. It contains no scripts, external images, external font requests, or embedded application logic. Its logical canvas is 1800 by 1600; that is not a guarantee about printing or every viewer's font rendering. Color reinforces written meaning rather than being its only carrier.

The revised sheet was rendered and visually inspected at its full logical size and at a 960-pixel embedded width during the 8 October 2026 documentation revision. That check covered labels, margins, arrows, and alignment with this reading; it establishes presentation only, not runtime coverage, implementation correctness, or model behavior.

This sheet replaces the prior poster and fragment-recall collections in active navigation. Their exact historical bytes and original validation scope remain linked from the [source manifest](sources/SOURCE-MANIFEST.md). No retired or untouched figure is claimed as freshly inspected.
