# Requirement reconciliation and coverage

[Human requirements](../USER-REQUIREMENTS.md) · [Design guide](../design/README.md) · [Implementation catalogue](../implementation/REQUIREMENTS.md)

This is a map of design coverage, not a pass report. It connects the original user outcomes and later clarifications to their authoritative homes and the evidence needed to assess them.

![Human requirements govern the design. Each requirement has a home and an evidence question. Examples and source reports inform the review without turning a documented rule into a runtime pass.](../assets/sources.svg)

*Read the arrows as possible, recorded transitions—not promises that a result will improve.*

The intent remains continuous even where the representation changed. The mapping does not credit historical prompt edits as numerical maturation or turn assistant mechanisms into new human requirements.

## Resolved conflicts

| Earlier wording or interpretation | Current resolution | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Character-bearing prompt fragments define the operative character. | The persona’s owned numerical model carries operative character; files remain supporting resources or historical origin evidence. | The later human clarification requires character in each persona’s own parameters. |
| A separate fine-tuning/LoRA service is the way to mature. | The persona authors literal numerical edits; the host evaluates and records them. | The subsequent direct-edit clarification supersedes the assistant’s earlier recipe. |
| Initialization creates the model and ordinary work merely reuses it. | Experience can create durable development hypotheses, parameter candidates, adoption and later consequence tracking. | A permanently static beginning does not meet the requested maturation. |
| A generic manager activates a fixed team of specialists. | General-purpose personas choose collaborators and arrangements; peers accept independently. | The user rejected the fixed specialist-team interpretation. |
| More age, edits, social messages or stored lessons proves improvement. | Trace numerical change through later behavior and useful outcomes, with retained strengths and counterexamples. | The core intent is better future work and interaction, not activity counts. |
| One new document can sit beside conflicting old rules. | All active reading paths are reconciled; exact predecessors remain historical. | The user requires one coherent, understandable published design. |

## Meaning changed; identifiers preserved

UR-004–UR-010, UR-013–UR-016, UR-018–UR-027, UR-029, UR-032 and UR-053 are read with numerical character replacing the old character-fragment mechanism where relevant. Their broader aims—truthful origins, every-call maintenance, discoverability, coherent identity and useful later work—remain. UR-028, UR-031, UR-043 and UR-048 explicitly include the requested work/social and cross-domain transfer without assuming whole-solution transfer.

UR-050 continues to reject compulsory professions, managers and external task-solving dependence. UR-052 continues to remove application quotas and approval gates without removing record integrity, actual controls or truthful limitations. The [decision record](../DESIGN-DECISIONS.md) distinguishes these obligations from the engineering mechanisms chosen to meet them.

UR-017 and UR-036–UR-042 are not active and are not reused. Their historical context remains in the [pinned predecessor](https://github.com/ai-personas/ai-personas-design/blob/60fc268eea909ea2cfc4e91cf180c0020bde6ef1/USER-REQUIREMENTS.md) and its [historical ledger](https://github.com/ai-personas/ai-personas-design/blob/60fc268eea909ea2cfc4e91cf180c0020bde6ef1/evaluation/HISTORICAL-RESULTS.md).

## Coverage of the requested examples

The [walkthrough](WORKED-EXAMPLES.md) includes three unique beginnings; house work; circuit work; more time; five-persona circuit cooperation; house-to-skyscraper and circuit-to-printer transfer; unassigned social activity; concrete ordinary-call handoffs; numerical change; tool installation; useful, absent and harmful transfer; continuation; and stopping. These are hypothetical illustrations used to expose and resolve design gaps, not a narrowing of general-purpose capability.

## What the map does not establish

Coverage of every identifier is necessary for a coherent document set, but it is not proof that every implementation path works. Actual mechanisms, behavioral influence and useful maturation require their own evidence. Unresolved empirical claims remain in [status](../implementation/STATUS.md).
