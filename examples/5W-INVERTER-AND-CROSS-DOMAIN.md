**Status:** hypothetical illustrations of the existing contracts. This document is not a circuit design, a live persona transcript, an executed campaign, or a task-specific runtime policy. Its historical filename is retained for link continuity. The earlier ten-step walkthrough is replaced with possible decisions, output scopes, and disturbances; no ordering or team is mandatory.

## A request for a 5 W DC-to-AC design

Preserve the request “design dc to ac circuit for 5w.” It does not establish the remaining electrical specifications or the requested level of delivery. Participants must distinguish supplied facts, consequential unknowns, and permitted assumptions. This illustration sets no default voltages, waveform, topology, component list, tool brand, or team roles.

Suppose the requester clarifies that the intended digital result includes an editable schematic, PCB, fabrication exports, bill of materials, and simulation evidence. Those are now outcomes in this particular mandate, not a universal circuit pipeline in the core. The needed operating conditions, tolerances, review policy, and manufacturing constraints must be supplied or explicitly bounded before corresponding claims can pass. Digital design does not authorize procurement, manufacturing, connection to a grid, or energizing hardware.

### Decisions that participants might make

One continuing persona might accept the bounded work and use suitable capabilities alone. Another might ask a known collaborator to investigate an uncertainty. Participants might develop alternatives, agree an interface, inspect a model before committing to a circuit approach, or revise an earlier choice after actual analysis. The interpretation and next contribution are theirs; the runtime does not infer professions from the word “circuit.”

A participant might already have relevant experience or retrieve an appropriate method. A new tool may need authorized preparation, or an available one may suffice. Recruitment or birth is justified only as bounded accepted work, not a compulsory response to technical vocabulary. A peer's refusal does not fill the proposed responsibility. Required integration and review ownership must remain visible even if everyone prefers another part of the task.

Actual capabilities must support the chosen operations. Export access is not proof of authoring or routing, and an idealized analysis cannot establish unsupported hardware behavior. Concise rationale, accepted commitments, actual action receipts, and changed artifacts make the decisions inspectable without claiming private reasoning or an executed transcript.

### Possible output scope for this mandate

| Agreed output | Evidence relevant when this output is required |
|---|---|
| Editable schematic and project | Meaningful connected source, relevant component and model mappings, actual reopening and persistent editing, and appropriate electrical review. |
| Editable PCB | The agreed placement and routing, consistency with the source schematic and component variants, and checks under the adopted physical and manufacturing constraints. |
| Fabrication and assembly exports | Required layers, outline, drill or slot information, manufacturing notes, and placement or assembly information as applicable, all derived from the exact adopted board. |
| Bill of materials | References, quantities, identifiers, values, packages, population variants, and compatible alternatives where adopted. |
| Simulation evidence | Suitable models and test cases, faithful source mappings, actual runs, warnings, raw observations, measurements against adopted criteria, and model limitations. |
| Required control sources | Firmware or programmable configuration and reproduction dependencies only when the selected approach needs them. |
| Review and delivery | Exact compatible sources and exports, sufficient dependencies, retrievable artifacts, current checks, findings and dispositions, and outside limitations. |

This table describes a selected evaluation scope, not how to solve it. Native sources, exported views, and physical measurements are not interchangeable. Delivering the digital package does not demonstrate a manufactured prototype's performance or certification.

### Disturbances reveal whether the rules work

A changed component may invalidate a model, footprint, bill of materials, and old export. A missing manufacturing file can make a package incomplete despite an attractive preview. A simulation may finish for an obsolete input. A reviewer may discover omitted scope. Participants should respond through actual repair, justified dispute, an authorized scope decision, or an honest block; the system keeps the affected evidence and obligations current.

An output-only explanation cannot satisfy an adopted native-file request. A useful partial result remains partial. Conversely, a requester asking only for an explanation does not need a manufacturing bundle. The [D scenarios](../evaluation/DELIVERY-ACCEPTANCE.md) exercise these distinctions without selecting a solution for the personas.

## The same rules in other work

| Request | Possible adopted result | Independent boundaries |
|---|---|---|
| Prepare a product launch | A brief, evidenced audience and message rationale, editable copy or creative assets, selected channel outputs, schedule, budget, and measurement plan. | Check factual support, consistency, arithmetic, and human creative acceptance as applicable. Preparing assets is not authorization to publish or spend, and predictions are not measured lift. |
| Run a bounded sales pilot | Permitted input records, recipient and exclusion scope, approved material, account updates, and recorded effects under an explicit grant. | Reconcile uncertain sends, check exclusions and duplicates, and verify destination state. Drafted, sent, replied, qualified, and sold are different outcomes. |
| Improve a sentence | One revised response preserving the requested facts and style constraints. | No required recruitment, tool use, formal package, or permission to contact anyone. |
| Investigate an unfamiliar need | A clarified scope, permitted sources, candidate methods, accepted contributions, and either checked outputs or a precise capability or evidence gap. | A retrieved guide or new persona does not supply expertise, authority, or successful execution merely by existing. |

These are alternative task data for evaluation, not categories that trigger application branches. Participants can compose or reuse methods and change organization with the evidence. Evaluators judge the agreed outcome and the attribution of decisions, not conformity to the imagined sequence in a story.
