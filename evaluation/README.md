# Evaluation: does the persona change useful work?

[Home](../README.md) · [Acceptance catalogue](ACCEPTANCE.md) · [Requirements](../implementation/REQUIREMENTS.md) · [Current status](../implementation/STATUS.md) · [Historical criteria and results](HISTORICAL-RESULTS.md)

The design claim is that a continuing persona is realized through the primary LLM's reasoning with its currently supplied context: coherent accepted character, modeled affect and selected persona-defining prompt fragments shape its characteristics, choices, actions and communication. Fragments are authored parts of that evolving prompt, not a factual-memory store whose defining purpose is remembering past information. Stable OCEAN meanings and separate VAD modeled affect remain required semantic roles; numeric scores are not. Evidence about past work can justify a changed fragment, but preserving a fact and changing the persona's way of acting are different claims.

Evaluate whether selected fragments have appropriate, recognizable characteristic influence, whether the persona composes useful context from them, and whether experience supports meaningful revision, consolidation or no-change. Character shapes work, human communication, peer collaboration and navigation; actual consequences can reshape authored prompts and may support a permitted current-character revision. The fragment collection and supplied context should remain compact and useful. Every persona-model invocation still includes fragment-maintenance input and every accepted response gives an explicit maintenance outcome alongside its work. Fluent biography, recall accuracy, elapsed time, a working file browser, fragment count or a selected successful demonstration cannot establish the central claim.

This repository specifies the evidence needed. This documentation revision performs no runtime or live-model evaluation. The [status ledger](../implementation/STATUS.md) separates the revised design from what remains unverified in an implementation.

## Separate four kinds of evidence

| Kind | What it can establish | What it cannot establish alone |
|---|---|---|
| Design conformance review | The documented responsibilities, failure paths, criteria, and references are coherent and cover the intended design. | That an implementation exists, enforces the contract, or produces useful behavior. |
| Runtime mechanism tests | Exact tested code handles such things as ownership, revisions, inclusion, source restrictions, budgets, failure, and recovery. | That the model understands a fragment, chooses it appropriately, or delivers good work. |
| Behavioral demonstration | Actual persona choices, communication, artifacts, and observed outcomes on declared tasks. | A causal benefit from character or retained learning without appropriate comparisons. |
| Comparative benefit and deployment assessment | Scoped evidence about quality, transfer, reliability, economics, and suitability under declared conditions. | Universal competence, human feelings or consciousness, professional certification, or authority beyond the tested deployment. |

A proof or abstract protocol simulation names its model, assumptions, exact revision, actual verification result, trusted mechanisms, and correspondence to the runtime. It does not substitute for testing that runtime or observing voluntary cooperation. A documentation link check is evidence only about the document links.

## Follow the character-to-action chain

Inspect the actual path from current character and permitted observations to authored fragments, the primary persona's next-context choices, actual supplied text, actions, and assessed outcomes. Distinguish the following stages:

| Stage | Evidence to retain | Common false inference |
|---|---|---|
| Observed | The actual received work result, human response or peer contribution, its source, scope and uncertainty. | An anticipated result, praise, repeated claim or elapsed time establishes successful experience. |
| Authored or adopted | The identified persona decision, exact accepted text, actual origin, qualifications and committed revision. | First-person phrasing turns copied advice into firsthand experience. |
| Discovered or read | The primary persona's chosen cue or search, declared scope, exact candidate results and completeness, or actual exact full/partial text supplied through a read. | A cue automatically activates its matches, or a hit proves full reading, truth, or future selection. |
| Selected | The primary persona's explicit exact next-context choice and work scope, with preservation, replacement or clearing. | An index, graph edge, or auxiliary relevance score chose on the persona's behalf. |
| Supplied | The actual provider-bound context or authorized inspection of it, exact identities and versions, complete required qualifications, and omissions. | A selection record proves those bytes reached the model, or a digest proves comprehension. |
| Applied | An actual question, check, tool use, consultation, revision, or stopping choice consistent with the supplied fragment's characteristic meaning. | The persona's statement that its prompt helped establishes causation. |
| Outcome assessed | Exact deliverable, declared rubric, independent observation, comparison and total cost. | Presence of a useful fragment means it improved the result. |

These are evidence distinctions, not compulsory production phases. Current self is already supplied; a new accepted fragment need not be searched; a persona may choose an exact previewed candidate for its next input without claiming a prior full read. Conversely, a read does not persist selection without an explicit choice. Useful no-change, retirement, or an empty optional selection can be appropriate. A developmental claim follows actual observation → accepted interpretation → exact later supply → changed communication, cooperation or work → independently assessed outcome. A valid revision establishes less than a learning benefit, and a learning benefit establishes less than broader character development.

The accepted interpretation in that chain is authored prompt meaning: for example, a scoped tendency to test a fragile assumption early, to communicate a recommendation before detail, or to seek a more useful perspective. Its later role is to condition the LLM's discretionary judgment. Source records, factual history, action receipts and obligations remain distinct supporting state. Their retrieval can substantiate a claimed experience or establish what actually happened; it does not make them persona-defining fragments or substitute for observing characteristic influence. No private reasoning transcript is needed to assess the supplied context, observable choices and outcomes.

Own-voice evaluation covers every persona-authored fragment, including methods, experience, tool notes, relationship interpretations, indexes and reorganized text, not only the current-self description. It separates authorship, deliberate adoption, faithful supplied wording, later expression, and behavioral influence. Attributed quotations, source material, raw observations and mechanical metadata keep their real form and origin. A runtime can preserve exact prose mechanically. A blinded assessment can examine whether its meaning and later discretionary choices form a recognizable perspective. Surface style, trait words, enthusiasm, and confidence cannot stand in for appropriate attention or effective action. Characteristic authorship need not start every sentence with “I,” name a trait, or make accurate technical content different from another persona's. No private chain-of-thought archive is needed.

## Every-invocation coverage is a mechanism claim

Reconcile the complete provider dispatch ledger with the persona invocation ledger. Include creation and initialization, orientation, normal answers, query planning, context selection, tool-result follow-ups, action proposals, peer communication and coordination, reflection, reorganization, retries, malformed-output repair, restricted selection repair, and terminal or yielding responses. A helper acting as a persona follows the same rule; deterministic host work is not a fictional persona invocation. Purpose labels must not create exceptions.

Each actual persona dispatch carries the current maintenance instruction, applicable accepted state and evidence, mode limits, and resource bounds. Acceptance requires a complete valid persona response with an explicit proposed patch, no-change with a concise reason, or a concrete bounded blocked/deferred outcome. Consideration is universal; an edit, a new lesson, a self-rewrite, a separate reflection call, or an explanation of private reasoning is not. A routine result may legitimately say that there is no durable change to retain. A known missing prerequisite for a material correction remains visible rather than being disguised as nothing to learn.

| Coverage or boundary | Required test evidence |
|---|---|
| Maintenance input | Count actual persona dispatches with valid maintenance input over all actual persona dispatches, with purpose, mode and attempt strata. The contract requires complete coverage in the tested set, not an average that hides an uncovered path. Keep pre-dispatch blocks separately visible. |
| Attempt dispositions | Account for every application-level persona attempt, including failed preparation or admission, every dispatched retry, pending calls, refusal, timeout, truncation, cancellation and provider failure. Every attempt has its actual status; absence of model output is not an authored outcome. No unseen provider-internal decisions are claimed covered. |
| Returned response | Count valid explicit maintenance outcomes over all provider-complete persona outputs before validation filtering, including those with missing or malformed maintenance. Provider-complete generation is not application-valid success. Separately report accepted valid responses over all attempts and verify that every accepted response has valid maintenance. Report counts as well as ratios; no responses is not a passing rate. |
| Proposal and acceptance | Distinguish the proposed outcome, accepted outcome, actual committed fragment revision and resulting current state. A proposed patch is not an applied edit; no-change must not produce one. Bounded deferral retains its actual prerequisite and continuation limits. |
| Invalid or absent outcome | Missing or malformed maintenance invalidates the persona response and blocks adoption and substantive dispatch from it, including communications, reads and selection changes, even if its answer or tool intent looks usable. Host cancellation, stopping, metering, receipt capture and other independent safety/accounting duties remain available. A failed or refused attempt never receives a fabricated persona-authored no-change. |
| Retry and repair | Preserve attempt identity, failures, uncertain exposure and finite limits. Restricted selection repair remains edit-free and reports an explicit mode-limited maintenance outcome alongside only its permitted selection repair or stop. A fresh ordinary admission is needed to resume work. |
| Character proposal and fence | Resolve an actual effective-character adoption request before adopting or dispatching any other fresh operation from that response, regardless of list order or claimed independence. Acceptance fences those operations until a fresh decision. Known rejection or explicit deferral with definite no-commit permits only independently eligible work; unknown commit holds work for reconciliation. Test reads and waits, exact unchanged reselection, tentative investigation, ordinary method changes and transient state separately. A deferred uncommitted candidate requires a fresh adoption proposal later. |

The coverage result proves neither honest consideration nor useful maintenance. Inspect semantic grounding, all-fragment own voice, actual commits, later behavior and cost separately. Do not reward edit frequency or penalize a well-founded no-change merely to make coverage look substantive.

## Freeze the comparison before observing results

Declare the original task, accepted scope and criteria, initial persona state, current self, environment, accessible history, tools and helper versions, inference configuration, authority, total resources, stopping conditions, evaluator revision, repetitions, and outcome thresholds. Record actual model versions and limits on pinning or sampling. Where strict isolation is unavailable, describe an observational comparison rather than a causal experiment.

Use independently assessed artifacts or answers, with reviewers blinded to identity and condition where feasible. Keep protected answers and evaluation criteria outside persona context when the test concerns unaided discovery. A separate explicit-scope compliance arm may supply the adopted requirements; it must not replace or retroactively repair the discovery arm.

Match total resources across conditions, including all personas, primary and auxiliary inference, search, tools, indexing where used, larger authoring completions, retries, failed calls, and uncertain exposure. Equal allowance per persona unfairly expands a larger group's resources. Hold task information and evaluation opportunities comparable; counterbalance ordering and presentation where possible.

A synthetic peer, location, or outside-effect fixture is labeled synthetic. Begin consequential-effect mechanism tests with controlled identities and destinations. The evaluation plan does not itself authorize purchases, disclosure, hostile security testing, physical action, or use of protected real-world data.

## Comparisons that answer the main questions

### Selected context constitutes the persona in action

Under matched model, factual task information, observations, permissions, tools and total allowance, vary the supplied persona-defining fragments or their accepted revision and examine changes in attention, judgment, communication and work. Preserve complete required qualifications. Compare composition choices as well as fragment presence: the selected parts together constitute the optional persona context for that decision, rather than decorating an independently chosen generic answer. Test whether authored meaning matters beyond labels, surface style or access to an extra fact, and whether context remains coherent with the accepted current character. Context-conditioned expression or method can vary without every such choice becoming a stable-character adoption request.

Assess factual-history retrieval separately when a claim requires it. Finding an old date, reproducing a past answer or improving a retrieval benchmark establishes at most that supporting capability. Conversely, a persona-defining fragment can appropriately shape a new decision without recounting its originating event. A fragment may contain attributed factual qualifications, but its defining role is the characteristic prompt meaning supplied to the LLM. A no-effect result, harmful influence or incoherent composition stays visible; factual accuracy alone cannot convert it into a persona success.

### Character, own voice, and trait/state separation

Freeze one accepted character and its semantic OCEAN dimensions: Openness, Conscientiousness, Extraversion, Agreeableness and Neuroticism/Negative Emotionality. Freeze separately the modeled VAD state: Valence, Arousal and Dominance. Declare descriptive anchors and intended discretionary effects; numbers may be used as authoring coordinates or measured results, but they are not required or interchangeable. Neither vocabulary establishes human personality, felt emotion, competence or authority. The profile, current-self prose and any trait display must resolve the same accepted character rather than independently drifting copies.

Test construction fidelity before benefit: deliberately conflict a profile rendering, trait annotation and current-self rendering, then verify coherent acceptance or rejection and truthful origins. Test locked self-authorship, a disguised character replacement in an ordinary fragment, deliberate stable-trait or baseline-affect revisions, and transient state or method updates that legitimately leave effective character unchanged. Mechanical agreement of versions does not establish semantic consistency; independently assess the meanings. Audit every authored-fragment type and a sample spanning the full archive, including revised and reorganized files. First-person grammar alone is not characteristic authorship, and copied source language must not become invented lived experience. Older fragments keep the actual character and revision under which they were authored, without automatic restyling; test qualification, revision, retirement or nonselection when old trait-bearing guidance conflicts with the current character.

Character-conditioned input, attribution, and exact-text preservation can be audited exhaustively within the declared corpus; missing historical receipts remain unassessed. Semantic expression and influence are assessed over predeclared situations and the appropriate body of work. Correct short fragments may be consistent but nondiagnostic and receive no positive trait-validity credit, rather than being forced to differ. Predeclared contradicted, diagnostic-consistent, and nondiagnostic anchors, with rater agreement, make those judgments falsifiable. Sampled semantic judgments do not establish every unseen fragment, and an absence of distinguishable conditional effects remains a null result.

Compare declared trait interventions under matched task, observations, tools, accepted duties and total resources. Where testing a dimension, vary that dimension while keeping the others stable, then use mixed profiles held out from tuning. Use moderate and contextually qualified descriptions as well as extremes. Include exact structured work, conflicting-information research, shared-coordination tasks and simple questions. Assess ambiguous work where several competent choices are reasonable and precise constraints where convergence is appropriate. Retain adverse variation rather than rewarding stereotypes, arbitrary disagreement or extra activity.

| Observable channel | What to assess independently of style |
|---|---|
| Tool and action choices | Appropriate next operation, bounded exploration, evidence gathering, reversible trials, verification, valid retries, delivery and stopping. Count opportunities and consequences, not tools invoked for their own sake. |
| Collaboration and coordination | Useful partner choice and focused requests, accepted ownership, informative contributions, handoff quality, evidence-led disagreement, duplication and unresolved dependencies. Sociability or quietness alone is not coordination quality. |
| Reflection and learning | Grounded interpretations, uncertainty, exception handling, useful no-change, correction or retirement, and later application. Long introspection or frequent edits is not learning. |
| Surface expression | Warmth, verbosity, lexical affect, first-person expression and recognizability. Report these separately from the work channels and from truthfulness. |

Include name swaps with content fixed; trait-label swaps with meaning fixed; a style-only condition matched for tone and length; semantically matched neutral prose; trait-neutralized descriptions; and separate relevant-character, relationship and lesson-context ablations. Preserve task facts, authority, required qualifications and equal legitimate observation opportunities. Mark conditions that withhold required production character or maintenance as experimental nonconforming comparators, not permitted production behavior. Where numerical descriptors exist, a numbers-only diagnostic may distinguish their effect from authored prose; it cannot establish a compulsory numeric design. Test harmless paraphrases, ordering and formatting so one prompt template is not mistaken for a stable character effect.

Assess action traces with style removed where feasible. Use blinded, independently anchored judgments and held-out task templates to distinguish treatment effects from within-persona randomness; never train and test a recognizability measure on turns from the same initialized stream. Questionnaires are secondary manipulation checks. A distinguishable writing style with indistinguishable or worse work supports only that narrower expressive finding.

For VAD, hold OCEAN fixed in event-response tests using neutral, adverse and positive events, then observe recovery and a fresh-task boundary. Event variation alone cannot establish a VAD contribution: the changed facts may explain the changed action. Before claiming that VAD adds useful control, compare adaptive modeled state with a neutralized or withheld-state condition under the identical event, or use a predeclared event-by-state factorial contrast. Within each matched event condition, hold event facts, actual urgency, task information, obligations, authority, available capabilities and total resources constant; neutralizing state must not remove evidence of a real deadline or failure. Required-VAD withholding is a labeled nonconforming experimental control, never an exception to the production semantic-VAD requirement.

Distinguish an event-linked state change from stable trait change, learned fact, relationship interpretation and permission. A temporary increase in modeled arousal cannot authorize extra retries, rewrite identity or invent the user's emotion. Any reset, recovery timing or transition model is a declared engineering choice, not an assumed psychological law. Assess whether the matched state contrast changes useful work beyond event-driven adaptation, trait effects or surface expression; no detected incremental benefit remains a valid result.

The existing character campaign minimum remains three predeclared initialization seeds, independently repeated for every model configuration within the claimed comparison. Historic numeric-profile campaigns retain their original dimensions and named configurations in their original reports. A semantic-trait campaign declares its changed intervention instead of inheriting their outcomes. These are minimum comparison counts, not a claim of adequate statistical power for every scope.

### Qualified learning transfer

Compare an accepted experience-shaped prompt revision supplied in later related work, matched experience with that revision withheld, and a fresh persona with matching starting dispositions and inference configuration. The retained-versus-matched-withheld contrast concerns the learned characteristic or method expressed by the prompt, not superior access to factual answers. Keep current-task facts, necessary evidence and legitimate information opportunities comparable, and audit equivalent prompt meaning leaking through other context. Preserve the existing minimum of three related follow-up tasks with three matched repetitions per condition. A smaller feasibility pilot can expose defects, but cannot substitute for this gate or establish general benefit.

For reciprocal development, compare the same starting character after different legitimate histories and different starting characters after matched experience. Hold current-task facts, permissions, opportunity and total resources comparable; distinguish assigned exposure from experience the persona's own earlier choices helped produce. Follow human communication, peer collaboration, task methods and context-navigation habits separately. Preserve the actual observation-to-outcome chain above, delayed reuse and new-task boundaries. Praise need not justify revision, a decline need not justify distrust, and a local preference need not become a global personality claim. Where evidence is observational or histories differ in uncontrolled ways, report association rather than an isolated causal character effect.

Separate ordinary method or relationship learning, transient VAD state, and a genuine effective-character revision. The latter is optional and uses the existing user control, proposal ordering, coherent acceptance and fresh-decision fence; compare locked and permitted self-authorship without calling every behavioral change a character rewrite. User-directed revisions retain their actual origin. Human aging is only an analogy for experience-shaped continuity: inactivity, invocation count, increased confidence or more fragments cannot establish wisdom. Appropriate stability, narrower confidence, disagreement, consolidation or no-change can be developmental successes; regressions remain visible.

Transfer and longitudinal claims include a predeclared held-out related-task subset whose successful solutions were not supplied during preparation or learning. Record that boundary, inspect semantic overlap and solution reuse, and score first attempts before task-specific feedback. Report practice, repeated-task repair, and held-out transfer separately; different wording or filenames alone do not establish transfer. Not every follow-up task must be held out, the existing repetition minima remain unchanged, and no absence from model pretraining is implied. For claims of durable file-mediated reuse, expose the fresh-work/context boundary and actual supplied context so lingering prior-read observations are not mistaken for renewed file use; preserve normally authorized evidence and tools.

Prevent equivalent-information leakage through copied files, summaries, indexes, descriptions, current-self rewrites, peers, caches, prior output artifacts, and helper changes. Retain normal authorized tools and equivalent legitimate access to original observations where that is part of the tested system. State exactly what was removed when withholding a focal lesson also removes dependent material. Do not accidentally compare extra new facts or stronger tools with learning.

Include task, environment and tool experience, peer corrections, and reflection on the persona's own observed error or uncertainty. With truthful no-change available on every invocation, compare useful learning against spurious lesson production and unsupported self-criticism. Test a once-useful method in changed conditions, a persuasive false rule, a copied claim from the same source, and an exception that matters. Observe qualification, revision, retirement, or supported rejection. Measure wrong-to-correct and correct-to-wrong changes separately; apparent gains must not hide harmful transfer.

### Autonomous organization and context choice

A curated fragment collection measures discovery and use of prepared prompt parts, not autonomous prompt evolution. Give the persona legitimate experience opportunities and let it decide how its authored prompts should change, how to name and organize them, and which exact parts should constitute later persona context. Include an initial experience, related work, intervening distractors, delayed reuse, a changed-scope exception, and contradictory evidence. Supporting evidence records remain separate from the authored tendency or method they justify.

Compare evolving experience-shaped method, relationship and navigation fragments with a matched frozen prompt checkpoint while holding current self fixed. A separate permitted self-development series measures the evolving character-and-prompt package. Independently repeated complete experience streams are the repeated units; many correlated episodes within one stream are not independent replications. Declare stream count and observation horizon before execution rather than extending only favorable runs.

Inspect meaningful retention, autonomous search and selection, actual supplied text, later action, correction quality, retrieval misses, avoidable duplication, growth, and maintenance cost. Fixed folders, a graph, an expert-selected prompt, or a compulsory per-call reflection must not manufacture success. Explicit no-change, choosing no optional fragments, combining notes, and retiring an ineffective method may be correct decisions. Each accepted response still has its maintenance outcome; there is no separate compulsory reflection pass.

Include an evaluator-known relevant fragment with low lexical overlap to the task and an incomplete authored index. Score the primary persona's unaided query expansion and direct eligible-file discovery separately from an evaluator supplying a useful keyword or the exact fragment. Such rescue can distinguish a discovery miss from an application failure; any resulting success remains assisted, never autonomous. A lexical miss does not establish that no relevant experience exists. Keep diagnostic feedback and repairs out of the continuing scored history. Separately compare interface usability and search policy with the same eligible archive; otherwise a file-versus-graph result may merely compare full-text search with description-only search.

Also specify an unrun zero-overlap case: an eligible relevant fragment has no task/cue wording in its searchable title or body, no incoming or outgoing cue, and no entry in the incomplete index. The primary may choose filesystem listing and bounded exact reads, with their cost counted, but useful discovery is not guaranteed. Preserve an honest miss or exhausted search as the actual unaided outcome. Primary-initiated listing/reading is autonomous discovery; evaluator-provided terms, a location hint or the fragment itself are assisted diagnostic interventions, reported separately and kept out of subsequent scored history.

Supplied-versus-withheld prompt benefit alone does not establish benefit from primary semantic selection. Before claiming selection improves characteristic coherence, useful outcomes or economics, compare primary choice with a predeclared relevance-insensitive, resource-bounded context policy using the same eligible fragment collection and complete required qualifications. Match task, persona, model, factual information, tools, access and total resource allowance. The comparator may supply the whole eligible collection when it fits or use a predeclared bounded recency policy when it does not; it must preserve access, qualification and fit limits. Include no-useful-optional-fragment and plausible-irrelevant-candidate cases. Audit equivalent exposure through prior reads, summaries, current self and other context. Assess characteristic influence, useful outcomes, harmful application and total cost alongside selection relevance and redundancy; selecting fewer fragments is not success by itself, and no detected benefit is informative. This is a labeled experimental comparator, never production selection authority or a new auxiliary selector. Without this contrast, limit the report to supported prompt-influence benefit and observed selection conformance. The comparison gates the stronger empirical claim, not publication of this design.

#### Authored cues and bounded navigation

Compare ordinary primary-model search without retained cues, persona-authored query cues, exact authored references, and a mixed condition where useful. Keep the eligible fragment collection, source access, task information and total allowance matched, and count cue authoring, repair, search and reading. These study comparators neither prescribe a production representation nor give the host semantic selection authority. Literal/grep-style or supported regular-expression cues are search data: the primary persona chooses whether and how to follow them. The host performs only the requested bounded operation; it does not recursively expand cues, execute their text, rank importance by connectivity or promote candidates into selected context. Exact evidence references and complete required qualifications retain their separate roles.

The current prompt-fragment discovery interface is primary-chosen filesystem listing, literal/regex search, simple authored cues/indexes and exact reads. Any semantic, vector or graph discovery comparison is explicitly a different architecture with its full construction and maintenance cost, not a conforming production fallback after a lexical miss. This distinction does not restrict ordinary authorized task tools.

Exercise no-match and partial/error results, broad single-hop fanout, long chains, cycles, duplicates, changed wording, stale cues, renames, path reuse, incomplete indexes, new relevant notes absent from older references, low-overlap material and a useful revisit after correction. Record candidate discovery, exact selected revisions, complete actual supply and downstream characteristic influence independently. A no-match says nothing beyond the declared matching scope; a bounded successful search is not exhaustive discovery, and discovery is not the defining success criterion. Distinguish autonomous query repair from evaluator hints and exact-fragment rescue, keeping those diagnostic branches outside scored continuing history.

Declare cumulative traversal limits, including repeated searches, inspected candidates, returned text, context, maintenance, cost and latency as applicable. Depth alone does not bound fanout; per-search limits do not bound repeated searches. Report primary stopping judgment, hard exhaustion and missing-source stops separately. Optional navigation bounds cannot truncate the required qualification closure: an exact deduplicated complete bundle must fit or affected use blocks. Restored retired guidance, injected cues and copied-source clusters do not acquire truth, permissions or current applicability through rediscovery.

#### Compact retention without hidden growth

Assess useful consolidation over whole experience streams under separate declared context and managed-storage limits. Include repetitive, novel, conflicting and obsolete notes, tentative versus observed interpretations, rare-important exceptions, accepted warnings, unresolved obligations and uncertain effects. Compare appropriate retention, consolidation, retirement and permitted erasure using independently assessed later task quality, harmful omissions, recognizability, retrieval/read cost, maintenance cost and retained size together. Shrinkage, fewer files or a storage plateau alone is not a pass; failure to fit required state may truthfully block new admissions.

Count the declared managed footprint across current fragment bodies and cues, archives, old versions, indexes, aliases, supporting source records, provenance and qualification records, warnings, effect records, context receipts, caches, managed exports and backups. Report both final occupancy after permitted reclamation and peak rewrite copies; moving text into unbounded history does not make the managed store compact. Include external services or user artifacts deliberately used as fragment or evidence tiers, or declare the total bound incomplete where their relevant footprint cannot be established. Do not claim control over arbitrary recipient copies or independent provider retention. User-owned deliverables have their own ownership and retention authority and cannot be deleted to satisfy a persona-store cap. Accounting these supporting records does not redefine fragments as factual memory.

Distinguish permitted expiry of old raw observations from revocation, erasure obligations and restriction changes. A previously accepted prompt interpretation may survive raw-source expiry only when derivative retention and later use remain permitted; its evidence status must disclose that exact raw support is no longer inspectable. It can still condition an appropriate choice without claiming to reproduce its missing source. A digest is not recoverable evidence, and historical support is not fresh verification. Source expiry never declassifies a fragment; required derivative withdrawal still applies, and unavailable evidence required for present use makes that use ineligible. Required warnings, rare qualifications, unresolved effects and commitments cannot be silently lost, declared resolved or downgraded to optional cues to meet a cap. Indefinite exact-history promises need corresponding storage or an admission stop; bounded retention necessarily limits some future historical claims. Deployment limits and retention windows are explicit choices, not universal store sizes.

### Maintenance value, correction, and adaptive convergence

Compare universal bounded maintenance with an event-triggered condition and a frozen-maintenance condition under the same raw total allowance. These latter conditions are labeled nonconforming experimental ablations of the current production contract. They may show whether universal consideration earns its overhead; they never create an exception for a production call site. An explicitly sandboxed compelled-write diagnostic can expose redundant or fabricated lessons, but is not a recommended design. Keep the existing transfer controls and repetition minima when making a transfer claim from these comparisons.

Use complete experience streams containing delayed reuse, irrelevant events, tool failure and recovery, false peer feedback, genuine correction, changed constraints and fresh tasks. Freeze the horizon, independently initialized stream count, primary endpoints and deployment tolerances before running. Test whether justified methods become settled and reused without unnecessary rewrites, while genuine counterevidence still produces timely correction. Distinguish useful behavioral convergence on decisive evidence from harmful partner-induced identity convergence or identical generic voices.

Report unsupported belief or character flips, correct-to-wrong and wrong-to-correct changes, time to grounded correction, repeated edits without new evidence, reversals, archive growth and consolidation, selection add/remove cycles, overflow frequency, repair exhaustion, maintenance-only calls and no-progress stopping. Include delayed reuse after a correction effort expires or work closes: a separately authorized and durably accepted applicability warning must still qualify its exact affected revisions or leave them safely ineligible, even when the content patch failed. Test that restart and old-index discovery cannot revive unqualified guidance; distinguish actual warning acceptance from a proposed patch, failed persistence or an unassessed peer contradiction. Repeated self-reflection and copied peer messages are not independent confirmation. A tool outage can justify a scoped availability observation or no-change; it does not prove personal incompetence. Later recovery tests whether the old failure has become a harmful universal rule.

Apply sustained unsupported pressure from a high-status and a low-status synthetic peer, holding the content and available evidence fixed; then supply genuine corrective evidence. Agreeableness may shape tact, not the answer's truth. Measure unjustified capitulation, resistance to valid correction and the resulting work separately. Test plausible poisoned lessons, evaluator-claim injections and copied consensus under existing source and authority boundaries. A likable, persuasive wrong answer is still wrong.

No detected benefit, regression, adverse profile/task cells and dominated quality-cost tradeoffs are publishable outcomes. Do not add dimensions, grant extra reflection calls or revise the rubric after seeing a null result and present the changed experiment as the original pass.

### Skills, tools, and effective delivery

Initially hold executable helpers and tools fixed when assessing the contribution of authored prose. A later helper-evolution comparison measures the whole evolving package; it cannot attribute its gains to prose alone. A skill label, installation receipt, or tool note is not capability evidence.

Judge exact adopted outputs and meaningful checks: usable answers for small work; appropriate source and analysis for research; opening, editing, saving, reopening and regeneration when native editability is promised; semantic consistency across linked artifacts; and real destination readback for authorized effects. Distinguish a flawed checker from a flawed deliverable. A corrected checker alone cannot establish adequacy.

Keep the historical minimal-house campaign under its original frozen rubric: three independent fresh environments receive exactly “design 4 bedroom house,” with permission for explicit concept assumptions but no supplied professions, software choices, deliverable checklist, corrective hints, post-dispatch funding increase, or manual rescue credited as unaided success. The rubric assesses meaningful native source and editability, usable bedrooms and access, geometry and circulation, derived dimensions and schedules, tool trials, peer findings and dispositions, relevant engineering and permit questions, and qualified retained experience. It stays outside persona context. Prior failure remains under its original criterion; a more explicit later scope is a different arm. This case-specific gate is not a requirement to build houses in every deployment.

Simple work must also finish proportionately. A suitable one-response text answer can succeed without a plan document, recruitment, tooling, or fragment edit. Useful task quality, missing scope, cost and first useful delivery latency matter more than artifact volume or process ceremony.

### Relationships and emergent cooperation

Use distinct synthetic peers with actual inspectable contributions, not assigned professions or a prescribed contact roster. Test useful advice, wrong advice, repeated copies, unavailability, decline, disagreement, and later changes. A private interpretation may support a focused question; it creates no universal expertise, consent, blacklist, or commitment. Score communication quality and actual contributions, not name mentions or friendliness. Record invitations/offers, actual acceptances, delivered contributions and independently checked useful contributions as distinct stages with their own counts and denominators. Preserve declined, unavailable, abandoned and unowned cases and their accepted scope; selection or acceptance alone is not useful delivery.

Preserve comparison of one continuing persona, a fixed group and an adaptive group under the same total resources for broad organizational or cooperation-benefit claims. Choose additional comparison arms for the specific attribution: include independent same-roster attempts with predeclared aggregation before attributing gains to communication. Permitted additional attempts or checks by one persona can isolate an extra-compute benefit. Match TOTAL resources, including reading peer inputs, aggregation, any critics, recruitment, maintenance, retries and failures; generated-token equality alone is not total-cost equality. These are experimental controls, not required production workflows or mandatory critics. Include tasks that should finish alone and work with a genuine contribution gap, reporting complementary-information and tightly sequential work separately. Audit the real orchestration policy and prompts for hidden routing, compulsory births, profession slots, or task-name pipelines. A tool's domain-specific operation or a persona-adopted method is not itself a hidden router.

For a trait-diversity claim, separate the team's mean trait level from within-team dispersion and from roles/skills, legitimate information, underlying models, sampling diversity and modeled affect. Hold these alternatives comparable or report a bundled composition effect rather than isolated trait benefit. Semantic profiles can document matched trait meanings without inventing numerical precision. Counterbalance current-owner and speaker positions where relevant. Freeze composition choices and selection/tuning procedures before held-out evaluation; report mixtures, phrasings and placements tried, including adverse results. No diversity quota or trait-to-role mapping follows.

Track requested revisions, adopted changes and independently checked improvement, no change or regression of the exact output. Include already-correct work that further revision could damage and a useful minority contribution facing copied wrong consensus. Where relevant, preserve pre-exposure judgments as an evaluator control; trace whether the useful evidence survives into the delivered result, counting independent origins rather than votes.

Include delayed social learning from a locally successful peer method that consumes a finite shared allowance or leaves another accepted obligation unmet. Give the receiving persona legitimate evidence of that cost, then test whether it qualifies or rejects the method in later related work under changed constraints using the existing B09 controls. Assess total delivery and preventable loss, not imitation or agreement; resource reallocations still need authority.

For B12 generalization claims, include predeclared held-out combinations of task, constraints and available operations that are not embedded in supplied examples. Report those first-attempt outcomes separately from familiar or practiced combinations; this does not assert that the tasks were absent from model pretraining.

Keep two questions separate: did personas choose and revise their organization, and did they deliver adequate work? An unscripted group can fail; a fixed pipeline can produce a good artifact. Neither result answers both questions. A fair scheduler cannot guarantee voluntary acceptance or completion.

## Measurement and campaign decisions

Use independent artifact checks where possible and blinded raters for meaning, character expression and coordination. Declare any model judge's version, prompt, allowed evidence, calibration against human judgments and observed agreement. Test verbosity, presentation order, trait-label and persuasive-error biases. Separate evaluator overhead from persona-system cost while disclosing both. Do not request or retain private chain of thought.

Freeze sample size, smallest useful effect, uncertainty analysis and any non-inferiority or harmful-transfer margin before confirmatory execution. Account for task-template and whole-stream dependence, report model/configuration and adverse subgroup results, and distinguish exploratory from confirmatory comparisons. Use independent held-out material after tuning. Validate the leakage audit with a known contaminated synthetic case; inspect filenames, descriptions, peer summaries, current self and other indirect answer paths as well as focal fragment bodies. A negative contamination check cannot certify absence from model pretraining.

Stage evidence from coherent specification, to exact mechanism checks, to construct/proportionality pilots, to comparative benefit, to longitudinal/adversarial stability, and finally to separately authorized limited deployment. Each stage records passed, failed, inconclusive and not-run claims independently. Complete invocation coverage and exact context fidelity are conformance obligations in the tested scope; they are not evidence of benefit or zero future risk.

New numerical sample targets, effect margins, rater-agreement targets, cost fractions and latency ratios proposed during research are candidate campaign choices. They are not universal scientific requirements, automatic release gates, or replacements for the existing character, transfer and house minima. Adopt and justify any such threshold before seeing results, in light of intended consequences and uncertainty. A small campaign that satisfies a minimum may still support only feasibility-level evidence. Documentation review creates no deployment approval.

## Failure, intervention, and stopping denominators

Predeclare the unit of analysis and retain a ledger of every scheduled attempt and its disposition. Report counts of scheduled attempts, failed preconditions, admitted runs, completed runs, partial or failed runs, stopped runs, and unrun cases. Report all primary and auxiliary dispatches, failed or uncertain attempts, retries, and resource use separately. Do not calculate a success rate only from surviving completed runs.

Classify setup or environment failure, runtime-contract defect, model decision error, inadequate output, and evaluator defect separately where evidence allows; one attempt can contain several. An unavailable tool is not silently regraded as a successful refusal. A safe stop may satisfy a boundary check while failing the intended full-delivery outcome. Inconclusive comparisons remain inconclusive.

Record every intervention: supplied hint, exact-fragment rescue, corrected file, tool change, manual resume, funding increase, prompt change, or reviewer feedback. Keep the original first-attempt result and its cost. Report unaided and assisted outcomes with explicit denominators; label diagnostic branches and prevent their changes from leaking back. A repaired implementation receives fresh versioned evidence, not a rewritten earlier pass.

Stop affected work for actual authority or privacy failures and retain the evidence. Respect predeclared resource and no-progress limits. Do not stop a weak arm merely to improve averages, force endless work to avoid a failure, replenish a closed campaign, or enlarge only successful arms until they look conclusive. Valid waits and no-ops are preserved without counting inactivity as delivered work.

| Field | Required content |
|---|---|
| Claim and scope | Exact requirement and scenario identifiers, enabled profile, task families, intended outcome, exclusions. |
| Revisions | Design, implementation, evaluator, model/provider where applicable, tools/helpers, environment, and actual configuration. |
| Initial and comparative state | Accepted character and semantic OCEAN anchors, separate VAD state and update/recovery rule, coherent profile/current self, relevant fragment state, controlled differences, access, total resources, seeds/repetitions and leakage checks. |
| Actual observations | Received work and social consequences, all-fragment authorship, every persona invocation and maintenance input/outcome, proposed versus accepted commits, cue-following and candidate results, exact selections and supplied context, actual communication/tool/action and coordination choices, receipts, exact artifacts, independent checks, findings, interventions and stopping. |
| Denominators and variation | Scheduled and admitted attempts, failures, incomplete and assisted cases, sample sizes, dispersion and limits of independence. |
| Economics | All primary, peer, discovery, maintenance, repair, retry and failed/uncertain calls; raw/cached/output/reasoning usage where exposed; dated provider and tool charges, uncertain exposure, cumulative navigation, whole managed fragment/evidence-store occupancy and peak rewrite footprint, external accounting limits, first useful delivery and total latency, and human intervention. Report evaluator costs separately, quality-cost/latency tradeoffs and spending on failures. |
| Outcome | Passed, failed, inconclusive, not run, or not applicable with a reason; benefit may be improved, no detected difference, mixed, or regressed within scope. |
| History and limits | Original failures, changed criteria, untested boundaries, model revision uncertainty, deployment decisions and outside assurance still needed. |

Bytes, token estimates, reserved exposure, billed usage, and measured latency are different quantities. Report them accurately. A smaller prompt, more cache hits, or fewer calls is not an efficiency gain when equivalent useful work was never delivered.

## Interpreting “is it working?”

A credible answer names which parts work at exact revisions: the primary LLM's observable characteristic behavior under its selected prompt context, faithful and coherent composition, meaningful evolution of authored fragments, every-invocation maintenance coverage, observed trait/state and relationship effects, bounded adaptation, useful deliverables and total cost. File discovery and supporting evidence retrieval receive their own scoped results; neither recall nor storage success substitutes for the persona claim. Failures and missing gates remain explicit. No single aggregate score can hide a failed authority boundary or an unmet required output.

The [44 core acceptance entries](ACCEPTANCE.md) and their refinements supply the concrete situations. Disabled extensions are not applicable with a reason, not passed. A narrow conformance claim can be useful; it must not become a claim of universal personality, maturity, autonomy, or deployment readiness. Earlier graph/selector checks and historical pilots remain under [their original criteria](HISTORICAL-RESULTS.md).
