## A changed design does not change an old result

The prior design baseline is [f4549bd594e6a0cf5205ac031ed422ab269def8d](https://github.com/ai-personas/ai-personas-design/tree/f4549bd594e6a0cf5205ac031ed422ab269def8d). Links on this page pin earlier criteria to that revision unless a different revision is explicitly named. They preserve history, not a second current specification. No historical file must be studied to understand the current design.

The current target, revised October 8, 2026, treats fragments as persona-defining authored prompt parts. Selected fragments constitute the persona context with which the primary LLM reasons and realizes characteristic choices, actions and communication; experience can change those authored prompts. They are not a factual-memory store. Their interface uses persona-authored first-person files, primary-chosen filesystem listing and literal or supported regular-expression search, simple authored cues/indexes, exact reading, and primary-owned next-context choice. Semantic OCEAN stable-trait dimensions, separate VAD modeled affect, one coherent accepted character, characteristic own voice across every authored fragment, and maintenance input and valid accepted outcomes on every persona invocation remain required.

This target supersedes required graph navigation, the former conditional graph-delegation contract and optional independent semantic selection, graph-specific structured disposition forms, and compulsory numeric character descriptors. Mandatory consideration is restored as an explicit current obligation; a new lesson, edit, separate reflection call or self-rewrite is not. The previous Jev selector was optional under authorized delegation, not mandatory for every old run. Semantic, vector or graph discovery may be studied only as explicitly different architectural comparators, not conforming fallbacks for lexical fragment discovery. Ordinary task tools remain available within their own grants. The strict on-demand-only, no-persistent-selection target in the earlier file proposal is also superseded: a primary-authored work-scoped selection can persist under the current contract.

Three dispositions below must not be confused:

- Carried concern: an underlying authority, continuity, usability, evidence, or behavioral obligation survives in current core criteria; its old graph- or provider-specific setup does not become a requirement for all implementations
- Superseded architecture: an old mechanism is no longer necessary for conformance; its original tests and outcomes remain valid history for that mechanism, never converted to current passes or erased failures
- Retained campaign: a declared historical behavioral task or comparison retains its original rubric, controls, minima, failures, and intervention record; a changed setup requires a new evaluator revision

The 21 invariants and 45 requirement identifiers remain active. M01–M26, B01–B12, and X01–X06 remain the active catalogue, with explicitly revised file/context refinements. The tables below preserve supplemental identifiers as traceability, not additional active runtime objects or another required catalogue. Similar labels never establish equivalent tested behavior.

## October 8 applicability and the maintenance change

The immediately preceding file-first wording made OCEAN and VAD optional and allowed ordinary no-edit responses without an explicit maintenance disposition. That wording no longer defines the current target. OCEAN and VAD now have required, distinct semantic roles without required scores. Profile and current-self representations resolve the same accepted character; historical numeric values remain what their original campaign actually supplied.

Every dispatched persona-model invocation now receives maintenance input, including initialization, context selection, tool follow-up, peer coordination, retries, repair and final answers. An accepted persona response contains a proposed patch, reasoned no-change, or bounded blocked/deferred outcome alongside its work. Missing or malformed maintenance invalidates the response and blocks substantive dispatch, while independent host safety and accounting continue. A failed or refused generation retains its actual failure status; it is not retroactively filled with a persona-authored no-change.

This change does not reinstate graph machinery or create another reflection author. Restricted selection repair stays edit-free and supplies a mode-limited maintenance outcome. At that October 8 revision, next-context updates were optional and omission preserved a valid choice. The 10 October explicit-intent correction supersedes that policy: every accepted response now states its fragment/resource next-context disposition; prior omission-allowing results receive no retrospective pass. A genuine effective-character change retains the existing fence on all fresh operations, including reads and waits; ordinary method corrections and transient VAD updates do not automatically trigger that fence.

The October 8 evaluation adds explicit invocation coverage, coherent trait/profile/current-self meaning, all-fragment own voice, trait/state separation, action versus coordination versus learning channels, neutralization and ablation controls, harmful transfer, pressure resistance, correction, adaptive convergence and whole-system cost. No historical pass covers these revised claims merely because the identifier is retained. Historical comparisons, failures, original criteria, minimum counts and denominators remain unchanged. New research-proposed numerical thresholds are optional campaign design choices to freeze before execution, not inherited results or universal requirements.

The same revision makes reciprocal development and a compact collection of cue-connected prompt fragments explicit. Character shapes work and human/peer communication; actual consequences can support changed authored prompt meaning, later changed choices and, when permitted, a current-character revision under the existing ordering and fresh-decision rules. New comparisons separate the same starting character with different histories from different characters with matched experience, and authored query cues from no-cue or exact-reference navigation. These clarify current B02–B04, B07, B09–B10 and B12; they report no observed maturation, learning benefit or universal aging law.

The expanded boundedness cases count cumulative traversal and the whole declared managed fragment/evidence footprint, including archives, versions, indexes, provenance, qualifications, warnings, caches and peak rewrite copies. External storage accounting limits, permitted raw-source expiry versus revocation, preserved rare exceptions and unresolved obligations, restored retired guidance and cue injection now have explicit disturbances under existing mechanical identifiers. A truthful capacity block can satisfy a boundary while failing delivery; silent loss of required state cannot. No new identifier, universal store size or passing result follows, and the three-seed, three-task/three-repetition transfer and three-fresh-environment house minima retain their exact existing scopes.

### Prompt-part clarification and the limit of memory benchmarks

Earlier file-first prose sometimes described fragments primarily as retained memory or treated recall as the defining success. The October 8 clarification replaces that framing with persona-defining prompt context and its evolution. Supporting factual histories, receipts and evidence remain separately governed records, available when a claim requires them; their preservation or retrieval alone does not instantiate the persona. Current evaluation must inspect how selected prompt meaning changes characteristic judgment and behavior under comparable factual information, not merely whether an old fact can be reproduced.

Historical memory, recall and retrieval benchmarks remain limited adjacent evidence for discovery, access, storage or experience-conditioned behavior in their exact tested setting. Their results, failures and original criteria are unchanged. They do not establish this target's ontology, selected-context characteristic influence or meaningful prompt evolution. Historical character or transfer findings likewise keep their precise intervention and scope; a new prompt-part comparison cannot inherit their pass. Legacy MEM and FR identifiers are retained traceability, not a requirement to define fragments as memory.

### Seed bootstrap and bounded supporting-resource context

The October 8 refinement also makes the initial sequence explicit: supplied trait seed and separate modeled state, initialLLM authorship of candidate own-voice persona fragments, coherent accepted handover, continuing primaryLLM self-harmonization, and later evidence-shaped evolution. InitialLLM names a bounded role that may share the continuing model configuration; it is not another standing writer. Seed fidelity, initial phrasing, coherent acceptance, actual observation and useful later change are distinct claims. Whole-bundle order, redundancy, conditional scope and correction across calls receive separate proposed comparisons; exact inclusion alone does not establish useful composition. Harmonization preserves meaningful tensions and qualified disagreement; it is not forced consensus or proof of an experienced history. Historical runs retain the seed, prompt and initialization procedure they actually used. They receive no retrospective bootstrap or harmonization pass.

Supporting histories/evidence, tool definitions, skill instructions, helpers/scripts and task data remain separately organized resources, not new kinds of persona-defining fragment. The current refinements test primary-requested bounded lexical discovery or exact known-resource access, explicit next-context choice and actual provider-bound exposure. Loading a catalog of hundreds of names/descriptions into the model and filtering it afterward is not the new target. Exact known-current resources can take a fast path without compulsory rediscovery. Cache/freshness, changed revisions, withdrawal, revocation, eviction, cumulative exposure and hidden schema expansion require current evidence.

The proposed comparisons retain M01–M26, B01–B12 and X01–X06, matched factual-information and characteristic-influence controls, all existing minima and complete failure/intervention denominators. They separately assess discovery quality, critical-capability misses, actual prompt exposure and adequate delivered work against total per-call and episode cost. The full-catalog condition is a labeled experimental comparator, not current production behavior; external deferred-loading results motivate this comparison but cannot supply its outcome. No historical selector, file, tool, cost or live-pilot result establishes these expanded claims. No runtime code, experiment, paid model call, publication or deployment approval is produced by this documentation refinement.

The later October 8 clarification makes each accepted response's maintenance coverage unambiguous for both persona-defining fragments and supporting assets, alongside work and the bounded next-context selection affordance. That historical clarification allowed omission to preserve a still-valid selection. The 10 October correction replaces valid-response omission with explicit next-context judgment; it still requires neither a fixed preserve field nor a next call. Stored-state recovery after failure remains distinct from authored choice. Proposed creation, storage, revision, organization or permitted deletion identifies intended files/paths and useful future discovery cues; explicit shared no-change is valid when it clearly covers both categories. Proposed bytes or a pathname are not an actual stored revision, and authored histories cannot invent host observations or receipts. Restricted selection repair gains no semantic asset-writing authority. Missing coverage blocks fresh substantive dispatch under the existing invalid-response boundary. These are additional current cases under the existing IDs, not evidence that old runs supplied or passed this expanded response contract; their actual outcomes below remain unchanged.
