## Change of criterion

| Earlier target or evidence | What it can still establish | What it cannot establish now |
|---|---|---|
| Accepted character-fragment edit | An attributed prompt revision under the old rules. | A numerical parameter revision. |
| Better output using retained reflection | Useful context in that observed situation. | Benefit caused by changed character parameters. |
| New model-package hash | A different complete package. | Changed numbers when only metadata changed. |
| Parameter edit on explicit instruction | Editing and activation mechanics within the observed exercise. | Spontaneous useful development from ordinary experience. |
| Completed hypothetical assessment | Actual comparison outputs under its stated inputs. | Later live benefit, generalization or physical action. |
| New editable/native artifact | Actual artifact production and any separately observed checks. | Completion or correctness of every part of the original request. |
| A new documentation release | A revised design and reading path. | Implemented mechanisms or a new live success. |

## Editable-model reports

The [9 November initial report](https://github.com/ai-personas/ai-personas-runtime/blob/1aabb15c56b8a1f3c0403bdb28feeedb57c5bd7e/docs/live-validation-2026-11-09.md) describes a main run with 143 accepted decisions and no post-initialization parameter edits or comparative assessments. Its separate explicitly instructed mechanics exercise reports a bias edit, assessment, activation and rollback. It also identifies confounds and incomplete deliverable scope.

The [manufacturing report](https://github.com/ai-personas/ai-personas-runtime/blob/1aabb15c56b8a1f3c0403bdb28feeedb57c5bd7e/docs/manufacturing-validation-2026-11-09.md) retains its frozen 10 November 11:39:35 UTC observation that all five main-run models were still at epoch 1 without organic revisions. Iterative runtime upgrades and incomplete engineering outcomes remain part of the report’s conditions.

These are repository-reported observations. This publication did not access their private execution databases, reproduce their runs or inspect the user’s current installation. The source audit is not a live validation campaign.

## Reporting later evidence

New observations should identify the exact numerical packages, reasoning configuration, resources, tasks, ordinary versus assisted conditions, evaluator revision and retained artifacts. Preserve failed attempts, null findings, regressions and unknown costs. A changed success criterion needs new relevant evidence, not a revised label on an old outcome.
