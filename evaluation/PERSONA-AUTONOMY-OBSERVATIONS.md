# Observing persona-owned work

[Evaluation guide](README.md) · [Persona-owned process](../design/PERSONA-OWNED-PROCESS.md) · [Memory and context](../design/02-memory-and-learning.md) · [Capabilities and workspaces](../design/04-capabilities-and-action.md)

**Status: evaluation protocol and proposed design clarification, not reported results or a runtime workflow.** These observations do not add a requirement family, assign professions, require tool counts, force reflection, or make an evaluator's preferences binding on a participant.

## 1. Separate missing affordances from choices and outcomes

For a minimally specified creation request, preserve the exact dispatched brief, amendments, starting identities, model configuration, capability bindings, resource ceilings, execution profile and permitted context. Freeze an external rubric before examining results. Do not repair one cohort with hints, assigned professions, added funding or operator resumes and then compare it to an untouched cohort as though only one mechanism changed.

Audit five distinct possibilities: the operation did not exist; it existed but was not visible or understandable; the participant received the affordance and chose not to use it; the action was attempted but failed; the action succeeded mechanically but did not improve the result. A configured search binding is not a search attempt. Tool discovery, installation, representative trial, project use and result assessment are separate observations, not compulsory stages.

Use paired conditions to investigate whether context volume and presentation affect choices: unchanged authorized semantic content, different lossless representation; or a separately specified optional-descriptor presentation with exact visibility records. Never remove current obligations, unread inputs, source ancestry, failures, uncertain actions or selected material merely to produce a more favorable treatment. Record decision-context receipt separately from the inference that the persona noticed an affordance. A model deciding not to search after a readable affordance is available is evidence of that choice, not proof that searching would have helped.

A compact decision view can expose the exact need, current accepted commitments, the participant's own assumptions and unresolved observations, resources, execution facts and routes to permitted capabilities and contributions. It must not infer a hidden domain workflow from the brief. The participant discovers domain unknowns and chooses methods; the system reports truthful operational facts. A general opportunity to inspect evidence is not a search-first instruction.

## 2. Minimal request, useful result, and advisory challenge

For the illustrative input `design 4 bedroom house`, four bedrooms and a coherent house concept arise reasonably from the words. A layout that cannot connect occupied spaces to circulation without unexplained obstacles is open to substantive challenge even though the brief is short. This does not establish a site, climate, jurisdiction, verified dimensions, budget, structural design or construction approval.

The participants may author assumptions about site, size, orientation, occupancy, fidelity and output format, explain which are reversible, and develop a useful conditional result. Peers may challenge those assumptions or point to underdevelopment without forcing a human questionnaire. An assumed site is not a surveyed site, and peer agreement does not make it one.

Native CAD, a named application, a particular file extension, simulations, structural calculations or a fixed production sequence do not follow automatically from those four words. They may become explicit participant commitments, a known product expectation, or criteria of an external evaluation. Keep those categories separate. An evaluator may score native editability and deeper development without falsely reporting that a participant breached an agreement it never accepted.

Editable source can be useful without being native CAD; a native project can be empty or poorly developed. A diagram may be a useful concept or an inadequate final response depending on its claims and the need. Evaluate actual edit operations, dimensions, room relationships, circulation, consistency of representations, limitations, and the clarity of what has and has not been developed. File count, a successful generator, an extension or a content digest is not substantive adequacy.

A reviewer may publish an attributable finding such as “this circulation depends on crossing a private room” or “the declared editable source cannot reproduce this output,” naming the exact candidate and relevant evidence. The owner can investigate, disagree, revise, narrow the claim, seek input or retain the alternative. A judgment is advisory unless an explicit accepted agreement makes that particular review obligation binding. The runtime preserves the agreement and versioned evidence; it does not convert every negative review into a veto or every positive review into final adoption. A persona can create, investigate and assess without being assigned a creator, researcher or reviewer profession.

## 3. Context admission: mechanism tests before paid behavior

A repaired adapter must distinguish original selected data, the actual wire representation, conservative reservation, actual provider-reported usage, and the disposition of a returned decision. Previous measured usage is evidence about that previous call, not an exact size for a later blocked request. The absence of remote context overflow does not establish that a local bound was exact or unnecessary.

For a lossless representation, verify exact reconstruction and digest preservation across selected records, current obligations, unread inputs and their acknowledgement state, character context, privacy ancestry, failures, uncertain actions and media metadata. Verify that image bytes are still read, checked and sent through the existing exact-byte path. Test repeated identifiers with different versions or contents, duplicate occurrences, authored reference-like text, Unicode, empty and null values, integer limits, malformed references, deep contexts and optimizer work limits. Metadata about factoring must not become a source receipt, a learned lesson or a claim of understanding.

Keep all schema and instruction overhead in the estimate, including decoding instructions. Test that a unique oversized core still blocks; configuration, authentication and unsupported-media errors remain distinct; failures preserve uncertain exposure; selected-model identity and output reservation do not change. Test provider-reported usage above a declared bound as a violated sizing guarantee, not an opportunity to silently adopt an underfunded result. Repeated new input, restart and altered cursors must not refresh spent maintenance attempts.

Replay a preserved blocked request on a read-only or disposable copy of its evidence. Verify request and artifact digests, exact adapter configuration, transport, schema and model identity before comparing unchanged conservative admission with and without the representation change. Keep ordinary resources and maintenance state fixed; disable remote dispatch and application of decisions. Record component sizes, admission result, remaining headroom and whether all selected content reconstructs. Compare with the historical implementation as well as the candidate's unfactored path. A synthetic reconstruction is not the frozen replay. Missing fixtures are a missing test, not a passing one.

Only after those gates should a separately funded fresh behavior comparison examine attribution, tool exploration, voluntary consultation, selected lessons and result quality. An encoding can be lossless yet difficult for the model to interpret. Improved admission is not demonstrated persona improvement.

## 4. Concurrent workspace and publication regressions

The collision regression should create two accepted participants in the same work, run generators concurrently with the same relative filename and omitted execution directories, and verify distinct recorded draft directories and intact participant-specific contents. Each generator should produce a second file identifying its scheme so that publication tests can detect cross-scheme notes. Repeat with different work identities and with restart to test stable, work-scoped allocation. Creating an invalid or unauthorized request must not allocate host directories before its authority check.

A second condition deliberately chooses the same shared path. Verify that this remains possible: a default directory is not confinement. Supply a selected expected digest, let the other writer alter the path before capture, and verify a visible conflict rather than silent substitution. With no expected digest, the receipt must identify captured bytes without claiming they were previously inspected. Use a controlled read/write race to verify that the hash is computed over the exact captured bytes, not a pre-read of a subsequently reopened pathname. A changed source or expected version must not corrupt an already published immutable artifact.

Bundle tests should select an exact manifest of immutable members. Changing a selected member produces an exact-version conflict. A bundle whose individually valid notes describe the wrong scheme remains a semantic counterexample: the runtime must not claim consistency from digest validity. Detecting the mismatch, deciding whether it matters, revising, merging, discarding or adopting remain participant judgments. Publication permission is not inferred from path discoverability or a directory label.

## 5. Character effects without decorative personality

Use multiple independent starting-profile seeds and repeated runs under fixed model, funding, tool bindings, brief and assessment conditions. With mixed models, block analyses by model and rotate profile assignments within model so that a trait is not confounded with one model. Keep identity labels and presentation order from revealing treatment to outcome assessors.

Compare a full-character condition with a disclosed experimental character-context ablation or matched neutral-description condition. Keep substantive work state and available resources fixed, and report actual token and call consumption. The ablation is an evaluation intervention, not production personality routing. Also use within-seed profile permutations to distinguish effects of a particular name or model allocation from the supplied character context. Do not assign operations by trait thresholds.

Predeclare observed measures: actual search queries and source breadth, attempted and completed unfamiliar-tool trials, peer consultations and responses, changes made after evidence, evidence-checking actions, alternatives retained, and stopping rationale tied to actual remaining obligations and resources. Assess result quality blind. Self-descriptions, themed filenames and personality-themed drawings do not establish a behavioral trait effect.

Report run-level variability and uncertainty, not just two visually different arrangements. Evidence against the proposed mechanism includes no reproducible behavioral difference across adequately powered repeated seeds, effects disappearing after model/funding controls, only stylistic or self-reported differences, or identical action patterns when character context is withheld. A receipt proving character was supplied establishes delivery of an input, not causation. A single null result need not disprove every possible character mechanism, but it limits the supported claim under the tested conditions.

## 6. From a correction to a selected, useful lesson

The smallest useful opportunity is an attributable mistake or disagreement, its relevant source versions, a correction or unresolved outcome, and a permitted route to inspect them. The participant chooses whether to retain a lesson. A useful lesson states what might transfer, where it applies, what remains uncertain and a counterexample or condition that would defeat it. The runtime does not write the lesson, require an experience review or alter traits automatically.

For a later task, separately verify: discovery of the exact authorized candidate; participant selection of its exact revision; inclusion in the admitted representation and evidence of actual provider receipt; an attributable decision or action applying the content; and an independently assessed outcome. An index preview is not full selection, admission is not remote receipt, and an apparently related action is not automatically caused by the lesson.

Use matched experienced-versus-fresh personas with model, funding, tools, brief and non-experience profile controlled. Add an experienced-with-lesson versus experienced-with-that-lesson-withheld comparison to isolate that lesson from other history. Use related but nonidentical tasks to reduce answer leakage. Report both opportunity costs and harmful transfer. Do not credit extra funding, copied successful outputs, stronger tools or a changed evaluator as learning.

Exercise revocation before discovery, between selection and admission, and before a late result is adopted. Test snippets, derivatives and summaries as well as full fragments. Retain minimal accountability where appropriate, but do not replay revoked content or treat remembered substance as newly permissionless. A source restriction cannot be bypassed by changing a lesson's wording. Claims about deletion of copies already exported outside the system must state their limits.

## 7. Peer waits, visible ownership and voluntary handoff

Give permitted peers a compact current view of accepted owners, open obligations, waiting or blocked state, the disclosed dependency, offered handoffs and available contributions. Preserve distinctions between voluntary yield, a dependency wait, resource exhaustion and context-admission failure. Deliver a durable state-change notice only to an authorized audience and avoid repeated idle wakeups. Private blocked content stays private; the owner or operator can receive a more detailed diagnosis without widening peer permissions.

A peer may choose an independent contribution, answer a shared question, challenge an assumption, offer assistance, accept a proposed handoff or decline. Yielding does not transfer another participant's obligation. A runtime block does not appoint a replacement or authorize the system to fabricate acceptance. Handoff records should identify the exact obligation, scope, selected artifacts, known unknowns and remaining resources; transfer requires explicit acceptance and relevant authority. Until then, preserve the accepted owner or visible ownership gap according to the existing work contract.

Test an owner blocked while a peer voluntarily contributes, a peer declining a handoff, a late acceptance against a changed obligation, and a notification racing with wait registration. Confirm that independent work can continue without treating an unanswered human question as a global stop. A peer's answer or conditional assumption can support bounded concept work, but it cannot silently become human consent or a verified site fact.

## Interpretation and tradeoffs

Method neutrality removes an implied search preference; it also accepts that some personas will choose weak methods. Truthful host execution removes a false containment promise; it exposes real shared-account risks rather than hiding them behind directory names. Lossless factoring preserves evidence but may add cognitive indirection and may not save enough space. Participant drafts reduce accidental collisions but do not settle deliberate shared edits. Advisory review preserves agency but can leave disagreement unresolved. Voluntary reflection permits useful restraint but also missed learning opportunities.

These tradeoffs should be observed and challenged through attributable evidence, not repaired by silently replacing participant judgment with hard-coded roles, mandatory stages, automatic lessons or a hidden domain rubric.
