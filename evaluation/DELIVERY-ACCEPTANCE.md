# Supplemental delivery stress scenarios

[Evaluation method](README.md) · [Core acceptance catalogue](ACCEPTANCE.md) · [Active requirements](../implementation/REQUIREMENTS.md) · [Historical mapping](../implementation/DELIVERY-CONTRACTS.md)

**Revision: 18 September 2026, emergence consolidation. Status of D01–D16: specified, not executed.** These are refinements for testing existing requirements, not another active requirement family or instructions for runtime task selection. Their identifiers retain the earlier concerns; the wording now separates the general failure from any particular circuit or business example. Preserve the scenario revision when comparing results.

## Select task data, not a prescribed solution

An evaluator selects concrete inputs, adopted output scope, permissions, resources, criteria, and disturbances for applicable scenarios. The accepted task may legitimately specify a format, a standard, or a constrained method. That does not prescribe a hidden team roster, tool brand, dialogue, or task-solving choreography. Do not install the scenario as a runtime recipe or automatically inject its example solution into persona context.

Use varied task families and input forms. Keep the core and general orchestration policy unchanged between them; changing an external tool or adding its mechanical adapter is distinct from adding a task classifier. Tools, source material, initial experience, and permitted retrieval must be declared. For end-to-end claims, freeze operational criteria and assess meaningful final artifacts and current evidence, not just refusal behavior or final chat. Missing criteria are an evaluation setup gap, not a candidate pass.

## Interpreting evidence

Concrete engineering and business instantiations are available in the [task illustrations](../examples/5W-INVERTER-AND-CROSS-DOMAIN.md). They are not expected execution traces. Keep useful negative checks as well as successful whole-package runs; rejecting an empty file does not demonstrate producing a good one.

Report exact task and scenario revisions, actual artifacts, check coverage, findings, resource usage, and limitations under the [reporting method](README.md#reporting-a-result). Evaluate [emergent organization](README.md#evaluating-emergent-organization) separately from artifact quality. A hard-coded workflow can yield a good artifact; an unscripted group can fail to deliver. Neither dimension substitutes for the other.
