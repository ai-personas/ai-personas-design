# Continuing-development acceptance

[Design chapter](../design/08-continuing-development.md) · [Acceptance catalogue](ACCEPTANCE.md)

These are required evaluations, not executed results. Publish exact implementation and client revisions, configuration, actual evidence, failures, and limitations in implementation evidence records. A passing mechanism test must not be relabelled as observed behavioral benefit.

## Character-behavior pilot

Use matched trials within a model before making cross-model comparisons. Hold task, environment, tools, resource allowance, and inference settings constant; vary one declared profile dimension at a time. Predeclare three initialization seeds and evaluate three families: creating a technical artifact, investigating conflicting information, and coordinating a shared problem. Repeat the study separately for Luna, Terra, and Sol, recording their actual provider/model configurations rather than treating names as capabilities.

Include name-swap and character-withheld controls. Assess actual attention to uncertainty, useful alternatives, experiments, consultation or challenge, evidence that changes choices, independent outcomes, resource use, and unnecessary repetition. Record adverse as well as favorable effects. Do not require a stereotype in every run or infer influence from narrative descriptions of traits.

A pinned model revision and reproducible sampling may be unavailable. Record that limitation and describe the resulting study as observational rather than strictly causally isolated. Compare within-model effects before attributing differences between models to character.

## Learning-transfer pilot

Use three related follow-up tasks and three matched repetitions with three arms: an experienced persona with the relevant lesson available; a matched experienced persona with that lesson withheld; and a fresh persona with matched starting dispositions and model configuration. The retained-versus-withheld contrast is primary. The fresh-persona contrast additionally reflects broader experience.

Use separate equivalent environments to prevent leakage through files, summaries, peer messages, caches, or copied profile prose. Preserve ordinary unrestricted tool execution within each environment. Include a misleading lesson, a superseded lesson, and a task where the old lesson should be rejected.

For each trial, verify the originating experience, authored lesson and limitations, retrieval, deliberate selection, exact dispatch inclusion, subsequent actions, and independent outcome assessment against real runtime records. A manually populated comparison ledger is not a substitute for those records. Outcome assessors must not receive arm-specific hints or a changed rubric.

## Minimal-brief technical-artifact evaluation

Run three independent fresh trials with exactly **design 4 bedroom house**. Preserve the prior failed run unchanged as the baseline. Do not supply professions, software choices, a deliverable checklist, or house-specific corrective hints. Keep permission to adopt explicit concept assumptions, and keep the evaluation rubric outside persona context.

The evaluator examines native editable CAD/BIM or equivalent modeling source; successful reopening and a meaningful edit followed by regenerated outputs; four usable bedrooms with access; coherent doors, windows, circulation, and inter-storey connections; dimensions and schedules consistently derived from geometry; evidence-backed tool investigation and representative trials; substantive peer findings with repair or explicit unresolved disposition; and investigation of relevant structural, services, environmental, and permit questions with stated limits. Retained experience should reflect useful trials or corrections, not a required fragment count.

A concept remains labelled as a concept. Site uncertainty does not excuse avoidable internal geometry errors. CAM or simulation artifacts are required only when a demonstrated need justifies them. Publish failures and successes. Operator-assisted repair is a separate result, not success on the original minimal brief.

## Release decision

Publish a matched revision set and distinguish what was implemented, what deterministic tests actually ran, what live choices were observed, and what outcome improvements were established. List unavailable tests and unresolved defects explicitly. Do not mark this feature complete on profile initialization, prompt inclusion, successful compilation, or extra artifacts alone.
