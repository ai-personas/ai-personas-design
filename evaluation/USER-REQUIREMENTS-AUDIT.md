## Conclusion

This audit assesses all 47 active requirements against the named blueprint. Three-persona examples span technical work, everyday social exchange, voluntary task-free interaction and a later persona-originated request. A separate group trajectory distinguishes first adequate output, null work, rejected harm and a locally useful presentation revision over time. These are hypothetical operation and quality accounts, not runtime, installation, engineering or savings evidence; research/delivery and historical execution assignments remain separately scoped.

The design distinguishes externally enabled opportunities from persona-chosen engagement, topic, partner and need. A social exchange can end without an artifact or durable revision. Accountable continuing duties survive intermediate delivery; dormancy follows their proper disposition. Social continuity and useful later work require separate attributable evidence. The new finite scenes clarify the proposed mechanism without demonstrating a society’s emergence, reliable long-running behavior or lifetime maturation.

**Adequate design coverage and hypothetical illustration do not establish implemented reliability, global uniqueness, human-like lifetime maturation or better later performance.** Those strong outcomes remain the goals, and their empirical status remains open. Finite examples are not a defect merely because they do not exhaust every possible task or life stage.

## Research and design assessment, separate from user intent

The design's selective mutation, authority checks, source eligibility, complete-response validation, controlled character changes and evaluation controls are engineering responses. They must not be appended to the human-only requirement list merely because they seem useful. Equally, the user's strong goals of unique lifetime development and better later performance must not be replaced with the narrower statement that fragments may change without benefit.

The [research record](../sources/PERSONA-FILES-RESEARCH.md#what-kind-of-evidence-is-this) supports investigating components and explicitly limits transfer. This audit inspected that record as design evidence; it did not independently rerun the literature search or verify every cited experiment. It therefore makes no new literature-derived performance claim.

Five important assessment distinctions should survive further editing:

1. **Appropriate convergence is compatible with uniqueness.** Correct arithmetic, permissions and safety boundaries need not differ. Current technical and social examples illustrate meaningful choices where several responsible approaches remain possible: tool versus peer assistance, ordering of optional checks, amount and form of communication, stopping exploration, or adapting an existing coordination habit. Different prose alone is weaker evidence; these bounded choices still need repeated-run comparison before they establish a characteristic effect.
2. **Method refinement is useful but does not exhaust maturation.** Continuing trait-consistent judgment, reciprocal social adaptation, relationships, resource organization, communication and coordination belong to the requested whole-persona outcome. No forced OCEAN drift or invented emotional experience is required to show them.
3. **Mechanism success and useful success are different.** A stored fragment, accepted selection, faithful supply and bounded stop can each be correct while the task fails or costs more. Conversely, a correct fresh answer can make retained context unnecessary. Both outcomes belong in the evidence.
4. **Continued pursuit, current sufficiency and later improvement are different.** A local limit cannot discharge unfinished accepted work; a satisfied current goal need not become endless optional refinement. Individual or collective judgments retain their actual decision basis and dissent. A continuing improvement duty survives intermediate delivery, while a new improvement proposal needs its applicable acceptance and resources. Neither more calls nor group agreement proves better work.

5. **Opportunity, social continuity and useful transfer are different.** External admission supplies an occasion and resources, not the persona’s topic, partner or self-chosen need. Voluntary conversation can meet its stated social purpose without an artifact. Later card adequacy, same-task P2 feedback and broader societal maturation require their own attributable evidence; elapsed age and consensus supply none automatically.

## Priority next work and evidence

1. **Keep assessment currency tied to exact evidence.** Reassess later changes rather than carrying this snapshot’s dispositions forward automatically. Further design changes should respond to a concrete inconsistency, missing requested behavior or research finding; finite illustrations alone are not a new gap.
