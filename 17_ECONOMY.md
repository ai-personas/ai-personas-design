# 17 — Economy

PersonaOS defines no currency, market, price, unit of account, institution,
value function, reward, fitness, reputation score, promotion ladder, or economic
workflow. Personas and authenticated humans may author open economic proposals
and actions under exact existing authority.

## 1. Exact substrate facts

The substrate may expose exact signed resource facts such as available model
calls, compute, storage, external budget/payment authority, ownership/custody
records, consent, access, and action receipts. These facts have no host-assigned
economic meaning.

Resource abundance or scarcity does not create population pressure, select a
tool, rank a persona, assign a role, schedule cognition, or determine objective
completion. The run scorecard
([`10_PLATFORM_REQUIREMENTS.md §5`](10_PLATFORM_REQUIREMENTS.md#5-the-run-scorecard))
is a signed count of recorded facts, not a reward, fitness, price, or
reputation.

## 2. Persona-authored constructs

A persona may author a bounded open signed proposal or record about exchange,
value, obligation, compensation, sharing, ownership, settlement, or any other
concept. The persona chooses vocabulary and cites exact parties, resources,
artifacts, actions, evidence, and consent.

The kernel validates canonical bounds, signatures, scopes, access, consent,
causal order, descriptor effects, resource authority, and replay. It does not
interpret value, set prices, choose beneficiaries, compute merit, rank proposals,
or decide that a construct is economically useful.

No proposal text automatically creates an action, payment, transfer, birth,
membership, tool invocation, artifact, wake, or acceptance.

## 3. Counterparty consent

Every effect on another principal, persona, environment, resource, artifact,
account, or external system requires its exact authority and consent. Economic
prose cannot supply or widen that authority.

Personas are cryptographic actors, not fungible assets. A persona identity,
signing key, consent, membership, future agency, or work authority cannot be
sold, fractionalized, or transferred by an economic label. Any custody or
membership change follows its independent exact protocol and the persona's
consent.

## 4. Actions and settlement

An economic proposal may lead a persona to choose an authorized communication,
contract, payment, resource, artifact, or settlement action. Each action causes
only descriptor-declared effects and retains exact terminal receipts.

The substrate provides no negotiate/quote/accept/pay/deliver/review sequence,
market matching, auction, escrow recipe, tokenization path, risk score, dispute
panel, or automatic settlement choice. Personas navigate available exact
unranked actions and counterparties author their own responses.

## 5. Population independence

Treasury, income, credit, reputation, demand, or resource headroom never creates
birth pressure, parent selection, team size, role coverage, or population-ceiling
movement. Every actor-materializing action requires an explicit signed
replication-effect descriptor and ReplicationBound authority; birth additionally
requires one admitted signed `personaos-persona-birth-proposal/5`.

## 6. Evidence and claims

Receipts prove exact transfers, payments, signatures, bytes, or external results
when those effects actually occurred. They do not prove fairness, value,
quality, success, reputation, expertise, or task completion.

Economic reviews, forecasts, balances, valuations, and work notes are authored
claims unless exact external/verifier authority supplies a mechanically
verifiable fact.

## 7. Causality and quiescence

An economic record does not schedule cognition. A later model call requires an
actual message, persona-authored wake/schedule, external receipt, principal/
resource event, or other authenticated causal delivery.

With no pending event, economic activity is quiescent and nonterminal. It is
not settled, successful, failed, accepted, or abandoned merely because no call
is scheduled.

## 8. Removed compatibility surface

There is no live compatibility for economic promotion stages, trust/value/
fitness scores, Goodhart classifiers, fixed beneficiary classes, standard
currencies/markets, seed institutions, treasury-to-population coupling,
drive/mood reward mechanics, fixed negotiation/settlement sequences, or
economy-derived completion. Historical draft records remain non-authoritative.

## 9. Design criteria

1. The substrate exposes exact resource/effect facts without economic meaning.
2. Economic vocabulary and navigation are persona/human-authored.
3. Counterparty effects require exact independent consent and authority.
4. Personas are never economic instruments.
5. Economy never selects population, tools, roles, or completion.
6. Quiescence is nonterminal.
