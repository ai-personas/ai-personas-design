# Consolidated user requirements

This file consolidates the user's requirements from the AI Personas design conversation through 10 October 2026. It is a single readable account of the requested outcome, architecture, examples, research, and delivery. Repeated requests are combined, and later clarifications control the meaning of earlier wording.

Only human-user messages supply the requirements in this file. Rephrase and consolidate those messages without adding assistant interpretations, design decisions, evidence safeguards, or implementation rules as user requirements. Preserve the user's intended outcomes rather than weakening them to fit the existing design. Later user clarifications govern earlier wording.

## Product requirements

### Purpose and design scope

- **UR-001 Design before implementation.** Keep this repository design-only. Produce and refine an implementation-ready blueprint describing how the personas should operate. Do not add application code as part of this design work.
- **UR-002 One coherent design.** Review all design documents, including published GitHub documents and unpublished work, against the user's requirements. Update, revise, refactor, add, or delete material as needed so the whole design coherently describes AI Personas and how they should work. Produce a solid, robust, reliable design that can be implemented.
- **UR-003 General-purpose, human-like personas.** Personas should have human-like characteristics and behavior, respond to whatever a user needs, and work on any kind of task. A user or requester may be a human or another persona; a need can originate from either. Their characteristics should shape tasks, tool use, communication, coordination, collaboration, self-directed activity, and socializing with humans and other personas. They should develop across these activities, socialize better, and perform better for the user.

### Persona identity and characteristic fragments

- **UR-004 Start with a persona profile.** Define an initial persona profile using traits such as OCEAN and VAD. Research and add other persona traits if needed. These traits must be expressed throughout the persona's characteristic fragments in its own voice.
- **UR-005 The initial LLM call authors the initial fragments.** Use the starting profile to produce the persona's initial characteristic fragments. These prompt parts must express the persona's own voice and character, providing the starting point for its subsequent development. Continuing fragment creation, revision, and self-harmonization must preserve that persona-specific characteristic meaning.
- **UR-006 Fragments embody character.** Every characteristic fragment must reflect its owner's unique traits and voice. These persona-specific prompt parts must carry the persona's characteristic and behavioral meaning, rather than serve as generic instructions with a persona label attached. The selected fragments form the character-bearing part of the context supplied to the LLM.
- **UR-007 The LLM and selected context constitute the operating persona.** The LLM provides reasoning, while the selected characteristic fragments and relevant supporting context shape this persona's responses and decisions. Character must influence the way it acts, rather than exist only in a separate profile or biography.
- **UR-008 Unique character drives behavior throughout development.** Each persona's own characteristics must shape its actions, tools and skills, responses, human and peer communication, coordination, collaboration, and self-directed activity, from initialization through its own aging and maturation. Behavior should influence which actions it chooses and how much and in what way it communicates: some personas may communicate more, others less, while some favor tools and others collaboration. Preserve each persona's uniqueness as it develops.
- **UR-009 Fragments are not memories or plain knowledge.** Use “fragment” to mean a characteristic prompt part, not an episodic memory, a general-purpose fact store, or plain domain knowledge. The persona is formed through these character-bearing prompt parts; it is not defined by a separate memory mechanism. Supporting histories and records can still be retained as assets.
- **UR-010 Do not introduce a separate learning mechanism.** Explain maturation through the ordinary LLM interactions that create, revise, organize, select, and delete fragments and assets. The user calls this development of fragments and assets, rather than a separate learning or memory mechanism.

### Supporting assets and historical context

- **UR-011 Keep assets identifiable separately from characteristic fragments.** Plain domain knowledge, data, tools, skills, scripts, code, historical messages, records of actions and tool interactions, and other useful resources are assets or supporting context. They remain distinguishable from characteristic fragments even when the persona or LLM creates them. Plain assets need not be rewritten in the persona's voice. Authorship alone does not turn an asset into persona character.
- **UR-012 Retain useful resources for later work.** Provide for storing and organizing the useful tools, skills, knowledge, data, scripts, code, history, and other assets that the persona acquires or produces. Later work must be able to find relevant assets without reconstructing everything from the beginning.
- **UR-013 Give retained material an intended location and useful discovery cues.** When an LLM interaction proposes new or revised fragments or assets, it must also address where that material belongs and how it can be found again, including the intended file or path and relevant search or regex cues. Storage and discovery must be part of the design, rather than an unspecified step after generation.
- **UR-014 Compose context from the relevant kinds of material.** An LLM request may combine characteristic fragments with relevant tools, skills, domain knowledge, data, and historical interaction or action records. Preserve the distinction between the character-bearing prompt parts and the supporting material while assembling the context needed for the current decision.

### The ordinary LLM interaction

- **UR-015 Integrate work and self-maintenance in every interaction.** Every persona LLM request and response must include the current work, fragment-and-asset maintenance requests and responses, and what is needed for the next context. This applies to tasks, communication, collaboration, coordination, tool use, and any other persona action.
- **UR-016 Support the full range of maintenance.** The same interaction must support new fragments, updates to existing fragments, reorganization, and deletion, together with maintenance of tools, skills, knowledge, data, history, and other assets. These changes shape and improve the persona and its characteristic behavior.
- **UR-018 Let the current interaction guide the next one.** The persona's current LLM interaction must say which characteristic fragments, tools, skills, data, and other context are needed next and how to find them. Select the relevant material from the files for the next LLM request, and continue this process through the work.

### File based discovery and context efficiency

- **UR-019 Use ordinary files and searchable links.** Prefer simple file organization and search over unnecessary architectural complexity. Store and organize prompt fragments in files, including JSON where useful, with filesystem search, grep, and regular-expression cues for discovery. Fragments may carry cues that lead to other relevant fragments or assets, forming a navigable network without requiring the whole collection in context.
- **UR-020 Select resources through the same selective discovery process.** Discovery must cover tools, skills, data, knowledge, history, and other assets as well as characteristic fragments. A fragment or current LLM result may indicate which resources to locate and which search cues to use.
- **UR-021 Select before loading the next model context.** Do not send a persona's hundreds of tools, its full catalogue, or its whole fragment and asset collection in the LLM request for the model to filter. Use the current request's need and selection instructions to retrieve only the relevant information for a subsequent request.
- **UR-022 Minimize unnecessary context and token cost.** Make each LLM request context-efficient by selecting the needed information rather than loading unnecessary material. Tokens cost money, so the design must minimize their cost and research its effectiveness and cost savings.
- **UR-023 Keep both the collection and the prompt compact.** Prevent fragments from growing indefinitely or becoming an inconsistent, bloated collection. Revise, combine, reorganize, and keep needed characteristic material so that the persona can navigate its resources, select the right fragments at the right time, and form compact, coherent context.
- **UR-024 Keep organization useful as the persona develops.** Resource organization and discovery cues must evolve along with the fragments and assets. The persona must be able to find useful earlier material after revision or reorganization, rather than merely accumulate files that are rarely used.

### Development and later usefulness

- **UR-025 Development arises through interaction.** Working on tasks, interacting with the environment, communicating and socializing with humans and other personas, and self-directed interactions must shape the persona's subsequent characteristic fragments and assets. Explain how these ordinary interactions reshape the persona without a separate learning process.
- **UR-026 Maturation affects the whole persona.** Personas should develop like humans over their lifetime. Their characteristics, behavior, affect, communication, coordination, collaboration, and ways of working should mature, with that development reflected throughout their work and actions. Their fragments and assets should improve and become better organized, allowing the right prompts and resources to be used at the right time.
- **UR-027 Later stages should perform better.** A matured persona should be better than it was at an earlier age. Its improved fragments, assets, characteristic behavior, and ways of prompting and working should help it perform subsequent work better. Show this benefit across the persona's behavior and actions.
- **UR-028 Examine useful transfer without assuming complete domain transfer.** Earlier work should help later work through relevant fragments, skills, tools, assets, methods, simulations, and developed characteristic behavior. The user does not expect every part of the earlier work, or its entire domain solution, to transfer. Work through the new task on its own and examine which parts of prior development help.

- **UR-048 Examine a developing society of personas, including without assigned tasks.** Use hypothetical examples to examine how personas continue communicating, collaborating, and maturing over longer periods of operation, including when no task has been assigned. Their own characteristics and behavior should shape these interactions and their development over time. Examine how a society-like pattern emerges through their ongoing interactions, both outside assigned tasks and when groups work together on a task or need, and how this maturation helps them perform later work better.

### Sustained work and reasons to continue or stop

- **UR-044 Examine sustained purposeful work and improvement.** Examine what happens when a persona has ample, or hypothetically unlimited, time to keep working on a user's task. Investigate whether continued work produces quality artifacts that meet the user's needs, improves the task or its output, improves and matures the persona itself, and involves useful interaction with other personas. Include groups of personas continuing to collaborate and improve their shared task, work, or response to a user's need over time. Explain how this continued work and improvement should happen.
- **UR-045 Justify continuation and stopping.** Review whether personas keep working usefully or stop arbitrarily with potentially low-quality work. Explain when and why a persona should continue working or stop, and how those choices are justified in relation to the user's task and the opportunity for further improvement.

- **UR-047 Let reflection, dialogue, and character shape completion and further improvement.** Explain how a single persona can use self-reflection and self-questioning, and how multiple personas can ask one another questions and clarify the work as they continue it. Their characteristic behaviors should shape individual or collective judgments about what can be improved, when and where to stop, and when to consider the work complete, taking account of the time available. Examine how they can deliver work they judge sufficient for now and return to improve it when more time is available.

### Clarification and progress from sparse requests

- **UR-046 Clarify sparse requests and proceed.** A user may begin with a simple question or a bare description of a task or need. When more clarity is needed, the persona should ask useful clarification questions of the user or relevant stakeholder. The user or task owner may or may not provide answers; the persona should not wait indefinitely for that input. It should be able to seek clarification from other personas, receive input when another persona volunteers it, or form its own reasonable interpretation and proceed with the work. Examine how these social interactions help clarify the task when the original requester is unavailable.

### Worked examples and design verification

- **UR-029 Compare three unique personas over time.** Use three personas with distinct initial profiles and initial LLM-authored fragments. Hypothetical walkthroughs must examine each persona's uniqueness and its own human-like aging and maturation based on its traits, showing its initial state, interactions, subsequent fragments and assets, and characteristic behavior across its later actions and communication.
- **UR-030 Include the house and circuit work cases.** Walk through designing a four-bedroom house and a five-watt DC-to-AC circuit with three personas. Show how their different characteristics shape their approaches, choices, collaboration, and designs, and how this work develops their subsequent characteristics, fragments, and assets.
- **UR-031 Show house-to-skyscraper improvement.** Give a later skyscraper-design task, potentially from another human user, to personas that participated in the house work. Show how their matured characteristics and relevant earlier fragments, methods, simulations, skills, tools, and assets help them perform better on this new task, without requiring complete transfer of the house design.
- **UR-043 Show circuit-to-3D-printer improvement.** Include a later 3D-printer-design walkthrough involving personas that worked on the DC-to-AC circuit. Show how their maturation and relevant prior work help them perform better in designing the 3D printer.
- **UR-032 Walk through how the design should operate.** Provide step-by-step hypothetical walkthroughs of the LLM requests and responses, selected fragments and supporting context, actions, fragment and asset changes, and next-context choices. Show how an implementation of the design should perform and how the personas develop through the work. These examples exercise the design; they do not restrict the kinds of tasks personas should handle. In these examples, examine how personas use tools and skills, manage their assets, and retain relevant data. Where several tools are available, examine whether they choose suitable tools and use them effectively, and how their skills, characteristics, and behavior shape different tool preferences. Include how tools are installed when installation is part of the work, including in the circuit and later 3D-printer examples.
- **UR-033 Examine help, lack of help, and missed benefit.** Investigate what prior development helps, what does not help, and whether something that could help is not being used or is not happening. Examine both domain-related resources and changes in characteristic behavior, affect, judgment, communication, and coordination to find out whether the design is solid.
- **UR-034 Use the examples to find and repair design gaps.** Audit the proposed design against the walkthroughs and all user requirements. Identify what works, what fails, missing behavior, contradictions, and unresolved questions; research them and refine the affected design rather than only appending examples or assurances.
- **UR-035 Deliver a robust, reliable blueprint.** Make the design solid and effective so that personas will work as intended when the design is implemented. Research and repair gaps in pursuit of the user's goal that the persona design work completely and reliably.
