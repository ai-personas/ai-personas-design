The current design is defined by the [persona core](../design/PERSONA-CORE.md), [work and boundaries](../design/WORK-AND-BOUNDARIES.md), and [contracts](../implementation/CONTRACTS.md), under the protected invariants. These source pages explain why the design uses its chosen mechanism and where earlier ideas and reports can be inspected. They do not add a competing specification.

- [Research and rationale](PERSONA-FILES-RESEARCH.md) connects ordinary files, own-voice learning, and primary-model context choice to relevant public precedents, with explicit limits on what those sources establish
- [The provenance manifest](SOURCE-MANIFEST.md) pins the complete prior repository and original supplied-source lineage, including retired manuals, graph/selector figures, numeric-character contracts, and earlier file proposals
- [The historical-results ledger](../evaluation/HISTORICAL-RESULTS.md) preserves original evaluation identifiers, reported observations, and their criteria without granting a current pass

Earlier S1–S5 briefs, the selector vendor-source page, and superseded design addenda now live in Git history. Their useful authority, resource, privacy, evidence, and cooperation requirements are consolidated into the current normative documents. Their old architecture choices are historical, not selectable alternatives in active navigation. Old paths are discoverable from the manifest and complete pinned tree.

The adopted target is not an assertion that the implementation has caught up. External research is precedent, not AI Personas execution evidence. New empirical claims need accessible primary sources, exact versions or dates, the precise supported claim, and limitations. New runtime claims additionally need exact implementation revisions, observations, and the applicable evaluation criteria.
