# Sources: intent, design choices and evidence

[Home](../README.md) · [Source manifest](SOURCE-MANIFEST.md) · [Runtime audit](RUNTIME-AUDIT.md) · [Research](PERSONA-FILES-RESEARCH.md)

Sources serve different purposes. A user requirement defines the target. A design decision proposes how to meet it. Source code shows an implementation path. A report describes observations. A research paper supports only the mechanisms and conditions it actually studies.

![Human intent supplies authority; design choices explain mechanisms; inspected code and actual reports supply bounded evidence; research motivates hypotheses without validating the combined system.](../assets/sources.svg)

*Read the arrows as possible, recorded transitions—not promises that a result will improve.*

There is deliberately no arrow from a paper or a diagram directly to “this system works.” The relationship must be established by observations of this system under its actual configuration.

## The source collection

The [manifest](SOURCE-MANIFEST.md) records exact predecessor revisions, the five original attachment copies, the conversation clarifications integrated here and the limits of their availability. The [runtime audit](RUNTIME-AUDIT.md) separates inspected mechanisms from repository-reported live results and proposed repairs. The [research note](PERSONA-FILES-RESEARCH.md) states a few directly relevant primary-source claims without treating adjacent research as validation.

Original document bytes, rewritten summaries, generated explanations and current design obligations are different artifacts. Integrity statements about one do not transfer to another. Historical private evidence stays subject to its actual availability; public links do not make unavailable logs reproducible.

## Source-use rule

State the claim, exact source/version, what was inspected and what remains unknown. Preserve failures and limitations. Do not insert private session URLs, credentials, local execution databases or raw conversation transcripts into this public repository. The user has authorized publication of the relevant design and requirement synthesis, not unrelated personal content.
