The current design is defined by the [persona core](../design/PERSONA-CORE.md), [work and boundaries](../design/WORK-AND-BOUNDARIES.md), and [contracts](../implementation/CONTRACTS.md), under the protected invariants. The persona is the whole context, LLM reasoning, action, feedback, and self-revision loop. Persona-authored fragments are persona-defining prompt parts; selected fragments form characteristic-bearing context for the LLM reasoning function. These source pages explain why the design uses its chosen mechanism and where earlier ideas and reports can be inspected. They do not add a competing specification.

Earlier S1–S5 briefs, the selector vendor-source page, and superseded design addenda now live in Git history. Their old architecture choices are historical, not selectable alternatives in active navigation. Old paths are discoverable from the manifest and complete pinned tree.

The 8 October revision requires semantic OCEAN coverage and distinct VAD modeled affect without requiring numeric scales or stereotyped action mappings. Every authored reusable fragment embodies the persona's own perspective. Each persona invocation combines work, explicit fragment and supporting-asset maintenance outcomes, and an explicit next-context disposition for fragments and resources in every accepted response under the 10 October correction. A shared justified no-change can cover maintenance; next-context preserve/no optional need must also be explicit. Every-call asset consideration is a requested design hypothesis, not a result proven by progressive-loading literature. The meaning, call coverage, and failure rules live in the normative chapters. The source pages explain the motivation and preserve the earlier, weaker obligations for honest comparison.

The adopted target is not an assertion that the implementation has caught up. Human psychometrics supplies vocabulary; LLM studies support bounded component claims; the complete integration remains untested here. Neither citations nor structured-output compliance provide a 100% guarantee of useful character, grounded maturation, or reliable effects. New empirical claims need accessible primary sources, exact versions or dates, the precise supported claim, and limitations. New runtime claims additionally need exact implementation revisions, observations, and the applicable evaluation criteria.

The bootstrap/resource refinement retains that evidence boundary. It calls for evaluating whole-context composition and whole-episode cost, including unsuccessful discovery, repeated input, provider expansions, caching, initialization, and maintenance. Keeping hundreds of catalog entries out of an LLM request is a chosen observable boundary; finding useful resources and producing adequate work more efficiently remain separate unrun claims. Current mutable documentation was inspected on 8 October 2026, which does not make that date its publication date or an adapter-validation record.

Memory and retention literature provides adjacent evidence about discovery, bounded storage, and provenance; factual remembering does not define the persona or establish its characteristic behavior. The compact-context refinement does not import a validated aging curve, forgetting score, or automatic model training. Managed indexes, archives, versions, metadata, external memory tiers, and peak copies count toward any whole-store claim; arbitrary recipient/provider copies remain outside promised control. User-owned deliverables retain their own authority and retention boundaries. A smaller fragment collection is useful only when it preserves effective characteristic context, its retained guidance remains appropriately qualified, and its losses are honestly represented.
