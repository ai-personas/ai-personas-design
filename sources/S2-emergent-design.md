# S2 — Emergence: let useful organization arise from the work

[Source guide](README.md) · [Provenance](SOURCE-MANIFEST.md) · [Work and cooperation](../design/03-work-and-cooperation.md)

**Role:** design rationale. This brief explains the intended meaning of emergence without code, branch comparisons, or a claim that useful multi-persona behavior has already been demonstrated.

## Six observable ambitions

| Ambition | What should be observable | What would not establish it |
|---|---|---|
| Understanding | Relevant constraints, uncertainties, and omissions are discovered beyond the literal request. | Repeating the request in different words. |
| Improvement | A bounded comparison supports a better outcome or an informative rejection. | Describing an attractive idea as already successful. |
| Priorities | New evidence changes what a persona actually does next. | Rewriting a plan while continuing stale work. |
| Organization | Responsibilities and coordination are negotiated around the need. | Filling predetermined profession slots. |
| Population | A bounded new continuing participant makes an inspectable contribution when useful. | More identities, welcome messages, or duplicated authority. |
| Learning | Retained experience changes later behavior usefully under a fair comparison. | Counting memory entries or personality changes. |

These are goals for behavior, not names of required software components. They allow restraint: the right outcome may use one persona, no tool, no new memory, and no birth.

## Planning and scheduling are different

Personas interpret evidence, choose approaches, negotiate commitments, and reconsider priorities. The supporting system determines whether actions are authorized, whether resources remain, how conflicting writes are handled, and whether a participant is ready to act.

The system may ensure fair access to finite resources without deciding the semantic value of every task. A universal score, fixed role map, or compulsory leader would hide the very judgment the design aims to make attributable.

## Preserve individual and shared views

Each persona has its own agenda. Shared work exposes an unranked opportunity and obligation board. Actual coordination depends on accepted commitments and dependencies, some ordered and some independent.

The earlier source used a shared attention-frontier description. The later consolidated decision is more precise: shared visibility does not replace individual priorities. The current design preserves that resolution, rather than blending incompatible accounts.

Agreements identify actual endorsers and dissent. A collective summary must not invent unanimity. Relationship history may affect whom a persona asks for help, but it does not grant universal expertise or permission.

## Improvement must coexist with finishing

An opportunity starts with an observation and a proposed benefit, not an assumed success. It needs a meaningful comparison, an allowance, adoption authority, a preserved baseline, and a stop or revisit condition.

A failed experiment can reduce uncertainty. A useful partial result can be the right disposition. Optional novelty must not consume the capacity needed for required review and honest closeout.

## What remains unproved

More personas may or may not help a particular task. A new name does not establish an independent line of reasoning. Character descriptions are not validated psychology. A plausible authored story is not a measured cooperation trace.

The [behavioral checks](../evaluation/ACCEPTANCE.md#behavioral-checks) compare relevant persistent state, partner context, memory availability, actual correction, adaptive membership, and no-expansion cases under comparable total resources. They define what evidence would support a narrow claim rather than promising universal emergence.
