# S5 — Stress scenarios: make failures visible before claiming success

[Source guide](README.md) · [Provenance](SOURCE-MANIFEST.md) · [Acceptance catalogue](../evaluation/ACCEPTANCE.md)

**Role:** design-review scenarios and evidence limits. These are authored counterexamples, not observed live persona behavior. This page is a newly edited brief, not a rerun of the historical stress report.

## Why test an inconvenient story?

A story in which every persona volunteers for the right work, chooses the right method, and finishes on time does not exercise the design's difficult boundaries. A useful review includes missing information, plausible bad choices, conflicting versions, delayed effects, and absent reviewers.

The historical house scenario supplied that pressure. Its value is the questions it forces the design to answer, not a claim that a house was actually designed.

## Counterexamples and observable corrections

| Counterexample | What the user might wrongly conclude | What must instead be observable |
|---|---|---|
| All participants prefer attractive early work. | A busy project means the whole need is covered. | Accepted continuation, required outcomes, ownership gaps, and a response to omitted work. |
| A synthetic site assumption enters a summary. | The real site's condition has been verified. | The assumption, permitted scenario, affected claims, and outstanding confirmation. |
| A newborn is described as an expert. | New identity supplies credentials and accepted work. | Truthful provenance, limited orientation, actual membership and commitment responses, and scoped operation evidence. |
| A model-writing operation is still running. | The file found at the output location is the new completed result. | Pending status, the actual terminal receipt, exact produced version, and a fresh decision before publication. |
| Two designs use different coordinate or unit conventions. | Local passes establish a coordinated package. | Interface agreement, exact source-to-analysis mapping, integrated checks, and unresolved mismatches. |
| Participants wait for each other's final inputs. | All dependencies are recorded, so progress is assured. | Diagnosed deadlock, optional bounded provisional iteration, and honest non-convergence when unresolved. |
| A finding is acknowledged then omitted from a summary. | The review issue has been fixed. | An open finding tied to responsibility until the required disposition and evidence exist. |
| Optional improvements use the entire allowance. | The project is almost complete and needs only another small action. | Protected finishing resources, an intact baseline, and explicit funding or scope decisions. |
| An input changes after review. | The newest result inherits the earlier acceptance. | Immediate stale or pending applicability and an exact release that cannot combine incompatible states. |
| An outside request times out. | Repeating the action is harmless. | Effect-unknown status and reconciliation before any duplicate effect. |

## A more demanding review of cooperation

A strong trace shows an actual finding about an exact output, another participant's accepted response, a changed artifact or justified disposition, repeated affected checks, and suitable review. Friendly discussion and many review requests are insufficient when nobody performs the review.

The reviewer must inspect the original accepted scope as well as the team's chosen checklist. A missing plumbing or electrical system cannot disappear merely because all listed visual-design tasks were closed. The [house example](../examples/WORKED-EXAMPLES.md#a-coordinated-four-bedroom-house) states the substantial fixture explicitly without putting that domain knowledge into a fixed core workflow.

## What the historical review did and did not establish

The original report states that sixteen small abstract protocol checks were executed and that the persona dialogue was authored. Those are historical reported checks, not tests repeated here. No live persona campaign, native engineering package, simulation result, runtime binary execution, or house deliverable was established by that report.

This edition preserves the distinction rather than relabeling the source “validated.” Mechanical checks test particular guards and event orderings. Actual individuality, cooperation, learning benefit, accomplishment, and deployment suitability require their own evidence.

## Turn scenarios into an evaluation

Use the [mechanical checks](../evaluation/ACCEPTANCE.md#mechanical-checks) for repeatability, authorization, resources, sequencing, currentness, recovery, and stopping. Use the [behavioral checks](../evaluation/ACCEPTANCE.md#behavioral-checks) for real changes in choices and outcomes under matched conditions.

Publish failed and inconclusive cases. Explain what was controlled, what was observed, what remains unknown, and whether the result concerns the mechanism, the persona behavior, the domain output, or the deployment. A good stress test makes unsupported success harder to claim; it does not manufacture guaranteed competence.
