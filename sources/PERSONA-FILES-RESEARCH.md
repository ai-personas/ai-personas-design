## Engineering reasoning adopted here

The two-way development relationship is a requested product property. Direct parameter authorship, numerical identity, exact use records, an experience index, durable hypotheses, comparison and later consequence tracking are design choices intended to make that property executable and inspectable. Their composition remains unvalidated.

Several consequences follow directly from the arithmetic or state semantics. A metadata-only package change need not change numbers. A changed number can have zero effect on an inactive path. New numerical outputs can be supplied without being used by the reasoning model. A successful result can be caused by a better tool rather than the weights. These are reasons to separate the evidence levels, not claims borrowed from unrelated benchmarks.

The human-development analogy describes continuity and experience-sensitive change. This repository does not infer consciousness, feelings, a childhood, validated personality measurement or inevitable maturity from the network. Increasing all inclination values is not an optimization objective.

## What remains empirical

Whether the numerical channel is expressive enough, whether personas author warranted revisions without outside coaching, whether changes improve later work and social interaction, whether individuality is retained, and whether total cost is favorable all require scoped observations. The [evaluation method](../evaluation/README.md) states how to avoid confusing those claims with better memory or more computation.
