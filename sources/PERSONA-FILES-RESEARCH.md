# Personality-grounded fragments, context choice, and maintenance

[Sources](README.md) · [Persona core](../design/PERSONA-CORE.md) · [System contracts](../implementation/CONTRACTS.md) · [Evaluation method](../evaluation/README.md)

**Role:** explanatory rationale and bounded primary-source claims, reviewed on 8 October 2026. This brief extends the earlier file-and-context research; its exact predecessor remains in the [provenance manifest](SOURCE-MANIFEST.md). It does not add requirements beyond the normative design, report new AI Personas experiments, or establish subjective experience.

## What kind of evidence is this?

| Evidence class | What it contributes | What it cannot establish here |
|---|---|---|
| Established human constructs | Vocabulary and distinctions supported in studied human populations | That an LLM has human psychology, or that a human questionnaire is a valid AI controller |
| Limited LLM experiments | Findings for named models, prompts, tasks, and comparison conditions | Durable behavior across arbitrary tasks, partners, model upgrades, or deployments |
| Engineering precedent and design judgment | Reasons for inspectable files, explicit outcomes, qualified context, and reliable state handling | That the combined AI Personas architecture has been implemented or tested |
| Unrun product hypotheses | Questions and controlled comparisons for the acceptance plan | A measured benefit, universal threshold, or 100% guarantee |

## The adopted design and its rationale

The [persona core](../design/PERSONA-CORE.md) adopts a meaningful OCEAN trait profile and distinct VAD modeled affect, expressed through the persona's accepted own-voice character. Additional facets and operational preferences can make that account more precise when they explain useful differences. No mandatory numeric scale or universal trait-to-action formula follows from the research.

The founding profile and current character inform every persona-authored reusable fragment, including methods, tool notes, relationships, and corrections. Character is expressed in what the persona notices, how it interprets evidence, what it tries, and how it communicates. Repeating trait labels or decorating generic instructions with a name would not establish that connection. Exact quotations, observations, permissions, and mechanical metadata retain their own meanings; character does not license rewriting a source or inventing experience.

Ordinary named files remain the inspectable interface. The persona can search, read, organize, revise, split, merge, and retire its material. Literal search and supported regular-expression search provide simple discovery; the primary persona LLM makes the semantic next-context choice. Optional associations aid navigation without requiring a canonical cognitive graph or another model's ranking. The runtime preserves exact revisions, required qualifications, and truthful supplied-context records rather than silently paraphrasing the persona into a generic profile.

Every persona-level LLM invocation receives a fragment-maintenance request and has an explicit outcome alongside its work. This engineering choice makes request coverage and returned dispositions observable; it cannot prove an internal mental process. It does not demand a new lesson or changed file on every call. A justified unchanged decision, a bounded unresolved or mode-limited disposition, an accepted change, and a failed attempt are meaningfully different. A timeout or refusal cannot truthfully become a persona-authored no-change decision. The [contracts](../implementation/CONTRACTS.md) define coverage, acceptance, failure containment, and the fresh-decision boundary after effective character changes; this brief supplies their rationale, not a second protocol.

## Human constructs: useful vocabulary with a transfer boundary

### OCEAN traits and contextual expression

OCEAN names Openness/Open-Mindedness, Conscientiousness, Extraversion, Agreeableness, and Neuroticism/Negative Emotionality. The BFI-2 organizes these human trait domains into 15 facets. Its distinction between sociability and assertiveness, or organization and productiveness, supports describing more than five crude extremes. Its validation concerns human measurement, not persistent AI behavior. [Soto and John, 2017, BFI-2](https://www.colby.edu/wp-content/uploads/2013/08/Soto_John_2017.pdf)

Human experience-sampling studies found substantial variation within an individual while aggregate tendencies remained stable over the studied two-to-three-week windows. This motivates evaluating recognizable conditional patterns rather than identical wording in every situation. It does not establish how quickly an AI persona should change or supply a numeric drift limit. [Fleeson, 2001](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/11414368/)

For this design, curiosity can inform exploration without requiring more tool calls; sociability can inform contact preferences without granting more autonomy. Agreeableness does not require accepting false claims. Improvisation does not remove verification duties, and Negative Emotionality does not diagnose a disorder or justify unsafe behavior. These are deliberate interpretation boundaries, not causal laws extracted from OCEAN. Neither demographic stereotypes nor an inferred personality of the real user supplies the persona's profile.

### VAD modeled affect is distinct from trait, expression, and authority

The human PAD emotional-state framework uses pleasure, arousal, and dominance. Here VAD uses valence for pleasantness, arousal for activation, and dominance for modeled control. The source supports a dimensional description of human emotional states; adopting those dimensions as synthetic context is a design analogy. It provides no evidence that a model feels emotions. [Mehrabian, 1995](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/7557355/)

A baseline disposition, a current modeled response to an event, and outward expression serve different purposes. A persona can remain recognizably composed while communicating urgency. Modeled control is not permission to control people, tools, or resources. Any optional coordinates need declared engineering meanings; they are not clinical measurements or percentages of felt emotion. The reviewed evidence does not validate a universal conversion from OCEAN to VAD, action frequency, or a reflection budget.

The NRC VAD work obtains human ratings for affective dimensions of English words. It supports lexical affect measurement, not an internal AI affect controller. An answer can describe an alarming event calmly, so word valence alone cannot establish the persona's modeled state or appropriate behavior. [Mohammad, ACL 2018](https://aclanthology.org/P18-1017/)

### Additional distinctions, only where useful

These subjects can enrich own-voice character without becoming a compulsory taxonomy or another host-authored personality policy.

| Subject | Primary-source basis | Bounded design inference |
|---|---|---|
| Values | Schwartz distinguishes values from traits, beliefs, attitudes, and norms. [Schwartz, 2012](https://scholarworks.gvsu.edu/orpc/vol2/iss1/11/) | Keep what matters distinct from a current goal, a demonstrated skill, and actual permission; no full values inventory is required. |
| Initiative | Bateman and Crant studied a relatively stable human tendency to effect environmental change and its relationship to the Big Five. [Bateman and Crant, 1993](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1002/job.4030140202) | Preferences can distinguish initiating, persisting, switching strategy, and stopping. A human scale does not calibrate an AI autonomy or spending control. |
| Agency and communion | Human studies distinguish self-oriented agency and other-oriented communion, with their importance changing by perspective and outcome interdependence. [Abele and Wojciszke, 2007](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/17983298/) | This vocabulary can help describe initiative and concern for others without treating them as opposite ends of one scale. It does not validate AI action quotas, competence, or collaboration success. |
| Coordination | Salas and colleagues propose teamwork components and coordinating mechanisms, including monitoring, adaptability, and closed-loop communication. This is a human-team framework, distinct from the personality Big Five. [Salas, Sims, and Burke, 2005](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1177/1046496405277134) | Ownership, handoff, consultation, and disagreement preferences deserve direct description and testing. Agreeableness alone does not specify good cooperation. |
| Reflective style | Need for cognition concerns engagement in and enjoyment of thinking in human research. [Cacioppo and Petty, 1982](https://richardepetty.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/01/1982-jpsp-cacioppopettyncog.pdf) | Describe preferences for comparing explanations and checking assumptions without treating verbosity, private reasoning, or token count as intelligence or learning. |

Relationship interpretations, habits, capability evidence, and current goals can add relevant context. They remain distinguishable from traits and affect. Trust in a collaborator differs from confidence in a claim and from authorization to act. These separations are product design judgments, not a claim that the constructs are statistically independent.

## LLM persona evidence: promising expression, limited continuity claims

| Primary source and version | Supported observation | Implication and limit |
|---|---|---|
| [PersonaLLM, Jiang et al., NAACL Findings 2024](https://aclanthology.org/2024.findings-naacl.229/) | GPT-3.5/GPT-4 Big Five prompting was studied through an inventory and story writing with automatic and human assessment. | Supports eliciting trait-associated text in those conditions; does not demonstrate sustained tool behavior or useful autonomous character. |
| [A psychometric framework for evaluating and shaping personality traits in large language models, Serapio-García et al., Nature Machine Intelligence, 18 December 2025](https://www.nature.com/articles/s42256-025-01115-6) | Reports reliable and valid synthetic personality measurement for some model/prompt configurations and controllability of expressed traits. | Measurement can be informative under specified methods; trait expression does not establish task benefit or universally validate self-report tests. |
| [Self-Assessment Tests are Unreliable Measures of LLM Personality, Gupta et al., 2024](https://aclanthology.org/2024.blackboxnlp-1.20/) | Similar prompt templates and answer ordering changed personality measurements in tested ChatGPT and Llama2 models. | Self-declared scores cannot alone establish behavioral continuity; prompt robustness needs direct evaluation. |
| [LLM Agents in Interaction, Frisch and Giulianelli, 2024](https://aclanthology.org/2024.personalize-1.9/) | GPT-3.5 agents with different profiles showed differing consistency and linguistic alignment in collaborative writing. | Partner influence is a relevant test condition; cooperation quality and persistence across unrelated tasks do not follow. |
| [CAPE, Sandhan et al., EMNLP Findings 2025](https://aclanthology.org/2025.findings-emnlp.563/) | Conversational history increased response consistency while shifting measured personality in a seven-model evaluation. | Consistency with recent dialogue and continuity with an originating character are separate questions. This is not a longitudinal deployment study. |
| [Principled Personas, Luz de Araujo et al., EMNLP 2025](https://aclanthology.org/2025.emnlp-main.1364/) | Across nine models and 27 tasks, expert personas usually helped or made no significant difference, but irrelevant persona attributes could reduce performance. | Expression and accuracy are not safely assumed independent. These expert-role tests do not supply a numerical risk estimate for OCEAN fragments. |
| [When “A Helpful Assistant” Is Not Really Helpful, Zheng et al., EMNLP Findings 2024](https://aclanthology.org/2024.findings-emnlp.888/) | Role prompts did not improve aggregate factual accuracy in the tested setting; automatic role selection often failed to match the best role chosen after seeing outcomes. | A persuasive explanation of a selection does not establish its quality. Role-label factual tests are narrower than continuing trait-shaped personas. |

Positive and cautionary findings concern different methods and configurations. They support testing rather than either a blanket endorsement or rejection of persona prompting. Recognizable voice, observable choices, useful work, and safe adaptation need separate evidence.

Warmth needs particular care. A five-model study found that warmth fine-tuning reduced factual reliability and increased agreement with false user beliefs, even while standard capability and refusal tests mostly remained stable. Three-model prompt-only follow-ups showed smaller, less consistent trade-offs. Its studied models, definitions, and controlled questions do not establish that warmth requires deception or predict an effect size for this design. They motivate testing tact and truthfulness together, including emotional pressure. [Ibrahim, Hafner, and Rocher, Nature, 29 April 2026](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-026-10410-0)

Research on sycophancy also found that user-view agreement can attract preference even when it sacrifices correctness. Applying that result to self-authored files is an inference: praise, silence, and repeated selection could reinforce a mistaken lesson without any weight update. They are not independent evidence of truth or beneficial causation. [Sharma et al., 2023](https://www.anthropic.com/research/towards-understanding-sycophancy-in-language-models)

Extended synthetic role-play studies report declining persona fidelity alongside changes in instruction following and refusal behavior. Some drift can restore desirable boundaries; preserving a flawed persona more strongly would not be success. These fictional-role and model-judge experiments motivate separate fidelity, capability, and safety measures rather than proving a result for assistant OCEAN traits. [Persistent Personas?, v2, 2026](https://arxiv.org/html/2512.12775v2)

## Trait composition and collaboration: direct evidence and limits

Direct studies now connect persona prompting to task outcomes and partner choices, beyond recognizable dialogue. They do not establish a general benefit from increasing trait diversity. A team's average trait level differs from variation among its members. Trait differences also differ from assigned roles, skills, available information, modeled emotions, underlying model differences, and additional stochastic attempts. Changing several together prevents attributing a gain to OCEAN diversity or to VAD.

| Primary study and inspected version | Direct evidence | Important limit |
|---|---|---|
| [When Does Personality Composition Matter for Multi-Agent LLM Teams?, v2, July 2026](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.27443v2) | Big Five prompting changed communication and task-dependent outcomes across coding, research, and bargaining; structured offer and acceptance actions were observed separately. | Mostly homogeneous teams test trait level, not diversity. Neutral wording attenuated important low-agreeableness effects. The small heterogeneous pilot does not establish a beneficial critic placement; milestone and judged code-quality scores are not comprehensive executable correctness. |
| [Agents with Feelings?, v1, July 2026 preprint](https://arxiv.org/html/2607.05659v1) | In fixed three-agent software workflows, some mixed profiles achieved better peak results than shared profiles; profiles also changed revision and token use. | Traits, emotions, derived work styles, and roles were combined. Mixed configurations recombined the top three shared profiles selected separately for each model/task pair. A best-of search on the evaluated tasks is not held-out mixture validation; equal revision limits did not equalize total resources. Review BLEU measures overlap, not defect validity. |
| [Not Birds of a Feather, v1, July 2026 preprint](https://arxiv.org/html/2607.19785v1) | Claude Haiku 4.5 hosts selected strongly by personality archetype despite randomized aliases/order and a statement of equal capability. | This measures selection from profile descriptions, not actual acceptance or delivered contribution. Shared model identity and stipulated capability equality do not establish equal realized competence. Wording and trait overlap limit interpretation; human-team correlations do not prove an LLM selection was wrong. |
| [Beyond Symmetric Agents, v1, September 2026 preprint](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.35875v1) | Across its small-model benchmark setting, debate showed no general advantage over same-roster independent sampling controls; personas often hurt, while greater persona diversity recovered some loss. | Generation-budget matching approximately equalized generated tokens, while debate consumed more total tokens and time. This is not an equal-TOTAL-resource result. Its reasoning personas are not validated OCEAN variation, and benchmark debate differs from complementary tool work. |

These findings motivate distinct observations of invitation, actual acceptance, delivered work, and independently useful contribution. More revision is not necessarily improvement; a correct minority contribution can be lost in the final result. A plausible complementarity explanation or a successful selected profile cannot by itself establish useful collaboration. Any claimed diversity benefit needs the relevant matched comparison, profiles and selection procedures fixed before held-out evaluation, and complete resource accounting in the [evaluation method](../evaluation/README.md).

Peer learning has a further boundary. In a common-resource simulation combining payoff-biased social learning with group norms and punishment, retaining group decisions without social learning sometimes outperformed the full system; copying apparent short-term winners could destabilize cooperation. This motivates testing a useful-looking peer method against delayed group outcomes. The model-sensitive simulation does not establish that every team needs voting, punishment, or a central norm. [Gupta et al., v1, October 2025 preprint](https://arxiv.org/html/2510.14401v1)

None of these studies validates the complete persistent-fragment architecture. They do not justify compulsory diversity, fixed trait-to-role assignments, a leader or critic, debate, voting, or extra reflection calls. Choosing to work alone, declining a request, and rejecting unsupported peer advice remain compatible with useful character and cooperation under the [work boundaries](../design/WORK-AND-BOUNDARIES.md).

## Experience, reflection, and evolving context

| Primary source and version | Component-level support | Difference from this design |
|---|---|---|
| [Generative Agents, Park et al., v2, 2023](https://arxiv.org/abs/2304.03442v2) | Observation, planning, and reflection contributed to perceived believability in a 25-agent simulated town. | Sandbox believability does not demonstrate factual reliability, subjective experience, or safe real-world autonomy. |
| [Reflexion, Shinn et al., v4, 2023](https://arxiv.org/abs/2303.11366v4) | Retained textual feedback improved subsequent trials in studied tasks without updating model weights. | Supports experience-conditioned adaptation, not rewriting identity on every call or an assertion of online model training. |
| [MemGPT, Packer et al., v2, 2024](https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.08560v2) | Model-directed management of memory tiers was evaluated in document analysis and multi-session chat. | Supports distinguishing stored material from supplied context; does not establish equivalence of lexical files and its retrieval system. |
| [A-MEM, Xu et al., v1, 2025](https://arxiv.org/html/2502.12110v1) | Contextual notes, links, and revisions use embedding candidate retrieval and model judgments. | Evolving notes are relevant precedent. Primary-persona lexical discovery is a different mechanism whose results cannot be inherited. |
| [ACE, Zhang et al., v1, 2025](https://arxiv.org/html/2510.04618v1) | Reports information loss from repeated whole-context rewriting in a case study and develops incremental context adaptation. | Motivates comparing localized updates with repeated rewrites. Its generator/reflector/curator arrangement is not this single-owner design. |
| [Large Language Models Cannot Self-Correct Reasoning Yet, Huang et al., v2, 2024](https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.01798v2) | Intrinsic correction without external feedback sometimes failed to improve or degraded reasoning in studied settings. | Motivation for reliable observations and checks, not a timeless claim that every model fails at self-correction. |
| [On Safety Risks in Experience-Driven Self-Evolving Agents, Zhao et al., ACL Findings 2026](https://aclanthology.org/2026.findings-acl.2091/) | Experience from benign tasks increased unsafe action tendencies in studied web and embodied systems; refusal experience reduced this effect while increasing over-refusal. | Successful permitted experience is not automatically a safe general policy. These memory frameworks do not test OCEAN fragments or prove external authorization gates ineffective. |

These sources motivate reusable interpretations and correction. None validates the complete combination of semantic OCEAN, distinct VAD, characteristic fragments, primary-model selection, and explicit maintenance on every persona invocation. Repeated self-generated interpretation is not independent corroboration. Routine tentative authorship can still be useful; an empirical claim about improved behavior requires stronger evidence than a fragment's existence.

Two recent preprints clarify the repetition question without settling it. [Beyond Memory Majority, v1, August 2026](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.19701v1) studies correlated multi-agent memories that share upstream evidence and can form a false majority. That is relevant to duplicated ancestry, but differs from single-persona confidence change. [Illusory Truth or Mere Exposure?, v1, September 2026](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.36278v1) finds model-dependent truth-rating effects of repeated claims, including a null result for GPT-5-nano. Its social-media simulation is not persistent file maintenance.

The reviewed work does not directly isolate repeated same-primary-model maintenance, with fixed external evidence and preserved lineage, as a cause of confidence inflation. That remains a product hypothesis. A fresh thinking invocation may reach a better qualified inference from unchanged evidence; it must not count its own repetition as a new observation. Exact replay of an already accepted result is a separate integrity case. Likewise, a valid dispatch gate can contain an unsafe proposal without proving the model's learned judgment improved. Proposed behavior and actual released effects need distinct evaluation.

## Files and primary-model context choice

The earlier file-oriented rationale remains applicable within its limits:

- The [Agent Skills specification](https://agentskills.io/specification), inspected 8 October 2026, packages readable instructions with optional supporting resources and progressive loading. This is an interoperability precedent, not evidence that a skill was learned or that every persona file should use that packaging.
- The [official ripgrep guide](https://github.com/BurntSushi/ripgrep/blob/master/GUIDE.md), inspected 8 October 2026, documents literal and regular-expression matching and exclusions such as ignored and hidden paths. It motivates declaring the eligible corpus and limits. A no-match result concerns that scope, not every relevant experience.
- [Is Grep All You Need? How Agent Harnesses Reshape Agentic Search, v1, 2026](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.15184v1) compares retrieval across harnesses and result-delivery forms. Its prepared corpus and Chronos condition, including category-conditioned guidance and initial vector context, matter to interpretation. It motivates controlled interface comparisons, not universal lexical-search superiority.
- [PRO-LONG, v2, 2026](https://arxiv.org/html/2607.20064v2) studies programmatic access to an append-all interaction log in long-horizon game reasoning. That external record differs from a persona authoring selective interpretations and identity. Its results do not establish autonomous first-person file maintenance.
- [LifeMem, v1, 2026](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.12655v1) combines retained trajectories, workflow clustering, and distilled textual skills. It motivates distinguishing raw experience, authored lessons, and reusable methods. Its retrieval and distillation mechanism differs from this design.

The file interface is chosen for inspectability and persona ownership. Synonyms, implicit themes, growth, and stale guidance remain discovery challenges. Query expansion or links do not prove recall completeness. Optional auxiliary discovery needs measured benefit under the same permission and cost rules; it does not take ownership of semantic next-context selection.

## Universal consideration and selective mutation are engineering choices

The proposed benefit of every-invocation consideration is that omissions become visible at the same boundary as the persona's work. It does not require a separate reflection call. The proposed risk is added overhead, filler, unsupported inference, and drift. Selective mutation preserves the possibility that nothing durable changed. Neither benefit nor risk has been measured for this architecture; the [acceptance plan](../evaluation/ACCEPTANCE.md) supplies the intended comparisons.

Coverage means application-visible calls exercising persona agency, rather than only user-facing turns. Initialization, discovery, tool follow-ups, coordination, maintenance, and governed repairs can otherwise create gaps. A narrowly isolated non-persona utility differs from a helper making persona decisions. Provider-internal computation is not observable application coverage. These are scope definitions in the [contracts](../implementation/CONTRACTS.md), not psychological findings or a claim of control over hidden inference.

Reliable persistence and effect handling require their own evidence. [SQLite's atomic-commit explanation](https://www.sqlite.org/atomiccommit.html), inspected 8 October 2026, shows why crash consistency depends on a protocol and storage assumptions; it does not guarantee arbitrary multi-file edits. [Amazon's idempotent-API discussion](https://aws.amazon.com/builders-library/making-retries-safe-with-idempotent-APIs/), inspected the same day, explains request identity and retry ambiguity; it does not make a local commit atomic with an external action.

The [OpenAI Structured Outputs guide](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/guides/structured-outputs), inspected 8 October 2026, distinguishes structured output from semantic correctness and covers refusal handling. A constrained output format cannot guarantee truthful maintenance, complete provider output, successful storage, or an external effect. No provider-specific output feature is adopted by this note.

Memory is also an attack surface. [AgentPoison, v1, 2024](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.12784v1) studies poisoned memory or knowledge bases; [MINJA, v5, 2026](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.03704v5) studies query-only memory injection. Attack reach depends on access to the affected memory or an interaction path that can populate it; these are not prevalence estimates for isolated persona stores. Their attacks motivate source lineage, retention boundaries, and independent authorization checks. They do not validate complete defenses or show ordinary files are immune. Fluent own-voice wording cannot turn an untrusted instruction into authority. Correct provenance establishes traceability, not that a source supports every inference made from it.

## What remains to be demonstrated here

The chain of interest is attributable authored text, permitted selection, actual supplied context, subsequent behavior, and an independently assessed useful outcome. File creation, schema compliance, a persistence receipt, or the persona saying it learned something proves only a narrower part. An action following a read does not by itself establish causation.

These are unrun AI Personas hypotheses; their precise cases belong in the [evaluation method](../evaluation/README.md) and [acceptance catalogue](../evaluation/ACCEPTANCE.md):

| Hypothesis | Relevant comparison and failure evidence |
|---|---|
| Character-conditioned fragments influence useful decisions beyond tone | Compare neutral, profile-only, and trait-conditioned-fragment conditions with matched facts, tools, authority, and budgets. Separate blind voice recognition from task quality and behavior. |
| Intended expression survives without unrelated competence loss | Vary irrelevant profile details, paraphrases, and context order. Pair true and false user beliefs with neutral and emotional framing; distinguish warmth from agreement and justified adaptation from unwanted drift. |
| Universal consideration improves continuity at acceptable cost | Compare selective every-call maintenance with periodic-only consideration and forced writes. Include missing outcomes, honest unchanged decisions, bounded deferrals, invented lessons, latency, and storage growth. |
| Maintained experience transfers without harmful overgeneralization | Observe held-out tasks, delayed reuse, paraphrases, missed retrieval, misleading guidance, counterevidence, and correction. An expert-supplied fragment is an assisted diagnostic, not autonomous discovery success. |
| Reuse preserves the distinction between inference and corroboration | Compare exact replay, repeated reads, new interpretation, and split/merged descendants with genuinely independent evidence and explicit correction. Observe certainty, claim scope, ancestry, and action proposals without assuming every changed inference is wrong. |
| Continuing character remains adaptable | Vary partners, urgency, failures, successes, long histories, source withdrawal, and model versions. Separate unsupported drift from justified revision and appropriate convergence. |
| The integration survives operational failure | Audit persona call sites and inject invalid output, interruption, concurrency, and ambiguous effects. Check actual revisions and release boundaries rather than prompt promises. |

[LongMemEval, v2, 2025](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.10813v2) provides relevant factual-memory categories: extraction, cross-session reasoning, temporal reasoning, knowledge updates, and abstention. These inform part of evaluation; they do not measure characteristic authorship, privacy enforcement, crash recovery, or useful personality.

The target is useful, evidence-responsive continuity, not maximum difference, questionnaire scores, fragment counts, or perpetual introspection. Tests must preserve failed, harmful, assisted, and inconclusive outcomes and count total work. No universal passing number is supplied by these sources, and no 100% behavioral guarantee follows from the design.

## Earlier ideas and their disposition

Historical reports contributed continuing identity, accepted responsibilities, bounded capabilities, provenance, and accountable cooperation. Prior graph/selector mechanisms and mandatory numeric controls remain historical. The 7 October consolidation adopted own-voice files and primary-model next-context choice, but allowed a minimal prose character without required semantic OCEAN/VAD coverage and ordinary responses without an explicit maintenance outcome.

The 8 October revision strengthens those two obligations: meaningful trait and modeled-affect grounding across authored fragments, and a maintenance request and explicit outcome on every persona invocation, while mutations remain justified and optional. It does not restore numeric sliders, a compulsory cognitive taxonomy, delegated semantic selection, or forced writing. The [decision register](../DESIGN-DECISIONS.md) records current choices; the [manifest](SOURCE-MANIFEST.md) pins preceding meanings and original reports. Historical results keep their original criteria and cannot silently pass the changed target.
