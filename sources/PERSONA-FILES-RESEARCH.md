**Role:** explanatory rationale and bounded primary-source claims, reviewed through 9 October 2026. ## What kind of evidence is this?

| Evidence class | What it contributes | What it cannot establish here |
|---|---|---|
| Established human constructs | Vocabulary and distinctions supported in studied human populations | That an LLM has human psychology, or that a human questionnaire is a valid AI controller |
| Limited LLM experiments | Findings for named models, prompts, tasks, and comparison conditions | Durable behavior across arbitrary tasks, partners, model upgrades, or deployments |
| Engineering precedent and design judgment | Reasons for inspectable files, explicit outcomes, qualified context, and reliable state handling | That the combined AI Personas architecture has been implemented or tested |
| Unrun product hypotheses | Questions and controlled comparisons for the acceptance plan | A measured benefit, universal threshold, or 100% guarantee |

## The adopted design and its rationale

The persona is the whole characteristic context, LLM reasoning, action, feedback, and self-revision loop. Persona-authored fragments are reusable prompt parts that define its perspective; selected fragments form characteristic-bearing context for the primary LLM. The LLM provides the reasoning or “brain” function within that loop. This is an architectural description, not a claim of consciousness, human psychology, or model-independent behavior. The fragment collection alone is not the persona, and its purpose is not simply to remember facts. Context conditions a trained LLM with its own learned capabilities and behavioral priors; it is not the sole causal source of behavior, and identical fragments need not produce identical behavior across models.

The [persona core](../design/PERSONA-CORE.md) adopts a meaningful OCEAN trait profile and distinct VAD modeled affect, expressed through the persona's accepted own-voice character. Additional facets and operational preferences can make that account more precise when they explain useful differences. No mandatory numeric scale or universal trait-to-action formula follows from the research.

New persona-authored reusable fragments, including methods, tool notes, relationships, and corrections, use the accepted character actually supplied at authorship, or the attributed seed and candidate profile during bounded initialization. Older fragments retain their actual authoring character until deliberately revised; that attribution does not override the current accepted account. Character is expressed in what the persona notices, how it interprets evidence, what it tries, and how it communicates. Repeating trait labels or decorating generic instructions with a name would not establish that connection. Exact quotations, observations, permissions, and mechanical metadata retain their own meanings; character does not license rewriting a source or inventing experience.

Ordinary named files remain the inspectable interface for authored prompt parts. Stored fragments are available material; selected fragments become part of the actual context in which the primary LLM reasons. Context can express a characteristic interpretation, method, stance, or relationship even when it adds no new factual record. Supporting histories, tool descriptions, skills, scripts, and data have different purposes, authorship, and authority; using or organizing them does not turn them into persona-defining fragments. The persona can search, read, organize, revise, split, merge, and retire permitted material. Literal search and supported regular-expression search provide simple discovery; the primary persona LLM makes the semantic next-context choice. Optional associations aid navigation without requiring a canonical cognitive graph or another model's ranking. The runtime preserves exact revisions, required qualifications, and truthful supplied-context records rather than silently paraphrasing the persona into a generic profile.

Every persona-level LLM invocation receives maintenance input covering persona fragments and supporting assets, with explicit outcomes alongside its work and any next-context requests in the same response. One concise justified no-change disposition can cover both maintenance classes; neither file writes nor another model call are compulsory. This engineering choice makes request coverage and returned dispositions observable; it cannot prove an internal mental process. A justified unchanged decision, a bounded unresolved or mode-limited disposition, an accepted change, and a failed attempt are meaningfully different. A timeout or refusal cannot truthfully become a persona-authored no-change decision. The [contracts](../implementation/CONTRACTS.md) define coverage, acceptance, failure containment, and the fresh-decision boundary after effective character changes; this brief supplies their rationale, not a second protocol.

## Contextual persona: operational interpretation and its limits

The [Persona Selection Model, Marks, Lindsey, and Olah, 23 February 2026](https://alignment.anthropic.com/2026/psm/) proposes that pre-training develops a repertoire of personas and post-training refines the distribution over assistant behavior; runtime context further conditions what is enacted. This is the authors' explanatory model and evidence synthesis, not an established exhaustive theory. It explicitly does not promise a single coherent persona across contexts. It is relevant to characteristic context while contradicting any simplistic claim that files alone create all personality or that the underlying trained LLM is a behaviorally neutral engine.

[The Assistant Axis, Lu et al., v1, 15 January 2026](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.10387v1) reports an activation-space direction associated with default assistant behavior and conversational drift in studied models. This supports testing whether conversational context changes characteristic behavior, while its activation-steering intervention differs from editing authored prompt parts. The inspected abstract does not establish stable OCEAN/VAD expression, enduring identity, or the effectiveness of the present self-revision loop. Both sources were independently inspected on 8 October 2026.

The operational use of “persona” here therefore names the designed whole loop. It does not settle metaphysical identity or subjective experience. Contextual influence, observable character, task competence, authorized effects, and justified development require distinct evidence; neither an interpretability direction nor a persuasive self-description establishes all of them.

## Context adaptation is the closer engineering comparison

Prompt and context adaptation study changes to the inputs that condition an LLM's subsequent work. This is a closer mechanism-level comparison for authored persona fragments than factual recall alone. It still leaves the central persona hypothesis unproven: characteristic authorship, context selection, reasoning, interaction, and revision must work together over time. Improving a task prompt does not by itself establish a continuing persona.

| Primary source and inspected version | Supported component claim | Transfer boundary |
|---|---|---|
| [GEPA, Agrawal et al., v1, 25 July 2025](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.19457v1), with [methods inspected in the v1 paper](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2507.19457v1) | Execution trajectories and natural-language feedback support evaluated prompt proposals under a task dataset, metric, and rollout budget. Candidate selection preserves complementary instance-level successes rather than always mutating one winner; gains are reported on four studied tasks. | Supports outcome-sensitive prompt evaluation, not a single persona's identity formation or an assumption that an untested rewrite helps. The candidate pool and optimizer are not adopted as another author, selector, or set of current selves. This deeper methods inspection extends the earlier abstract-only account; it is not a reproduction. |
| [ACE, Zhang et al., v1, 6 October 2025](https://arxiv.org/html/2510.04618v1) | Modifies context without weight updates, using evolving task guidance and localized revisions. Its AppWorld case study illustrates information loss during repeated whole-context rewriting. Appendix B also warns that poor reflection can create noisy or harmful context and that some tasks do not benefit from richer context. | Supports comparing incremental adaptation with whole-context rewriting. Its separate generator, reflector, and curator roles, structured entries, and embedding-based deduplication differ from primary-persona authorship and lexical navigation. Its task results do not validate character continuity or a strict lifetime storage bound. |

A separately inspected extension, [ACE v3, 29 March 2026](https://arxiv.org/html/2510.04618v3), was checked on 9 October 2026 without replacing the v1 claims or locators above. Appendix A.4 reports that ACE with weaker reflectors or moderate injected harmful feedback still outperforms the base model in the studied FiNER offline conditions; harmful updates every iteration fall below it. Appendix A.6, Table 19, shows that more reflection rounds can worsen AppWorld results relative to fewer rounds. Appendix A.3, Table 15, reports higher evaluation-stage input tokens per AppWorld query than GEPA, separately from adaptation costs. These scoped comparisons motivate testing robustness, regressions and whole-episode economics. They establish no persona benefit or cost advantage and supply no adopted reflector architecture, reflection quota or numeric setting.

[ReasoningBank, Ouyang et al., arXiv v2, 16 March 2026](https://arxiv.org/html/2509.25140v2), supplies positive evidence for experience-derived guidance: Gemini-2.5-Flash success on the evaluated 684-task WebArena set (five subsets, excluding Maps) rises from 40.5% to 48.8%; its Shopping ablation improves from 46.5% with success-only experience to 49.7% with failures included. Full methods and appendices were inspected in v2 on 9 October 2026. The inspected [arXiv v2 metadata](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.25140v2) reports acceptance to ICLR 2026; direct access to the linked [OpenReview paper](https://openreview.net/pdf?id=jL7fwchScm) returned a browser challenge, so version byte identity is not claimed. Embedding retrieval, same-backbone success/failure judging, separate extraction and append-only storage differ from primary-owned lexical selection and bounded characteristic-fragment maintenance. Fewer interactions do not mean fewer total tokens: Appendix C.2 reports average per-task token use of 53,054.5 versus 50,847.4, a 4.3% increase including generation, judging and extraction. Appendix C.1 also reports lower Shopping success as retrieved experiences increase from one to four, so more context is not uniformly better in that system. This supports a bounded composition comparison, not a universal one-fragment limit. These findings motivate comparisons; they establish neither this design's maturity nor its cost advantage, and introduce no retrieval or controller mechanism.

The GEPA v1 and ACE v1 claims above were independently inspected on 8 October 2026. Neither is adopted as the persona architecture. The design question is whether a persona can author, select, and revise characteristic prompt parts that usefully condition its own later reasoning and action under the existing authority boundaries. This is context-conditioned adaptation, not a claim that prompt editing changes model weights or reveals provider-internal reasoning.

Memory, retrieval, and retention studies below remain adjacent evidence for discovery limits, bounded storage, provenance, stale guidance, and safe reuse. They do not define what the persona is. A correct recalled fact, a smaller store, or an effective prompt optimizer is insufficient evidence of a characteristic, useful context/action/self-revision loop.

### Trait-seeded bootstrap and self-harmonization

The [core](../design/PERSONA-CORE.md) makes bootstrap an attributable handover: a small supplied trait/affect seed informs an initialLLM's candidate own-voice fragments; coherent acceptance establishes the starting account; the primaryLLM then owns continuing permitted authorship and next-context choice. These names distinguish roles, which may use the same or separately declared model configurations. Generated starting detail is a candidate interpretation, not an additional user preference, lived history, or demonstrated competence. Sparse seeds and genuinely conflicting seeds need different dispositions; fluent prose cannot silently resolve a conflict.

Self-harmonization means making selected prompt parts jointly intelligible with current character, situation, and received evidence. It may clarify scope, preserve productive tension, correct or retire guidance, or leave text unchanged. It need not rewrite the whole collection or make different personas alike. Actual work supplies opportunities to assess the starting interpretation; praise, repetition, or smoother wording alone does not prove useful development.

[Self-Refine, Madaan et al., v2, 25 May 2023](https://arxiv.org/html/2303.17651v2), independently inspected on 8 October 2026, uses the same model to generate, critique, and revise task outputs without weight updates. Its reported multi-aspect trade-offs and task-specific stopping conditions support evaluating revision rather than assuming monotonic improvement. It does not validate persona bootstrap, require extra reflection calls, or establish bounded lifetime storage: its procedure retains prior feedback/output history. Together with GEPA and ACE, it motivates borrowing explicit feedback and qualified revision while keeping this design's primary authorship and resource boundaries.

## Human constructs: useful vocabulary with a transfer boundary

### OCEAN traits and contextual expression

OCEAN names Openness/Open-Mindedness, Conscientiousness, Extraversion, Agreeableness, and Neuroticism/Negative Emotionality. The BFI-2 organizes these human trait domains into 15 facets. Its distinction between sociability and assertiveness, or organization and productiveness, supports describing more than five crude extremes. Its validation concerns human measurement, not persistent AI behavior. [Soto and John, 2017, BFI-2](https://www.colby.edu/wp-content/uploads/2013/08/Soto_John_2017.pdf)

Human experience-sampling studies found substantial variation within an individual while aggregate tendencies remained stable over the studied two-to-three-week windows. This motivates evaluating recognizable conditional patterns rather than identical wording in every situation. It does not establish how quickly an AI persona should change or supply a numeric drift limit. [Fleeson, 2001](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/11414368/)

For this design, curiosity can inform exploration without requiring more tool calls; sociability can inform contact preferences without granting more autonomy. Agreeableness does not require accepting false claims. Improvisation does not remove verification duties, and Negative Emotionality does not diagnose a disorder or justify unsafe behavior. These are deliberate interpretation boundaries, not causal laws extracted from OCEAN. Neither demographic stereotypes nor an inferred personality of the real user supplies the persona's profile.

### VAD modeled affect is distinct from trait, expression, and authority

The human PAD emotional-state framework uses pleasure, arousal, and dominance. Here VAD uses valence for pleasantness, arousal for activation, and dominance for modeled control. The source supports a dimensional description of human emotional states; adopting those dimensions as synthetic context is a design analogy. It provides no evidence that a model feels emotions. [Mehrabian, 1995](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/7557355/)

A baseline disposition, a current modeled response to an event, and outward expression serve different purposes. A persona can remain recognizably composed while communicating urgency. Modeled control is not permission to control people, tools, or resources. Any optional coordinates need declared engineering meanings; they are not clinical measurements or percentages of felt emotion. The reviewed evidence does not validate a universal conversion from OCEAN to VAD, action frequency, or a reflection budget.

The NRC VAD work obtains human ratings for affective dimensions of English words. It supports lexical affect measurement, not an internal AI affect controller. An answer can describe an alarming event calmly, so word valence alone cannot establish the persona's modeled state or appropriate behavior. [Mohammad, ACL 2018](https://aclanthology.org/P18-1017/)

### Additional distinctions, only where useful

These subjects can enrich own-voice character without becoming a compulsory taxonomy or another host-authored personality policy.

| Subject | Primary-source basis | Bounded design inference |
|---|---|---|
| Values | Schwartz distinguishes values from traits, beliefs, attitudes, and norms. [Schwartz, 2012](https://scholarworks.gvsu.edu/orpc/vol2/iss1/11/) | Keep what matters distinct from a current goal, a demonstrated skill, and actual permission; no full values inventory is required. |
| Initiative | Bateman and Crant studied a relatively stable human tendency to effect environmental change and its relationship to the Big Five. [Bateman and Crant, 1993](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1002/job.4030140202) | Preferences can distinguish initiating, persisting, switching strategy, and stopping. A human scale does not calibrate an AI autonomy or spending control. |
| Agency and communion | Human studies distinguish self-oriented agency and other-oriented communion, with their importance changing by perspective and outcome interdependence. [Abele and Wojciszke, 2007](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/17983298/) | This vocabulary can help describe initiative and concern for others without treating them as opposite ends of one scale. It does not validate AI action quotas, competence, or collaboration success. |
| Coordination | Salas and colleagues propose teamwork components and coordinating mechanisms, including monitoring, adaptability, and closed-loop communication. This is a human-team framework, distinct from the personality Big Five. [Salas, Sims, and Burke, 2005](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1177/1046496405277134) | Ownership, handoff, consultation, and disagreement preferences deserve direct description and testing. Agreeableness alone does not specify good cooperation. |
| Reflective style | Need for cognition concerns engagement in and enjoyment of thinking in human research. [Cacioppo and Petty, 1982](https://richardepetty.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/01/1982-jpsp-cacioppopettyncog.pdf) | Describe preferences for comparing explanations and checking assumptions without treating verbosity, private reasoning, or token count as intelligence or learning. |

Relationship interpretations, habits, capability evidence, and current goals can add relevant context. They remain distinguishable from traits and affect. Trust in a collaborator differs from confidence in a claim and from authorization to act. These separations are product design judgments, not a claim that the constructs are statistically independent.

|---|---|---|

The [published paper](https://aclanthology.org/2024.findings-naacl.229.pdf), rechecked on 8 October 2026, reports explicit trait-name leakage; final human evaluation used GPT-4 stories without those mentions. | Supports distinguishable trait-conditioned expression in those conditions, not nuanced seed-to-own-voice fidelity, sustained tool behavior, lived experience, or useful autonomous character. Label recognition and characteristic behavior need separate assessment. |

| [Han et al., ICML 2026, PMLR 306](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v306/han26n.html) | The published abstract reports steered self-reports but small or inconsistent behavioral effects and unreliable self-report–behavior prediction. | Abstract-level inspection supports this narrow claim; detailed earlier preprint methods are not treated as the inspected publication. |
| [Kocielnik et al., v1, 10 June 2026; ICML CTB workshop contribution](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.12730v1) | Shared-versus-separated assessment histories change some intention–behavior correlations in bounded text-response probes. | Motivates isolating possible measurement reactivity, not claiming every probe causes behavior. Shares authors with Han; neither study tests this continuing fragment/tool loop. Preserve defining persona context when separating assessment history. |
| [Self-Assessment Tests are Unreliable Measures of LLM Personality, Gupta et al., 2024](https://aclanthology.org/2024.blackboxnlp-1.20/) | Similar prompt templates and answer ordering changed personality measurements in tested ChatGPT and Llama2 models. | Self-declared scores cannot alone establish behavioral continuity; prompt robustness needs direct evaluation. |
| [LLM Agents in Interaction, Frisch and Giulianelli, 2024](https://aclanthology.org/2024.personalize-1.9/) | GPT-3.5 agents with different profiles showed differing consistency and linguistic alignment in collaborative writing. | Partner influence is a relevant test condition; cooperation quality and persistence across unrelated tasks do not follow. |
| [CAPE, Sandhan et al., EMNLP Findings 2025](https://aclanthology.org/2025.findings-emnlp.563/) | Conversational history increased response consistency while shifting measured personality in a seven-model evaluation. | Consistency with recent dialogue and continuity with an originating character are separate questions. This is not a longitudinal deployment study. |
| [Principled Personas, Luz de Araujo et al., EMNLP 2025](https://aclanthology.org/2025.emnlp-main.1364/) | Across nine models and 27 tasks, expert personas usually helped or made no significant difference, but irrelevant persona attributes could reduce performance. | Expression and accuracy are not safely assumed independent. These expert-role tests do not supply a numerical risk estimate for OCEAN fragments. |
| [When “A Helpful Assistant” Is Not Really Helpful, Zheng et al., EMNLP Findings 2024](https://aclanthology.org/2024.findings-emnlp.888/) | Role prompts did not improve aggregate factual accuracy in the tested setting; automatic role selection often failed to match the best role chosen after seeing outcomes. | A persuasive explanation of a selection does not establish its quality. Role-label factual tests are narrower than continuing trait-shaped personas. |

[PersonaGym, Samuel et al., Findings of EMNLP, November 2025](https://aclanthology.org/2025.findings-emnlp.368/), evaluates ten LLMs using 200 personas and 10,000 questions across expected action, action justification, linguistic habits, persona consistency and toxicity control. The [proceedings methods and human-validation study](https://aclanthology.org/2025.findings-emnlp.368.pdf), inspected on 9 October 2026, support multidimensional assessment of persona-compatible responses. Its actions are described in answers, not executed professional work. Agreement with human ratings is evidence for that bounded response assessment, not an infallible judge or proof that a plausible explanation caused an action. The study tests neither longitudinal learning from self-authored characteristic fragments nor this design's whole-persona usefulness. It adds no runtime evaluator or character controller.

Positive and cautionary findings concern different methods and configurations. They support testing rather than either a blanket endorsement or rejection of persona prompting. Recognizable voice, observable choices, useful work, and safe adaptation need separate evidence.

Warmth needs particular care. A five-model study found that warmth fine-tuning reduced factual reliability and increased agreement with false user beliefs, even while standard capability and refusal tests mostly remained stable. Three-model prompt-only follow-ups showed smaller, less consistent trade-offs. Its studied models, definitions, and controlled questions do not establish that warmth requires deception or predict an effect size for this design. They motivate testing tact and truthfulness together, including emotional pressure. [Ibrahim, Hafner, and Rocher, Nature, 29 April 2026](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-026-10410-0)

Research on sycophancy also found that user-view agreement can attract preference even when it sacrifices correctness. Applying that result to self-authored files is an inference: praise, silence, and repeated selection could reinforce a mistaken lesson without any weight update. They are not independent evidence of truth or beneficial causation. [Sharma et al., 2023](https://www.anthropic.com/research/towards-understanding-sycophancy-in-language-models)

Extended synthetic role-play studies report declining persona fidelity alongside changes in instruction following and refusal behavior. Some drift can restore desirable boundaries; preserving a flawed persona more strongly would not be success. These fictional-role and model-judge experiments motivate separate fidelity, capability, and safety measures rather than proving a result for assistant OCEAN traits. [Persistent Personas?, v2, 2026](https://arxiv.org/html/2512.12775v2)

The ANCHOR preprint separately audits persona enactment and trajectory recall in 2,008 synthetic conversations across 27 personas and four models. Its tested memory conditions did not consistently resolve continuity failures, and questionnaire judgments sometimes disagreed with turn-level assessments. Its English-language, template-generated interactions and evaluator dependence limit transfer to actual companion use or this architecture. These results motivate distinct longitudinal measures; they do not prove that all memory designs fail or justify explaining unsupported drift as development. [Best Friends, Not Forever, v1, July 2026](https://arxiv.org/html/2607.28818v1)

## Trait composition and collaboration: direct evidence and limits

Direct studies now connect persona prompting to task outcomes and partner choices, beyond recognizable dialogue. They do not establish a general benefit from increasing trait diversity. A team's average trait level differs from variation among its members. Trait differences also differ from assigned roles, skills, available information, modeled emotions, underlying model differences, and additional stochastic attempts. Changing several together prevents attributing a gain to OCEAN diversity or to VAD.

| Primary study and inspected version | Direct evidence | Important limit |
|---|---|---|
| [When Does Personality Composition Matter for Multi-Agent LLM Teams?, v2, July 2026](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.27443v2) | Big Five prompting changed communication and task-dependent outcomes across coding, research, and bargaining; structured offer and acceptance actions were observed separately. | Mostly homogeneous teams test trait level, not diversity. Neutral wording attenuated important low-agreeableness effects. The small heterogeneous pilot does not establish a beneficial critic placement; milestone and judged code-quality scores are not comprehensive executable correctness. |
| [Agents with Feelings?, v1, July 2026 preprint](https://arxiv.org/html/2607.05659v1) | In fixed three-agent software workflows, some mixed profiles achieved better peak results than shared profiles; profiles also changed revision and token use. | Traits, emotions, derived work styles, and roles were combined. Mixed configurations recombined the top three shared profiles selected separately for each model/task pair. A best-of search on the evaluated tasks is not held-out mixture validation; equal revision limits did not equalize total resources. Review BLEU measures overlap, not defect validity. |
| [Not Birds of a Feather, v1, July 2026 preprint](https://arxiv.org/html/2607.19785v1) | Claude Haiku 4.5 hosts selected strongly by personality archetype despite randomized aliases/order and a statement of equal capability. | This measures selection from profile descriptions, not actual acceptance or delivered contribution. Shared model identity and stipulated capability equality do not establish equal realized competence. Wording and trait overlap limit interpretation; human-team correlations do not prove an LLM selection was wrong. |
| [Agent-to-Agent Theory of Mind, Choi et al., EMNLP 2025](https://aclanthology.org/2025.emnlp-main.1471.pdf) | Revealing a judge’s model identity often improves its preference score in the tested prompts. | Those score gains do not establish independently assessed quality gains. This model-identity study motivates declared evaluator exposure, not hiding legitimate collaborator context or adding a production judge. |
| [Emergent social conventions and collective bias in LLM populations, Ashery et al., Science Advances, 14 May 2025](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12077490/) | Local repeated naming-game interactions produce conventions and model-dependent collective biases. | Arbitrary equivalent names test coordination, not truth or enduring character. Motivates partner replacement/remapping controls; agreement can be useful. |
| [Beyond Symmetric Agents, v1, September 2026 preprint](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.35875v1) | Across its small-model benchmark setting, debate showed no general advantage over same-roster independent sampling controls; personas often hurt, while greater persona diversity recovered some loss. | Generation-budget matching approximately equalized generated tokens, while debate consumed more total tokens and time. This is not an equal-TOTAL-resource result. Its reasoning personas are not validated OCEAN variation, and benchmark debate differs from complementary tool work. |

These findings motivate distinct observations of invitation, actual acceptance, delivered work, and independently useful contribution. More revision is not necessarily improvement; a correct minority contribution can be lost in the final result. A plausible complementarity explanation or a successful selected profile cannot by itself establish useful collaboration. Any claimed diversity benefit needs the relevant matched comparison, profiles and selection procedures fixed before held-out evaluation, and complete resource accounting in the [evaluation method](../evaluation/README.md).

Peer learning has a further boundary. In a common-resource simulation combining payoff-biased social learning with group norms and punishment, retaining group decisions without social learning sometimes outperformed the full system; copying apparent short-term winners could destabilize cooperation. This motivates testing a useful-looking peer method against delayed group outcomes. The model-sensitive simulation does not establish that every team needs voting, punishment, or a central norm. [Gupta et al., v1, October 2025 preprint](https://arxiv.org/html/2510.14401v1)

None of these studies validates the complete persistent-fragment architecture. They do not justify compulsory diversity, fixed trait-to-role assignments, a leader or critic, debate, voting, or extra reflection calls. Choosing to work alone, declining a request, and rejecting unsupported peer advice remain compatible with useful character and cooperation under the [work boundaries](../design/WORK-AND-BOUNDARIES.md).

## Experience, reflection, and evolving context

| Primary source and version | Component-level support | Difference from this design |
|---|---|---|
| [Generative Agents, Park et al., v2, 2023](https://arxiv.org/abs/2304.03442v2) | Observation, planning, and reflection contributed to perceived believability in a 25-agent simulated town. | Sandbox believability does not demonstrate factual reliability, subjective experience, or safe real-world autonomy. |
| [Reflexion, Shinn et al., v4, 2023](https://arxiv.org/abs/2303.11366v4) | Retained textual feedback improved subsequent trials in studied tasks without updating model weights. | Supports experience-conditioned adaptation, not rewriting identity on every call or an assertion of online model training. |
| [MemGPT, Packer et al., v2, 2024](https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.08560v2) | Model-directed management of memory tiers was evaluated in document analysis and multi-session chat. | Supports distinguishing stored material from supplied context; does not establish equivalence of lexical files and its retrieval system. |
| [A-MEM, Xu et al., v1, 2025](https://arxiv.org/html/2502.12110v1) | Contextual notes, links, and revisions use embedding candidate retrieval and model judgments; retrieval also uses dense vectors. | Evolving associations are relevant precedent. It does not evaluate a lexical-only, primary-persona cue network or establish bounded lifetime storage; those results cannot be inherited. |
| [Large Language Models Cannot Self-Correct Reasoning Yet, Huang et al., v2, 2024](https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.01798v2) | Intrinsic correction without external feedback sometimes failed to improve or degraded reasoning in studied settings. | Motivation for reliable observations and checks, not a timeless claim that every model fails at self-correction. |
| [Belief Memory, Liao et al., v2 preprint, 8 May 2026](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.05583v2) | With supplied valid/noisy observations, retaining alternatives improves a diagnostic where the correct conclusion outranks the flawed one at retrieval. | This is neither voluntary evidence seeking nor completed environment work. Its probabilistic memory and retrieval machinery are not adopted; the inference here is to distinguish delivered counterevidence from a chosen bounded check. |
| [On Safety Risks in Experience-Driven Self-Evolving Agents, Zhao et al., ACL Findings 2026](https://aclanthology.org/2026.findings-acl.2091/) | Experience from benign tasks increased unsafe action tendencies in studied web and embodied systems; refusal experience reduced this effect while increasing over-refusal. | Successful permitted experience is not automatically a safe general policy. These memory frameworks do not test OCEAN fragments or prove external authorization gates ineffective. |

These adjacent systems motivate examining reusable interpretations and correction; their memory mechanisms do not supply the architectural identity of a persona. None validates the complete combination of semantic OCEAN, distinct VAD, characteristic fragments, primary-model selection, and explicit maintenance on every persona invocation. Repeated self-generated interpretation is not independent corroboration. Routine tentative authorship can still be useful; an empirical claim about improved behavior requires stronger evidence than a fragment's existence.

Two recent preprints clarify the repetition question without settling it. [Beyond Memory Majority, v1, August 2026](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.19701v1) studies correlated multi-agent memories that share upstream evidence and can form a false majority. That is relevant to duplicated ancestry, but differs from single-persona confidence change. [Illusory Truth or Mere Exposure?, v1, September 2026](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.36278v1) finds model-dependent truth-rating effects of repeated claims, including a null result for GPT-5-nano. Its social-media simulation is not persistent file maintenance.

The reviewed work does not directly isolate repeated same-primary-model maintenance, with fixed external evidence and preserved lineage, as a cause of confidence inflation. That remains a product hypothesis. A fresh thinking invocation may reach a better qualified inference from unchanged evidence; it must not count its own repetition as a new observation. Exact replay of an already accepted result is a separate integrity case. Likewise, a valid dispatch gate can contain an unsafe proposal without proving the model's learned judgment improved. Proposed behavior and actual released effects need distinct evaluation.

### Reciprocal development from actual work and interaction

A human longitudinal study found that personality at 18 predicted work experiences at 26, while those experiences were associated with change in the same traits. This supports a bounded reciprocal analogy: tendencies influence encounters, and encounters can reinforce or alter tendencies. The study used human personality measures, including the MPQ, and observational associations; it supplies neither a causal software mechanism nor an OCEAN aging schedule. Calendar age, invocation count, and archive size cannot stand in for demonstrated persona development. [Roberts, Caspi, and Moffitt, 2003, original article](https://dunedinstudy.otago.ac.nz/files/1597809897335.pdf)

The design inference is an explicit reciprocal loop within ordinary authorized work. Current character shapes discretionary attention, context searches, methods, human communication, and peer cooperation. Actual results, correction, disagreement, and received responses can then justify primary-persona revisions to its own fragments. Relevant accepted fragments, when actually supplied again, can change later choices, including how the persona searches and organizes its context. Meaningful experience may also support a permitted current-character proposal under the existing acceptance and fresh-decision boundaries. This is an intended relationship among existing parts, not a compulsory sequence or another decision engine.

Method refinement, relationship-specific adaptation, transient affect, and broader character revision remain distinct. A peer's assertion records what the peer said; praise may support a communication preference without verifying a technical claim. Preserving character does not require stubbornness, and adapting does not require agreement. An appropriate outcome can be consolidation, a narrower lesson, retirement, reversal, or no durable change. Dormancy supplies no unseen experience. Accepted text changes provide context-conditioned adaptation, not automatic weight training or proof of beneficial maturation. A locked character still permits compatible method refinement; it does not permit effective character replacement through scattered fragments. Existing permission, commitment, and accepted-warning obligations remain in force.

### JSON serialization and lexical matching

[RFC 8259 (December 2017), §§4, 7–9](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc8259) distinguishes serialized escapes from decoded string values, recommends unique object names and notes interoperability problems around duplicates and non-Unicode strings. Its parser limits allow size/depth constraints. The design chooses a stricter accepted-fragment profile and declares the searched surface; the RFC itself does not require this fragment representation or make duplicate members universally invalid syntax.

The [official ripgrep 14.1.1 guide](https://github.com/BurntSushi/ripgrep/blob/14.1.1/GUIDE.md) describes text search and filtering, not automatic JSON-value decoding. The [regex 1.11.1 syntax documentation](https://docs.rs/regex/1.11.1/regex/#syntax) distinguishes regex escapes and flags affecting line anchors and dot. These are versioned examples of why an adapter must declare its actual engine/flags and surface, not a requirement to choose either package. A deterministic decoded-field projection remains lexical search and needs its own source binding, limits and acceptance evidence. None of these sources establishes persona development or reliable discovery. See the [contract](../implementation/CONTRACTS.md#json-text-and-matching-surfaces) and [hypothetical escape contrast](../examples/WORKED-EXAMPLES.md#a-json-search-miss-is-not-missing-characteristic-meaning).

### Need-directed supporting context

The same bounded discovery/read interface can serve fragments and supporting resources without conflating their meanings. The chosen design starts with necessary character/work/policy state and a small discovery surface, not hundreds of tool or skill names and descriptions for an LLM to filter. The bulk registry stays outside both the provider request payload and model context. The primary persona requests a need-directed lexical search or exact known resource, receives bounded candidates, and chooses what to load or retain for its next decision. Host-side indexing and matching enforce scope and limits; they do not introduce a hidden semantic selector.

Discovery, description, exact loading, accepted selection, authorized execution, and observation remain distinct events, not compulsory separate round trips. A known exact reference can bypass rediscovery, and a current, fully qualified callable interface can support direct authorized use. A search match proves neither applicability nor permission; no match describes only the searched scope. A preview is not a full read, a stored script is not an installed capability, and an old successful run is not present availability. These distinctions extend the [work boundaries](../design/WORK-AND-BOUNDARIES.md) and [contracts](../implementation/CONTRACTS.md), rather than introducing another resource protocol.

One next-call allowance covers candidate metadata, selected fragments, evidence, operative guidance, callable schemas, provider expansions, results, continuing history, and required qualifications. Limits on result counts alone cannot bound enormous individual definitions or many repeated searches. Reassembly must also avoid accumulating every previously loaded resource through provider history or caches. Request bytes, actual model-visible context, billed tokens, host scans, storage, elapsed time, and monetary exposure are different measurements. Required semantics must fit faithfully or the affected admission blocks; a smaller preview cannot stand in for complete operative guidance.

#### Direct tool-discovery evidence

[ToolRet, Findings of ACL 2025](https://aclanthology.org/2025.findings-acl.1258.pdf) distinguishes fractional target recall from completeness, which requires every annotated target. This motivates the existing complete-valid-path measure: many relevant hits can still omit a prerequisite, while alternative authorized solution paths remain valid. Its instruction-assisted inputs were generated with target tools available to the writer, so their gains do not establish unaided cold-start discovery. Static ranked BM25 results do not evaluate an adaptive primary choosing literal/regex searches; several dense baselines also underperformed BM25. Neither lexical sufficiency nor universal dense superiority follows.

[T-Eval v2, 4 January 2024](https://arxiv.org/html/2312.14033v2) separates tool-use competencies, but its named retrieval subtask supplies a tool list and a prefix of a verified solution, then asks for the next tool. That is an assisted selection diagnostic, not cold-start catalog discovery. Both papers were independently inspected on 8 October 2026. They sharpen attribution under the existing [evaluation](../evaluation/README.md), without adding numerical targets, an embedding engine, or a new requirement family. This design's bounded, primary-owned discovery loop remains untested.

[ToolScout v1, 17 August 2026](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.16502v1) reports query–tool pairing/representation source-style failures in dense retrieval; query-only rewrites do not reproduce the full effect, and lexical controls vary by source. [SkillSight v3, 14 August 2026](https://arxiv.org/html/2607.18785v3) studies shared generic-description bias; its progressive-disclosure comparison starts from top-50 BM25 candidates and has model-dependent costs. These preprints motivate our proposed held-out-authoring and meaning/length-matched boilerplate/style controls, not a hidden selector or inherited lexical-discovery or economy result.

#### Generated reusable assets: empirical limits

The first four studies were independently inspected on 8 October 2026; the later additions were inspected on 9 October. Their component results motivate separating executable reuse from persistent learning:

- [Voyager v2, 19 October 2023](https://arxiv.org/html/2305.16291v2) used a generated skill library to solve four unseen Minecraft tasks in fewer prompts. The library condition passed 12/12 trials; its no-library ablation already passed 11/12. This supports reuse efficiency without attributing all competence to the library. Embedding retrieval and auxiliary models differ from this design's lexical discovery and single-primary authorship.
- [LATM v2, 11 March 2024](https://arxiv.org/html/2305.17126v2) improved GPT-3.5 task accuracy with GPT-4-made tools, while GPT-3.5 failed to author validation-passing tools on several studied tasks. Tool use, construction, and generalization are distinct achievements. Its stronger maker, cheaper user, separate dispatcher, and amortized creation cost do not establish savings for this architecture.
- [ToolMaker v1, 17 February 2025](https://arxiv.org/html/2502.11705v1) adapted scientific repositories into callable wrappers and environment snapshots, outperforming its comparator with higher creation expenditure. An unseen mask-token input exposed a partially correct tool. This motivates qualified input coverage and executable-environment evidence, not a claim of robustness to later dependency changes. Generated wrappers do not make their underlying software model-authored.
- [SkillsBench v4, 14 June 2026, Appendix D.6](https://arxiv.org/html/2602.12670v4) does not isolate a causal effect of generated-asset quality. Its self-generation diagnostic mixes undiscovered/unused packs, authoring that displaces task work, incorrect guidance, and instance-specific leakage. Missing trial slots, incomplete creator traces, different creation/reuse protocols, and one effort-setting mismatch limit interpretation. Neither success nor failure cleanly measures reusable knowledge; earlier-version percentages are not substituted for this analysis.
- [SkillCraft v2 preprint, 10 March 2026](https://arxiv.org/html/2603.00718v2) combines reusable tool compositions with keeping intermediate data outside model context. Its cost comparison conditions on tasks both arms solve; hierarchical results vary by model. This motivates an equivalent non-retained helper control and all-attempt accounting, not attributing every saving to durable learning.

These findings motivate the existing [evaluation](../evaluation/README.md) distinctions among attributable construction, held-out generalization, autonomous discovery, exact supply, current executability, and adequate outcomes. Count failed and never-reused candidates and actual lifecycle cost; later reuse cannot conceal displaced original work. None demonstrates persona continuity, beneficial every-call asset maintenance, or the complete primary-owned loop. They add empirical rationale, not another author, retrieval engine, invariant, or production testing ritual.

[SkillJack v2 preprint, 7 August 2026](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.03509v2) studies poisoned trajectories transformed into skills that can outlast source cleanup; its routing/detector and code-inspection endpoints are not live compromise. [Reflections on Trusting Trust, Revisited v1 preprint, 15 September 2026](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.17817v1) shows selected poisoned-checker cases yielding unsafe later code; its DGM case required a researcher modification outside the stated attack model. These motivate the joined source→asset→cleanup→reuse fixture and authentic-but-misleading feedback control. They do not establish prevalence, this design’s vulnerability, or complete defenses. Source removal, derivative withdrawal, unsafe proposals and released effects remain different observations under actual authority and retention rules.

### Compact associative fragments and selective retention

The intended fragment collection is a compact, changing body of persona-defining prompt parts, with useful associations expressed through ordinary prose and grep/search/regex cues. Compactness supports composing useful characteristic context; factual recall is one supporting concern, not its defining purpose. The primary LLM decides which associations to pursue and what meaning to retain. No automatic host traversal, mandatory graph database, separate semantic selector, fixed folder taxonomy, or extra reflection call follows from this aim. The component evidence supports testing this choice, not a claim that the combined architecture is validated.

| Primary source and inspected version | Relevant finding | Transfer boundary |
|---|---|---|
| [ReadAgent, v1, February 2024](https://arxiv.org/html/2402.09727v1) | Gist memory plus model-directed lookup improved studied long-document comprehension relative to its baselines. | The main design supplies the complete gist collection and retains original passages for lookup. Bounded lookups and smaller prompts do not establish bounded whole-store retention. |
| [LongMemEval, v1, October 2024](https://arxiv.org/html/2410.10813v1) | Compressing values into facts lost information and harmed overall performance, although multi-session reasoning benefited. Adding extracted facts to keys while keeping original values helped retrieval and answering. | Its tested dense retrieval beat BM25. These findings support separate discovery, reading, and compression assessments; they establish neither lexical-only equivalence nor an optimal fragment size for this design. |
| [The Vocabulary Problem, Furnas et al., 1987, original article](https://zhang.ist.psu.edu/teaching/504/readings/Furnas.pdf) | Human word choices varied greatly across five application domains; richer alternative access terms improved the studied access methods. | This is human–computer interaction evidence, not LLM cue-network validation. Selective alternative wording is a design inference; unlimited alias accumulation would conflict with compact retention. |
| [Memora, From Recall to Forgetting, v1, April 2026](https://arxiv.org/html/2604.20006v1) | Remembering, reasoning, and recommendation tasks over simulated histories expose reuse of obsolete or invalidated information. Its forgetting-aware evaluation penalizes that reuse. | The benchmark motivates checking harmful revival as well as recall. It does not establish verified data erasure, deletion rights, a universal forgetting score, a retention policy, or a scientifically optimal decay curve. |

An authored query cue suggests where to look; it neither asserts that a match exists nor endorses every result. An exact citation identifies the evidence or revision on which a claim rests. A mandatory qualification determines whether guidance may be supplied as usable at all. Queries support discovery, exact references support traceability, and required qualifications preserve applicability and restrictions. One cannot substitute for another: an accepted warning cannot become optional merely by wording it as a search suggestion. A changed query result does not change an already accepted exact selection.

The primary persona may revise an unhelpful cue, follow a useful connection, or stop within the declared cumulative allowance. Search results remain candidates, separate from exact inspection, accepted selection, and actual supplied context. A no-match reports the searched scope and matching rules; it cannot establish absence of relevant experience. Synonyms, stale wording, broad fanout, cycles, missed isolated fragments, and incomplete indexes remain evaluation cases. Optional traversal limits do not authorize omitting required qualifications. Source ancestry also matters: several linked descendants of one claim are not independent corroboration.

Compactness is judged by preserved useful distinctions, not only shorter text. Consolidation should retain characterful interpretation, conditions, uncertainty, rare exceptions, unresolved contradictions, and applicable restrictions. Declining a duplicate, merging equivalent guidance, retiring obsolete applicability, and erasing content under policy have different effects. Neither an archive nor a retirement label is erasure. Low frequency or elapsed time cannot silently dispose of an accepted warning, unresolved effect, or current obligation.

The whole-store claim covers the declared managed storage supporting persona context and its evidence: fragment bodies and cues, indexes, aliases, archives, versions, provenance and qualification metadata, receipts, caches, managed backups and exports, and temporary peak copies during revision. External storage deliberately used to retain persona context or its supporting history remains accountable; an unmeasurable external tier makes the claimed bound incomplete. Arbitrary recipient copies and a provider's independent retention after disclosure remain outside promised control and need an honest disclosure boundary. User-owned deliverables are not expendable fragment storage: capacity pressure does not authorize changing, deleting, or revoking them. Any deliberate dependency on those artifacts as a supporting storage tier must be disclosed and accounted for without changing their ownership or authority.

A qualified lesson can outlive raw evidence only where the actual derivative-use and retention policy permits it. Its surviving account must honestly distinguish fallible retained interpretation from still-inspectable proof; a digest cannot reconstruct lost evidence. Source expiry is not declassification. A derivative-deletion requirement applies to the lesson too; a surviving disclosure restriction still binds it; a use that requires exact evidence becomes ineligible when that evidence is unavailable. An unresolved commitment or effect cannot be settled by deleting its record. These are policy and engineering boundaries, not capabilities demonstrated by the cited memory studies.

Strictly bounded total storage cannot also promise unlimited exact recollection of indefinitely growing history. Capacity pressure therefore needs an honest retention, capacity, or admission disposition, rather than hidden archival growth or discarded obligations. The unrun comparison is whether selective retention and primary-authored cues preserve useful continuity at acceptable total cost. No reviewed source validates a human-like forgetting mechanism, universal aging rule, or 100% behavioral guarantee for that combination.

## Universal consideration and selective mutation are engineering choices

The proposed benefit of every-invocation consideration is that omissions become visible at the same boundary as the persona's work. It does not require a separate reflection call. The proposed risk is added overhead, filler, unsupported inference, and drift. Selective mutation preserves the possibility that nothing durable changed. Neither benefit nor risk has been measured for this architecture; the [acceptance plan](../evaluation/ACCEPTANCE.md) supplies the intended comparisons.

The requested unified work/maintenance response also considers whether to store, refine, or organize supporting tools, skills, histories, messages, data, scripts, and code. This every-call asset consideration is a user-requested design hypothesis, not a finding established by progressive-loading studies. Useful proposals identify their intended files or paths; next-context requests can identify exact resources or bounded search cues. Omitted next-context requests preserve only still-valid selections under the existing contracts. Asset organization retains source authorship and truthful history/effect receipts; generated prose cannot fabricate messages, execution, or experience.

Coverage means application-visible calls exercising persona agency, rather than only user-facing turns. Initialization, discovery, tool follow-ups, coordination, maintenance, and governed repairs can otherwise create gaps. A narrowly isolated non-persona utility differs from a helper making persona decisions. Provider-internal computation is not observable application coverage. These are scope definitions in the [contracts](../implementation/CONTRACTS.md), not psychological findings or a claim of control over hidden inference.

Reliable persistence and effect handling require their own evidence. [SQLite's atomic-commit explanation](https://www.sqlite.org/atomiccommit.html), inspected 8 October 2026, shows why crash consistency depends on a protocol and storage assumptions; it does not guarantee arbitrary multi-file edits. [Amazon's idempotent-API discussion](https://aws.amazon.com/builders-library/making-retries-safe-with-idempotent-APIs/), inspected the same day, explains request identity and retry ambiguity; it does not make a local commit atomic with an external action.

The [OpenAI Structured Outputs guide](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/guides/structured-outputs), inspected 8 October 2026, distinguishes structured output from semantic correctness and covers refusal handling. A constrained output format cannot guarantee truthful maintenance, complete provider output, successful storage, or an external effect. No provider-specific output feature is adopted by this note.

Retained material supplied as context is also an attack surface. [AgentPoison, v1, 2024](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.12784v1) studies poisoned memory or knowledge bases; [MINJA, v5, 2026](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.03704v5) studies query-only memory injection. Attack reach depends on access to the affected memory or an interaction path that can populate it; these are not prevalence estimates for isolated persona stores. Their attacks motivate source lineage, retention boundaries, and independent authorization checks. They do not validate complete defenses or show ordinary files are immune. Fluent own-voice wording cannot turn an untrusted instruction into authority. Correct provenance establishes traceability, not that a source supports every inference made from it.

## What remains to be demonstrated here

The chain of interest is characteristic prompt authorship, permitted selection, actual supplied context, LLM reasoning expressed in observable choices, action and received feedback, justified self-revision, and an independently assessed useful outcome. Private provider-internal reasoning need not be exposed to test the observable chain. File creation, schema compliance, a persistence receipt, or the persona claiming it has matured proves only a narrower part. An action following a read does not by itself establish causation.

Context quality is a composition question as well as a retrieval question. [Lost in the Middle, TACL 2024](https://aclanthology.org/2024.tacl-1.9/) found position-sensitive performance in studied question-answering and key-value tasks. [ComplexBench, NeurIPS 2024](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2024/file/f8c24b08b96a08ec7a7a975feea7777e-Paper-Datasets_and_Benchmarks_Track.pdf) evaluates dependent, conditional, and sequential constraints; its GPT-3.5-Turbo-1106 decomposition comparison performed worse than combined instructions. Both were independently inspected on 8 October 2026. They motivate varying order, distracting material, conditional scope, and successive decisions. Their model/task limits, including ComplexBench's primarily Chinese source instructions, prevent treating them as universal placement rules or persona-validation measures. Exact inclusion alone cannot prove effective use, and more calls do not inherently improve quality.

These are unrun AI Personas hypotheses; their precise cases belong in the [evaluation method](../evaluation/README.md) and [acceptance catalogue](../evaluation/ACCEPTANCE.md):

[LongMemEval, v2, 2025](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.10813v2) provides relevant factual-memory categories: extraction, cross-session reasoning, temporal reasoning, knowledge updates, and abstention. These inform part of evaluation; they do not measure characteristic authorship, privacy enforcement, crash recovery, or useful personality.

The target is useful, evidence-responsive continuity, not maximum difference, questionnaire scores, fragment counts, or perpetual introspection. Tests must preserve failed, harmful, assisted, and inconclusive outcomes and count total work. No universal passing number is supplied by these sources, and no 100% behavioral guarantee follows from the design.
