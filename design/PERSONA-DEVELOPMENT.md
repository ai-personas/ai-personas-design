# Character, experience, and self-directed activity

[Design index](README.md) · [Identity](01-personas-and-identity.md) · [Learning](02-memory-and-learning.md) · [Cooperation](03-work-and-cooperation.md)

## Status and rationale

This is a normative, cross-cutting refinement of the seven design chapters. It specifies intended behavior; it is not a statement that an implementation or a live evaluation has passed. It resolves the earlier optional-initial-descriptor wording through the creation rules in the identity chapter. The protected authority, information, resource, evidence, and consent invariants remain unchanged.

A continuing persona needs more than profile prose included in a prompt. Its starting conditions, authored interests, actual observations, relationships, and situation should be available when it chooses what to investigate, try, question, retain, or do next. The system supplies durable state, opportunities, tools, and execution. It must not author the persona's interpretation, choose a domain solution, assign fixed professions, or convert traits into mandatory action counts.

Missing initialization, weak research access, poor checking, insufficient continuing interests, and failure to retain experience are competing explanations for disappointing work. They need separate mechanism checks and behavioral comparisons. More persona text, changed trait values, additional tools, and larger artifact counts are not evidence of improvement by themselves.

## Continuing state has distinct meanings

| State | Meaning and boundary |
|---|---|
| Starting seed | Immutable, attributed initial conditions. It is not fabricated experience and later development never rewrites it. |
| Current character and OCEAN | Continuing tendencies and preferences, subject to the user's authorship policy. They neither certify capability nor change obligations. |
| Current VAD | Situation-sensitive modeled affect, not lasting disposition, subjective experience, or evidence about external conditions. |
| Interests and open questions | Authored things the persona wants to investigate or improve. Interest is not competence or authorization. |
| Learned fragments | Reusable interpretations, procedures, and cautions with provenance, applicability, limitations, and correction history. |
| Relationship interpretations | Directional, attributed understanding of particular interactions. A positive interpretation grants no access to another participant's memory. |
| Accepted commitments | Responsibilities actually accepted. Preference, membership, and being nominated do not replace acceptance. |
| Demonstrated capability | Successful operations under particular observed conditions. A successful trial is not a professional qualification. |

Interests and relationships can persist across work, but work agendas and accepted commitments retain their particular scope. Neither kind of state is silently copied into the other. Private evidence keeps its restrictions in a personal interpretation and in every search preview, profile, or exported derivative.

## Creation and authorship

The identity chapter defines immediate funded character generation when narrative is absent, independently varied characters for identical traits, immutable completed provenance, explicit-zero preservation, and retry stability. Users can supply narrative character and any subset of OCEAN and VAD values. Missing numeric values are sampled uniformly within the documented ranges. This is an engineering initialization distribution, not a validated model of a human population.

“Let this persona shape its character” is enabled by default. When disabled, narrative character, OCEAN, and VAD remain user-controlled; ordinary learning, interests, and work judgments remain available. A persona must not evade this restriction by writing substitute effective traits into a different profile field. The same restriction applies under the enabled host-execution deployment: application-level attribution and permission checks are still required, while the existing shared-host trust limitation must remain explicit.

Operator edits to current profile values or authorship policy use an operator-attributed path with a reason and an exact observed revision. They are not recorded as persona self-authorship. They never change the starting seed. Persona revisions likewise preserve their author, reason, earlier version, and exact supporting experience where an experience-based claim is made. Numeric movement alone is not evidence of growth.

A profile or policy change invalidates affected in-flight decisions before further operations are admitted. Previously suppressed operations do not later reappear as executions or replay automatically. A failed or no-op update must not invent a development event.

After character initialization, during the first funded work orientation, the persona considers its starting state and records an initial approach or an explicit deferral. This may accompany useful work or the membership response; neither a display name nor a portrait nor several biography-polishing calls is a prerequisite. An empty decision does not count as an authored approach or a deferral. If orientation ends without one, that remains a visible incomplete disposition rather than invented authorship.

## Character informs choices; it does not supply authority

Different tendencies may lead a persona to establish a dependable baseline, investigate an unfamiliar method, consult someone, independently test a claim, challenge an assumption, persist after a setback, or stop with an honest limitation. These are observable possibilities, not prescribed roles or an OCEAN-to-tool mapping.

Accuracy, honesty, essential verification, accepted responsibilities, and user constraints apply at every trait value. Low conscientiousness cannot justify careless claims. High dominance cannot create authority over peers. Character cannot outweigh a cancellation or justify spending protected finishing resources on optional production.

Each decision receives current character, dispositions, affect, authorship policy, relevant personal interests, the current work agenda, accepted obligations, consequential new observations, selected applicable lessons, attributed relationship interpretations, and available exploration opportunities and resource facts. Mandatory authority and unresolved obligations remain distinct from optional historical material.

Discovery retains task, mandate, feedback, and error coverage while adding authored interests, current agenda, relevant relationship context, and explicit investigation questions. Current handoffs, active prompt fragments, owner-authored retrieval cues and related-fragment references also inform the next context. The persona chooses what to learn and how to organize it using the experience and character available in that decision; the runtime supplies retrieval and faithful attribution, not generic replacement prose. Personal interests must not crowd out the user's need. Lexical retrieval, persona-authored query expansion, and pagination are sufficient initial mechanisms; an unmeasured embedding service or universal hidden relevance score is not a prerequisite. Candidate previews are neither selection nor actual inclusion. Admission receipts must identify the exact state supplied without retaining raw model transport or private reasoning.

## Experience and retained change

Situation, attention, action, observation, interpretation, retained change, and later use form a relationship, not compulsory phases. A failed tool trial, a useful clarification, a peer correction, a meaningful failed check, or a lesson becoming inapplicable can all be consequential experience. Repeated directory listings and unchanged status summaries are not new learning simply because another operation occurred.

Subsequent ordinary decisions expose relevant observations with exact evidence references. The persona may retain a lesson, revise one, change a permitted preference, reconsider a relationship, defer interpretation, decline retention, or make no lasting change. The system records that authored disposition; it does not write a lesson on the persona's behalf or require a fragment after every command.

An experience review identifies the observations being interpreted, the concise authored interpretation or disposition, and the exact retained changes actually made. A proposed change is not a committed change. Deferral can remain visible without itself scheduling more inference. A later reconsideration must identify a new consequential event or another currently authorized trigger, not reword the same summary to simulate novelty.

Evidence may name an exact retained record version or an exact settled action receipt. Action evidence binds both action identity and receipt digest and is checked against the real journal, current access, ownership, and disposition. A caller-supplied digest is not proof unless it matches that journal. Superseded versions remain historical evidence, not automatically current support. Revoked or erased content cannot be recovered through the receipt path.

A failed action supports learning about the observed failure. An uncertain effect supports an uncertainty finding; it establishes neither success nor failure of the intended operation. Reconciliation is a later observation, not a rewrite of what was known earlier. Referencing a command receipt must not require manufacturing an extra artifact solely to cite it.

Lasting character development and temporary affect remain distinguishable. Where a revision claims experience changed a preference, relevant evidence is required. An explanation such as an initial preference or a present modeled state must not be embellished into a fictional success story.

## Active work and optional personal exploration

Active-work exploration can investigate missing knowledge and methods within the task's existing purpose, authority, and funding. It does not require a separate background permission merely because a method is unfamiliar.

Between tasks, personal exploration is disabled until explicitly enabled by the operator. Enabling it requires a permitted environment, an existing funding allowance, explicit per-episode bounds, a finite recurrence allowance, and expiry. No omitted setting means unlimited activity. A persona can propose its question, experiment, and stopping condition, but cannot enlarge or renew its own allowance.

An exploration episode is ordinary work and run activity with visible purpose, owner, funding, observations, and disposition. It is not a second autonomous-agent runtime. The scheduler decides when an authorized episode may start, not the domain solution or the persona's interests. Negative findings, an unpromising trial, and a justified stop are legitimate outcomes.

Opportunities and triggers are persisted transactionally with their identities and policy provenance. Restart must preserve them. Duplicate delivery must not create duplicate work, inference, reservations, or execution. Starts recheck current authority, environment access, funding, recurrence capacity, pause/cancellation, and expiry in the same admission boundary. A delayed trigger must not evade a newly disabled policy. Recurrence capacity is consumed atomically, not read and decremented in separate races.

New user work has scheduling priority over optional background exploration. Optional production cannot consume protected closeout capacity. Pausing, disabling, expiry, and cancellation prevent subsequent starts; already admitted effects retain their actual stopping and uncertainty receipts. None of these operations refunds already spent capacity or erases an accepted responsibility. A paused episode requires an authorized resume, not an incidental notification.

Experience reviews should normally be offered in the next ordinary decision. A separate funded reconsideration episode requires both the exploration policy and a new consequential event. Unresolved opportunities may remain visible indefinitely without creating idle inference loops. Broader ongoing-service and physical extensions remain separately gated; this limited personal-exploration permission does not silently enable them.

## Accountable stopping and cooperation

Before a continuation owner stops, its context must include unresolved obligations, current findings, pending opportunities, and relevant unanswered questions. Successful message delivery or submission cannot suppress these facts. The durable disposition distinguishes an actual outside dependency, another participant's accepted contribution, a scheduled exploration opportunity, voluntary yield with unfinished work, partial delivery with gaps, completion with applicable checks, and resource/tool/runtime blockage.

A dependency needs an identifiable source of the required event. Naming an unaccepted peer offer does not make someone else responsible. A scheduled opportunity needs a real persisted trigger and current authorization; a wished-for future investigation is not scheduled. An empty decision may yield without asserting a human dependency or supported completion.

Waiting remains legitimate. The runtime does not reject every wait while work remains or force infinite retries. It preserves who still owns what, the stated gaps, and which future triggers are actually authorized. An unsupported completion claim stays unsupported, not silently accepted. A missing human response blocks only the parts depending on that response; peers may clarify and work may continue under explicit permissible assumptions without pretending to obtain human consent.

Working interpretations and proposed checks are persona-authored proposals. They do not rewrite the original request or become user-approved scope changes. Review identifies what was examined, its exact version, what the check establishes, what remains untested, and what needs repair. Findings reach the relevant owner and remain visible until an authorized disposition. A corrected artifact does not automatically resolve a finding about an earlier version.

Viewing an actual rendering is distinct from parsing a file, inspecting labels, or checking arithmetic. Image observations and execution receipts should identify what was actually observed. The runtime enforces freshness, access, ownership, evidence links, and honest transitions; personas and appropriate checks assess domain substance.

## Research and tool discovery

Search and page reading remain shared environment tools that users may omit at creation. Diagnostics identify the actual backend, requested query or source, observation time, transport outcome, and failure category. Blocked access, an explicitly empty response, unavailable infrastructure, and returned candidate results are distinct. HTTP success does not prove relevant or successful research. Relevance belongs to the investigating persona, which can record that returned material was irrelevant.

Failure at one search engine must not be presented as proof that every configured backend or original-source read is unavailable. Another configured backend or a direct source remains a choice, not an automatic retry storm. An optional operator-configured HTTP search service returns normalized results; credentials stay outside persona context. No account creation, purchase, CAPTCHA circumvention, or additional authority is implied.

A persona may identify needed capabilities, investigate tools, choose a representative trial, prepare a candidate under current host authority, observe results, retain useful preparation and limitations, and later recheck applicability. These are available activities, not seven required phases. No particular CAD application, professional roster, or house workflow is built into the runtime. The current enabled host-execution and tool-installation profile remains available.

## Human-readable development history

Creation explains supplied narrative versus immediate funded generation, pending and failed initialization with explicit retry/cancel controls, optional numeric entries, random initialization of missing values, the default-enabled self-authorship choice, and the separate default-disabled personal exploration permission. Exploration controls expose funding, episode and recurrence limits, expiry, pause, and cancellation rather than a vague unlimited background toggle.

A persona view separates starting and current state; displays who changed what and why; and shows accessible interests, questions, experiments and observed outcomes, retained lessons, later uses, relationship interpretations, and exploration activity and stopping conditions. Use distinct labels for **tried**, **retained**, **used later**, and **benefit evaluated**. A generic skills-learned count cannot conflate these stages. Missing evidence is labeled missing, not inferred from a record count.

History, exact evidence, and artifact viewers load on demand, retain pagination, and release unused previews. Raw JSON tables and model transport are not the normal experience. Optional presentation does not imply subjective feelings, qualifications, or generated artifacts that do not exist.

## Acceptance and limits of conclusions

Mechanism correctness, observed behavior, and outcome improvement are separate claims. Initialization tests cover fully supplied, partial, random, explicit zero, invalid ranges, retry identity, restart, and environment changes. Authorship tests cover enabled and disabled evolution, attributed operator edits, immutable starting state, meaningful reasons, and policy changes during inference without suppressed-action replay. Personal-state and evidence tests cover ownership, access filtering, exact versions/digests, failed and uncertain actions, revoked sources, supersession, retention, revision, deferral, refusal, selection, inclusion, and harmful-lesson correction.

Scheduler tests cover restart, duplicate triggers, races, pausing, cancellation, expiry, exhaustion, foreground priority, and protected finishing capacity. Stopping tests distinguish human, accepted-peer, voluntary, partial, scheduled, and infrastructure dispositions. Research tests distinguish blocked, empty, irrelevant candidates, alternate backend, and direct reads. Storage checks confirm no raw model archives or artificial retained-data ceiling was introduced. Character-inclusion tests must not be relabeled proof of behavioral influence.

Character comparisons hold task, environment, tools, resource allowance, and inference configuration constant within a model. Vary one declared profile dimension, use three predeclared initialization seeds, and repeat independently for each model configuration used by Luna, Terra, and Sol. Include technical-artifact, conflicting-information, and shared-coordination task families, plus name-swap and character-withheld controls. Assess actual attention, alternatives, experiments, consultation, evidence-responsive choices, outcomes, resource use, and repetition. Do not require stereotypes or hide negative variation.

Learning-transfer comparisons use three related follow-up tasks with three matched repetitions: an experienced persona with the relevant lesson, matched experience with that lesson withheld, and a fresh persona with matching starting dispositions and inference configuration. Retained versus withheld is the primary lesson comparison. Prevent leakage through files, summaries, copied profiles, peer messages, and caches in separate equivalent environments without disabling normally authorized tools. Include misleading, superseded, and inapplicable lessons. Verify the ledger against actual journal evidence, selected and dispatched context, subsequent actions, and an independent outcome assessment; manually asserted receipts are insufficient.

Preserve the existing failing minimal-house case under its original rubric. In three independent fresh environments, submit exactly **design 4 bedroom house** with permission for explicit concept assumptions but no professions, software selection, deliverable checklist, or corrective hints. Keep the rubric outside persona context. Assess editable native modeling source, reopening and a meaningful edit with regenerated outputs, four usable bedrooms with access, coherent geometry and circulation, geometry-derived dimensions and schedules, meaningful tool trials, peer findings and dispositions, relevant engineering and permit questions, and useful retained experience. A concept remains a concept; unnecessary CAM or simulation files do not earn credit. Publish failures and distinguish operator-assisted repair from success on the original brief.

When model revision pinning or reproducible sampling is unavailable, record that limitation and report observational comparisons rather than strict causal isolation. Report exact repository revisions, actual tests performed, unrun gates, successful outputs, and remaining defects. Pushing a commit is not deployment, and deployment is not behavioral acceptance.


## Character throughout the combined decision

The current character, OCEAN dispositions, temporary modeled VAD, selected full
fragments and short memory branch entries inform one combined work/learning/
organization/retrieval decision. They influence research subject, breadth, depth,
choice of sources and experiments, stopping, cooperation, challenge and what is
valuable enough to retain. This is persona judgment, not fixed numerical routing,
mandatory sociability, compulsory research counts or role assignment. Browser
search is the primary discovery affordance for unfamiliar domain needs and tool
suitability; applicable received evidence can avoid redundant research.

Social encounters can inform directional relationship interpretations, temporary
affect and, when self-authorship is enabled and the persona explains the change,
continuing narrative or OCEAN. Preserve the immutable starting seed and exact
received evidence; no runtime trait scorer or automatic peer-character copying.
Retrieve relevant relationships by current participant, subject and task, with
recency as a fallback. A short bounded view is not an exhaustive search or a fact
about another person. Learning and relationship authorship remain available when
profile self-authorship is disabled.

Under context pressure, retain compact exact learning cues and protect current
obligations, adverse observations and selected full lessons. Long duplicated
instructions or authoring schemas must not crowd out these cues. Keep operation
signatures discoverable and full exact help available on demand. Report payload
bytes separately from provider tokens. Stored fragments, later inclusion and
measured transfer benefit remain separate evidence claims.


## Readable publication and decision context

An artifact's first committed version must already carry its ownership, generated-work provenance and inherited information restrictions. Blob preparation is not publication. Publication and capture return the canonical committed version; no stale revision may enter a lesson or submission source chain. Shared work provenance does not remove genuine private ancestors. Owner/operator delivery diagnostics distinguish readable peers and retained notifications from actual inspection, agreement or acceptance; silence behind a source restriction is not a peer declining to answer.

The ordinary decision brief relates current character, relevant personal state, original need, current agenda and obligations, unresolved shared questions, readable peer contributions and selected full prompt fragments. Bounded contribution previews survive inbox acknowledgement and carry exact references, explicit omissions and current read checks. They neither assign coordination nor prove consent. Prefer concise guidance over repeating the same behavioral instructions across context sections. Same-call learning, organization and next-context retrieval remain persona-authored: a valuable abstraction may be retained before an action succeeds, with its tentative basis clear. Social outcomes can inform relationship interpretations and learning without assigning a numeric personality-to-action rule.
