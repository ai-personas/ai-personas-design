# Development evidence, historical privacy, and shared funding

[Design index](README.md) · [Persona development](PERSONA-DEVELOPMENT.md) · [Exploration accounting](EXPLORATION-ACCOUNTING.md)

This normative clarification closes interpretation gaps in the development rules. It describes required behavior, not completed implementation, behavioral acceptance, or deployment.

## The original question constrains historical representations

When an exploration question supplies work instructions, its exact source restrictions apply to every representation of those instructions: initial and later work versions, invitations, participation instructions, archived receipts, selected context, and derivatives. A source field attached later in the construction transaction must not leave an earlier snapshot independently discloseable.

Historical bytes remain historical. Access may use durable construction provenance to bind an earlier representation to its actual source; it must not pretend that missing fields were present in the original bytes. Missing or inconsistent construction evidence must not silently fall back to an unrestricted read. The binding must be established by runtime records, not a caller's unsupported statement about where the text came from.

Current withdrawal, erasure, audience and export restrictions remain effective. Conversely, a later interpretation that cites a newly private source must not retroactively replace the exact question that originally supplied the work. Current authority and historical content are separate. A readable historic record does not confer permission to restart its episode.

An authorized read is not permission to discard provenance. Reading an earlier work or invitation through an action receipt, selecting it, or citing it in a lesson must carry the same original source restrictions into subsequent context and derivatives. Work membership, an invitation address, or ownership of a derivative cannot bypass those restrictions. The trusted operator and the documented shared-host execution boundary remain unchanged.

## A review must link the change it claims

A retained-change claim identifies an actual successful authoring operation and the exact committed payload. Each linked change explicitly cites at least one of the reviewed observations at its exact record version or action-receipt digest. Ambient context, a nearby timestamp, or an unrelated owned lesson is not an authored evidence link.

A revision disposition identifies a real revision, not merely a newly retained item relabeled as a correction. A reason-only profile update must not become character growth. These structural checks do not decide the domain truth or semantic relevance of the persona's interpretation. Failed and uncertain observations can support appropriately limited cautions without being rewritten as successful trials. Retention, later inclusion, later action and evaluated benefit remain separate facts.

## Deferral is not permanent prohibition or a self-wake

A persona may conclude an earlier deferred interpretation in an ordinary, already authorized decision. It may retain a properly linked change, revise one, or honestly decide that no lasting change is warranted. The earlier deferral stays inspectable; the concluding record identifies it and preserves the same observation-set identity.

Such closure is not a new experience, a new exploration opportunity or an inference trigger. Repeating a deferral or a terminal interpretation with different wording does not create novelty. A separate reconsideration episode still needs the existing exploration permission and the required new consequential event. Current source access and funding checks apply independently of the desire to reconsider.

## Foreground priority applies to the shared allowance

Optional exploration must yield to queued or running foreground user work competing for the same controlling resource root, including work owned by another persona. The check applies both before a scheduled episode starts and before an episode call is admitted. The per-persona priority rule remains an additional constraint; it is not a substitute for protecting shared funding.

An unrelated root does not create a global veto. Waiting, paused, cancelled, completed, removed or historical participation does not become active foreground work merely by remaining in storage. Later peers within an optional episode remain optional participation. This is a mechanical admission rule, not a domain priority score, a spending increase, or authority to interrupt effects already in progress.

## Separate verification claims

Acceptance checks cover source-less initial snapshots, exact construction provenance, current withdrawal and export, later source amendments, receipt and derivative propagation, unrelated retained changes, real versus claimed revisions, deferred-to-terminal closure, duplicate rejection, and foreground work across shared and separate roots. Interface checks cover exact references, qualified meanings and on-demand evidence readers.

Source-query tests and synthetic interface checks cannot substitute for compiled runtime tests, concurrency checks, live character and learning comparisons, or the minimal-brief outcome evaluation. Published reports must distinguish those gates and preserve failures rather than inheriting success from a different revision.
