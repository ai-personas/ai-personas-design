# Effort, persistence, and accountable stopping

[Design index](README.md) · [Character and development](PERSONA-DEVELOPMENT.md) · [Continuity and recovery](CONTINUITY-RECOVERY.md)

## Status and problem

This is a normative clarification of the existing character, accountable-stopping, resource, and evidence rules, not a new autonomous runtime or a claim of measured behavioral improvement. It resolves one ambiguity: an authored decision containing no actions may deliberately yield, but an absent scheduling choice is not an authored yield.

A persona may be recognizably different in voice while making the same work choices as another persona. Conversely, two personas may spend different amounts while producing equally useful results. Neither profile inclusion nor elapsed time alone establishes useful individuality. The question is whether current character and relevant experience inform observable, evidence-responsive choices and improve outcomes under the user's constraints.

## Character shapes effort allocation, not minimum competence

Within the same authority and allowance, a persona may choose different depth, alternatives, experiments, checking methods, collaboration, or optional refinement. A dependable baseline and a carefully investigated alternative can both be appropriate. The persona considers the likely value of another useful step against remaining uncertainty, costs, constraints, and the requested result. This is situated judgment, not a universal numerical priority formula.

Accuracy, honesty, essential checking, accepted commitments, and cancellation apply at every trait value. Lower conscientiousness does not authorize unchecked claims. Greater curiosity does not expand the task or its budget. There is no rule assigning a minimum number of calls, tools, minutes, revisions, or peer exchanges to a trait. More repetition is not evidence of stronger character or better work.

Model reasoning settings, context limits, and execution allowances remain separately configured capabilities and constraints. They are not silently derived from personality scores. Evaluations must separate their effects from the persona's choices.

## Make the work choice in the ordinary decision

Current character, the original need, accepted obligations, relevant findings, actual observations, selected applicable learning, and available resource facts inform the same ordinary work decision. The persona should identify the next useful outcome, or briefly explain why yielding is appropriate and what remains. This is a concise work disposition, not a demand for private reasoning or a separate reflection call.

Continuing requests another authorized, funded opportunity to decide or observe. It can be appropriate when no reusable lesson has been written and no immediate action is ready. A no-change learning disposition does not mean there is no work left. Memory authorship and work scheduling have distinct meanings.

Yielding may follow adequate delivery, a real dependency or boundary, or a deliberate pause with unfinished work. The last case must remain visibly unfinished; it must not become supported completion, abandonment of responsibility, or an invented human dependency. A preliminary message and a technically valid file do not establish adequate delivery. More conceivable refinement does not by itself make indefinite continuation obligatory.

The continuation or yield choice must be explicit. Missing, malformed, or unsupported output remains a decision-contract failure, not evidence that the persona wanted to stop. It also grants no permission to spend on automatic continuation. The system must not infer the choice from a summary, a memory disposition, a prior decision, another persona, or a trait value. Any recovery stays within the existing bounded recovery and admission rules.

This clarifies, rather than removes, the development chapter's permission for an empty decision to yield: the action collection may be empty while its scheduling choice is authored. It does not require extra action or learning to justify every deliberate wait.

## Preserve the meaning of stopping

Ending a batch to observe an asynchronous result, mutation, failure, or uncertainty is not necessarily ending the persona's participation. Suppressed actions do not execute later from the old decision. A fresh decision uses the actual result and current authority.

An exact-outcome wait identifies the actual action or transfer being observed. When registration also delivers an already-available result and refreshes the participation record, the new wait's reason and classification must survive that refresh. It must not inherit a previous voluntary, blocked, partial, or completion interpretation. The recorded outcome remains success, failure, cancellation, or uncertainty as actually observed; registration cannot change it.

The ordinary decision's authored explanation must remain the visible reason when
it yields without a separate wait operation. Present current recorded delivery
gaps, accepted ownership and unresolved findings beside that explanation without
turning them into an automatic quality verdict. A resume permits a fresh decision;
it is not new domain evidence or permission to discard an unmet obligation.

Repeated delivery of the same retained outcome is not fresh evidence or a new authorized wake-up. A new input, an explicit resume, or a real authorized trigger may permit later work. A pending learning cue, a sentence promising future investigation, or an unaccepted peer offer does not schedule it. Pausing and cancellation continue to prevent unauthorized resumption. Restarts preserve the actual wait and its ownership implications.

## Acceptance and requirement mapping

These scenarios refine existing requirements; they do not add identifiers or replace earlier evaluation criteria.

| Existing requirements | Additional checks |
|---|---|
| PER-02, PER-03; I03, I15 | Verify supplied current character separately from observed choices and independently assessed benefit. Do not substitute trait-to-iteration quotas for behavioral evidence. |
| PER-04, NED-02, NED-04; I07, I09, I18 | Distinguish an explicit empty-action yield from a missing scheduling choice. Preserve unfinished outcomes, ownership, and the actual reason for stopping. |
| ACT-03, SYS-01, SYS-02; I12, I17 | Test successful, failed, cancelled, and uncertain outcomes, already-retained notifications, replay, restart, and fresh-decision boundaries. The current wait interpretation must survive record refresh. |
| GOV-02, GOV-04; I16, I20 | Explicit continuation remains funded and cancellable. Missing choices grant no spending authority. Retained notifications and pending learning do not create idle loops. |
| EVD-05, UX-01; I14, I15 | Present contract failures, voluntary pauses, partial delivery, dependencies, supported completion, and infrastructure blocks distinctly. Report unrun tests and missing observations as such. |

Use the existing [character-comparison and learning-transfer controls](PERSONA-DEVELOPMENT.md#acceptance-and-limits-of-conclusions), including matched tasks, environments, tools, model configuration, allowances, independent repetitions, name swaps, and character-withheld conditions. Preserve the original failing-task rubric. Do not claim success from an operator-assisted repair under different instructions.

Alongside outcome quality, report admitted decisions, actual operations and checks, model-reported token use, exhausted allowances, rejected decisions, and stopping categories. Separate active work from provider latency, queue time, dependency waiting, and operator pauses. Measure distinct hypotheses: character was supplied; choices differed; the differences improved useful work. A supplied profile receipt establishes only the first.

Compare the same original obligation and evidence opportunity across profiles, not merely completion labels or time spent. Report beneficial, neutral, and harmful variation, as well as cases where different profiles converge on the same sound choice. Where model revisions or sampling cannot be controlled, describe observational comparisons rather than claiming strict causal isolation. Publication, implementation, passing mechanism tests, and demonstrated behavioral benefit remain separate claims.
