# Recovery without fabricated authority or completion

[Authority and resources](05-authority-and-resources.md) · [Delivery review](DELIVERABLE-PRODUCTION.md) · [Acceptance catalogue](../evaluation/ACCEPTANCE.md)

**Clarification: 1 October 2026.** These refinements apply to GOV-02–GOV-04,
COL-01–COL-05, ACT-01–ACT-04, EVD-01–EVD-05 and UX-02. They introduce no new
requirement family, profession map or compulsory task workflow. The scenarios
below are evaluation obligations, not claims of an executed behavioral pass.

## Distinguish three barriers

Unknown spending, unresolved execution and missing accepted responsibility are
separate states. Retaining a complete conservative charge can make a provably
closed, unapplied primary decision safe to leave behind; it does not refund
spending, free uncertain remote capacity, or turn membership into ownership.
The runtime must retain remaining blockers independently rather than collapse
them into one repeating explanation. An amended mandate may require fresh
consent even when the old inference was drained successfully. When no ordinary
capacity remains to consider new terms, expose the funding and consent gap;
do not use protected capacity by inventing acceptance.

## Usable sharing choices

A refused delivery should identify the intended persona recipient and the
current policies of blocking sources the actor owns, without disclosing private
sources owned by someone else. Reader identifiers denote personas, not a user
label, task, environment, mandate or allowance. The authenticated operator's
existing access is not another persona reader entry. Invalid identifiers fail
atomically and explain the correct identity type.

A policy replacement must make its replacement semantics clear, including any
implicit audiences it clears. The actor chooses whether to preserve intended
access, add a recipient, ask another owner, or work independently from permitted
sources. Diagnostics never automatically share a source, grant export rights,
remove provenance, or retry an external effect.

## Delivery is more than a local file or document

The existing publication and submission capabilities must remain discoverable
and usable even after an older execution receipt leaves the active context.
Producing bytes in a workspace, publishing a version, submitting that version,
adopting an assembly and accepting the result are distinct observations.
A document that names a local archive does not deliver that archive. Reporting
must keep failed verification, fidelity limits and missing artifacts visible.

Absent site details or acceptance do not inherently prevent reversible,
assumption-labelled development that is already authorized. Personas still
choose scope, means and what to do next; diagnostics and capability availability
do not prove that they made adequate choices or produced a usable domain result.

## Acceptance refinements

Evaluate an amendment while a bounded decision is in flight. The old response
may supply metering but must never supply actions; the next authorized response
uses the new mandate. Contrast this with explicit pause and verify that pause
still requests stopping and survives restart.

With several participants sharing an allowance, retain an interrupted primary
call's full charge. Prove closure and absence of attempted actions before
allowing eligible finishing; reject missing evidence, pending plans, uncertain
external effects and insufficient remote, token or cost capacity. Verify that
zero accepted responsibilities remains an independent, visible blocker before
and after ordinary capacity exhaustion. No test may manufacture consent as a
side effect of diagnostic inspection or admission.

Exercise invalid reader identities and policy replacement without disclosing
anything. Then exercise explicit permitted publication and submission of an
actual file after execution-history compaction. Distinguish mechanism tests
from live evidence that the artifact can be reopened, inspected, reproduced and
accepted for its intended use. Passing the former does not establish the latter.
