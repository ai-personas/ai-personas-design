# Context, memory and shared skills

[Design guide](README.md) · [Persona core](PERSONA-CORE.md) · [Contracts](../implementation/CONTRACTS.md)

**In plain language:** keep useful material outside the character model, make it easy to find, and let the persona choose what the next decision actually needs. Remembering a method and maturing in judgment can both help, but they are different mechanisms.

![The full resource collection remains outside the reasoning request. The persona uses targeted discovery and exact reads to select relevant resources. Current numerical character is supplied separately; selected and actually supplied content remain distinguishable.](../assets/context.svg)

*Read the arrows as possible, recorded transitions—not promises that a result will improve.*

The storage collection is larger than any one request. Discovery returns useful candidates; exact selection supplies their necessary content. A reference in a note is a pointer, not proof that its bytes were supplied. Character parameters are evaluated as a model rather than retrieved as personality prose.

## 1. Classify by purpose, not by author

Facts, knowledge, code, skills, guides, tool definitions, images, plans, working notes, histories and reflections are supporting resources. They remain resources when authored in first person or written by a persona. A description of a proposed behavioral lesson is evidence and explanation; the operative character change is the separately adopted numerical revision.

An experience index records what happened and where exact evidence is retained. A development agenda records what the persona wants to understand or change. Neither is a hidden second personality prompt. They may influence reasoning because they contain relevant evidence, which must be distinguished from the contribution of changed parameters.

Exact records preserve provenance, qualifications, versions and known uncertainty. Imported knowledge is not invented firsthand experience. User preferences and local relationship interpretations remain appropriately scoped and revisable.

## 2. Persona-owned selective discovery

The normal navigation tools are ordinary listing, useful owner-authored indexes, literal search, regular expressions and exact reads. The primary persona chooses the query, scope, reformulation and next resources. There is no hidden semantic selector, automatic graph traversal or full-catalogue model used to choose what the persona sees. Semantic or vector retrieval can be studied as a comparator, not silently added to the production design.

Discovery applies to all resource types, including tools, skills, exact history, model packages and developmental evidence. Already known current references can be used directly without ritual rediscovery. Finding a guide does not establish installation, readiness, execution or correctness.

Search output identifies its actual scope, matching surface, pagination, omissions and errors. An empty partial search is not proof that no useful resource exists. A stale index or changed vocabulary may require another query chosen by the persona; the host must not invent a semantic substitute.

### Lifetime history without lifetime prompt replay

The persona can explicitly search its attributable history across selected works or its lifetime, in addition to current-work history. Results provide metadata and exact references, not all raw content. A record from another context retains its source and qualifications. Receipt of another persona’s account is not the same as having lived its event.

Current obligations, controlling instructions, relevant warnings and actual unresolved blockers must survive selection. They are not optional background that a shorter prompt may silently omit. The runtime supplies required current state without ranking tasks by a hidden preference formula.

## 3. Faithful assembly of the next request

The next request contains the current numerical model’s usable outputs, the actual need, necessary current state, new observations and the supporting content explicitly selected by the persona. It does not automatically reconstruct the entire conversation.

Keep the host registry, provider transport and actual model-visible context distinct. The bulk catalogue stays outside both the choosing model’s input and the provider request. Provider schema expansion or externally retained context must be accounted for where observable; unknown exposure remains unknown.

A compact handoff can preserve factual findings, failed approaches, current artifact locations, chosen assumptions and the next concrete action. It is not a substitute for literal source text or exact data still needed for an edit or comparison. When combining separate observations, the persona retains the earlier necessary material until it has used it or decides it is no longer needed.

Selections can name exact complete resources or explicit text ranges with their necessary operative qualifications. A path alone does not bind a stable body. Renames, merges and edits preserve actual version lineage. Whole images remain exact captured bytes; a text excerpt does not mean an image crop, and selecting an image does not recapture a changing file.

A useful model update must not silently discard ordinary context or continuing duties. Initial activation is different: bootstrap seed prose and preparatory notes do not automatically become a second active character account.

## 4. Skills can be created and shared

A persona may discover that a procedure, script, checklist, analysis method, image-selection approach or tool combination is useful. It can publish the resource in a common discoverable location with its purpose, intended path, dependencies, evidence, limitations and useful cues.

Another persona chooses whether to discover, read, use or adapt it. The adaptation retains the source and produces its own version. The recipient’s character influences that choice; the original author does not become a hidden task router. Shared skills are not restricted to a fixed developer catalogue or a predefined domain.

A published file alone does not prove a skill was used or useful. Track actual selection, readiness, invocation, observations and later consequences. A better skill can improve task performance without changing numerical character; record that benefit separately.

## 5. Maintain organization without quotas

Creation, revision, reorganization, consolidation and retirement preserve useful discovery and exact evidence where retained. Current working material should stay compact and coherent. This is a goal for persona judgment, not an application storage, context, tool or lifetime cap.

Count the full managed footprint: current bodies, indexes, aliases, revisions, archives, provenance, warnings, caches, backups, retained provider context where observable, and temporary copies during rewrite. Unknown outside storage cannot honestly be reported as zero. User deliverables must not be deleted to make an internal footprint look smaller.

Deletion, retirement, source loss and resolution have different meanings. A hash cannot reconstruct unavailable bytes. An interpretation can survive as an attributed interpretation while its original evidence is unavailable; it must not be described as reverified fact. User-requested erasure reports what was actually removed and what external copies cannot be controlled.

## 6. Failure and recovery

A provider may reject actual context length or image capacity. Retain the rejected request and real receipt. A new decision can receive labeled metadata and exact pointers, inspect suitable excerpts, and select a workable context. The metadata does not count as reading the omitted content. Completed commands are not rerun merely to recover their output.

Do not silently truncate valid selections, resize images, remove operative warnings or substitute another model. Missing resources and failed reads remain explicit. A context-recovery decision does not acquire evidence for model activation merely by seeing candidate names; the actual relevant candidate and assessment must be observed.

## 7. Evidence of efficiency

Measure the complete activity rather than only the final prompt: discovery, scans, reads, schema exposure, history, maintenance, assessments, failures, retries, compaction, storage and actual provider usage. Bytes, tokens, elapsed time and billed charges are different measures. Compare usefulness and harmful omissions alongside cost. Selectivity is a design mechanism; savings remain an empirical claim.
